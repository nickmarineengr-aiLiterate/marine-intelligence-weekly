MIW Study Material Disclaimer — October 2026 (v1.0, Final)

1. What these notes are. These notes are independent study material prepared by Marine Intelligence Weekly (MIW) to help candidates prepare for the MEO Class I oral examination. They are not a textbook of record, a legal opinion or professional advice, and they do not cover every topic an examiner may ask about.

2. Source and material nature. This study standard constitutes an independent, heavily modernised, and updated edition compiled by MIW. While it references historical community study aids originally drafted by Sandeep Lohan, all technical content has been cross-referenced, verified, and re-authored directly from public international statutory frameworks (IMO, ILO, and Classification Society rules).

3. Not official. These notes are not approved, endorsed or issued by DGMA, any Mercantile Marine Department, the IMO, any classification society or any examining body. Where MIW relates a topic to the MEO Class I syllabus, that mapping is MIW's own editorial work.

4. Content currentness. While MIW has updated these materials to reflect regulatory frameworks up to October 2026, maritime regulations change frequently. This text is an educational guide, and candidates must verify the latest official circulars before sitting for exams.

5. Examination references. Questions, examiner names and examiner tendencies are drawn from candidate reports and MIW's own records. They show what has been asked, not what will be asked, and they are not a statement of any examiner's or examining body's practice.

6. Not for operational use. These notes do not replace the ship's approved documents, the Safety Management System, manufacturers' instructions, flag-State or class requirements, or the judgement of a qualified engineer. Do not use them to operate, maintain, survey or certify ship systems.
