> ### Two scope notes you must carry into the exam
>
> **1. The Act named in the syllabus is not the Act in the papers.** Item 11
> says *"the Marine Insurance Act 1906"* — the United Kingdom statute. Every
> written question in the MIW corpus that names an Act names the **Indian
> Marine Insurance Act 1963** (`QP2303-Q3`, `QP2306-Q3`, `QP2401-Q1`,
> `QP2501-Q6`, `QP2603-Q3`, `QP2608-Q4` and others). The 1963 Act is closely
> modelled on the 1906 Act, so the *principles* are the same — but **cite the
> 1963 Act** in an Indian examination and know that the syllabus wording points
> at its parent. Do not "correct" one to the other; know both and say which you
> are using.
>
> **2. Item 19 is the D02/D03 seam, and it runs the other way from item 11.**
> Budgeting and cost control are D02's official scope, but the ship-operating-
> cost written questions (`QP2312-Q6`, `QP2402-Q6`, `QP2606-Q6`) are filed
> `Human Element & Management` in their specs and therefore map to **D03**.
> If you have done Topic 02 you have already met them under D03 branch 12.
> Re-read them here through the *cost* lens rather than the *inventory* lens;
> do not study them a third time.

## MENTAL SKELETON

Nine branches. This topic rewards a skeleton more than any other, because
almost every question is a *relationship* between two concepts — GA and
particular average, P&I and H&M, salvage and GA, lien and arrest — and the
examiner's follow-up is nearly always the boundary between them.

1. **The principles of marine insurance** — insurable interest, utmost good
   faith (*uberrimae fidei*) and disclosure, indemnity, proximate cause,
   subrogation, contribution. *Asked directly and asked as the frame for
   everything else.*
2. **The insurance contract's machinery** — the policy, warranties (express and
   implied), deviation and its lawful excuses, perils of the sea and the
   Inchmaree clause, due diligence, the Sue and Labour clause, Institute Time
   Clauses Hulls, war risk cover and additional premium.
3. **Types of loss** — actual total loss, constructive total loss and the CTL
   test, particular average, general average, salvage charges, sue-and-labour
   charges. *Knowing which bucket a loss falls into is half of D02.*
4. **General Average** — the GA act, Rule A and the numbered Rules of the
   York-Antwerp Rules, common maritime adventure, extraordinary sacrifice and
   expenditure, contributory value, the average adjuster, GA security and
   bonds, the New Jason Clause, and the *port of refuge* case. **This is the
   single largest cluster in the topic.**
5. **P&I and H&M** — mutuality, the club, calls (advance, supplementary,
   overspill), the International Group and the pooling arrangement, certificate
   of entry and blue card, what P&I covers that H&M does not, the 3/4ths
   collision liability split, and the class-maintenance condition of cover.
6. **Salvage** — salvage vs towage, LOF and "no cure no pay", Article 13 award
   criteria, Article 14 special compensation, SCOPIC, the salvor's duties, place
   of safety, and how a salvage award interacts with GA.
7. **Liability and its limits** — LLMC 1976 and the 1996 Protocol, the
   limitation fund, heads of claim, conduct barring limitation; the pollution
   liability chain CLC 92 → Fund → Supplementary Fund → Bunkers Convention →
   Nairobi Wreck Removal → HNS; strict liability, channelling, and compulsory
   insurance.
8. **Admiralty and enforcement** — maritime lien vs statutory right in rem, in
   rem vs in personam, arrest of ships, order of priority, forced sale, the
   Admiralty (Jurisdiction and Settlement of Maritime Claims) Act 2017, and the
   Chief Engineer's exposure.
9. **Carriage and the commercial contract** — the bill of lading as receipt,
   evidence of contract and document of title; Hague / Hague-Visby / Hamburg /
   Rotterdam and the carrier's defences; charter parties (time, voyage,
   bareboat), laytime and demurrage, off-hire, safe port, and the CE's own
   charter-party duties — fuel management, sampling, machinery maintenance.

> ### Mapping defect — FOUND, AND NOW CORRECTED
>
> The previous edition of this pack recorded that **19 of D02's then-134
> orals were not commercial law or insurance at all**, and filed it as debt on
> the grounds that repairing it meant a taxonomy change. That reasoning was
> wrong in a useful way: the repair did not need the file→domain map touched
> at all. On **2026-08-23** it was carried out through the governed mapping
> system, and this pack now reflects the corrected boundary.
>
> **What was actually wrong.** A QB file title is a reliable statement about
> the *file* and an unreliable one about any particular *question* inside it.
> `map_oral_question` returned on the file-title hit and never looked at the
> question's own text, so a broad file such as `QB1_A` ("Conventions &
> Liability Law") minted HIGH-confidence D02 mappings for pollution
> conventions, UNCLOS jurisdiction and seafarer welfare — and because they
> were HIGH, they never entered the review queue where they would have been
> caught. **A high-confidence mapping was a claim about the file, not about
> the question.**
>
> **What changed.**
>
> 1. **The engine** (`mapping_engine.py`) now reads the question's own cues
>    even when the file title has already spoken. Agreement or silence keeps
>    HIGH; **contradiction** is demoted to MEDIUM / `REVIEW_PENDING`, keeping
>    the file's topic as a placeholder so no topic is emptied on a suspicion.
>    Records carry a new `mapping_evidence` field (what was read) alongside
>    `mapping_confidence` (what was decided) so a strong source can no longer
>    masquerade as a settled decision.
> 2. **Twenty questions were reassigned** by hand adjudication, because most
>    of them carry no domain cue at all and no automatic rule could ever have

6. Take the examiner follow-up.

One D02-specific rule: **always answer the boundary, not just the concept.**
Nearly every follow-up in this topic is "and how is that different from …?" —
GA from particular average, P&I from H&M, salvage from towage, lien from a
statutory right in rem, CLC from Bunkers. Prepare the pairs, not the singles.
