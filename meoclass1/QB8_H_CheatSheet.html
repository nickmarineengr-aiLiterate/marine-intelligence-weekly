Dual-coding recall sheet for QB8_H (9 questions) — colour-clustered flip-cards, an LNG containment-family diagram, and confusable-pair drills for Cargo Operations: Containers, Gas & Chemical.

Don't just read the cards — try to answer from the question before tapping. That retrieval attempt (even if you get it wrong) is what builds the memory trace, not re-reading the answer. Tick "Reviewed — confident" only once you can answer without tapping.

Concept Map — LNG Containment Families (IGC Ch. 4)

Secondary-barrier matrix: membrane & Type A = complete; Type B = partial (leak-before-failure earns it); Type C = none. That matrix plus the brittle-fracture argument is the whole examination answer.

Confusable Pairs — Examiner Favourites

⚠ Confusable Pair: Ship Type 1/2/3 vs Pollution Category X/Y/Z
Ship type (IBC Ch. 2, column e of Ch. 17) = safety/survivability grading — tank location and size. X/Y/Z (MARPOL Annex II Reg 6) = pollution hazard — discharge/prewash rules. A product has BOTH; they answer different questions.
⚠ Confusable Pair: Parametric vs Synchronous Rolling
Synchronous = classic resonance, encounter period = natural roll period, beam seas. Parametric = restoring moment (GM) itself fluctuates as waves pass the fine bow/wide stern; builds at half-period in head/following seas — the container-ship signature (MSC.1/Circ.1228).
⚠ Confusable Pair: TML vs FMP — and what the can test proves
FMP = lab-determined Flow Moisture Point. TML = 90% of FMP — the carriage limit. The can test (25 strikes, 0.2 m, 1–2 s) can only RAISE the alarm and force lab testing; it can never clear a cargo for loading.
⚠ Confusable Pair: Single/Two-Stage vs Cascade vs Indirect
Selection = condensing temperature vs cooling medium + cargo chemistry: butane ≈0 °C → single-stage; propane −42 °C → two-stage (ratio split, intercooler); ethylene −104 °C → cascade (closed refrigerant loop as the cold sink); polymerising cargo → indirect (never compress the cargo).
⚠ Confusable Pair: MARVS & Relief Settings — Universal vs Ship-Specific
Boiling points are physics (propane −42, butane −0.5, LNG −162 °C) — quote them. MARVS, PRV settings, pump discharge pressures, BOR, filling limits are certificate/manual values — answer "per my Certificate of Fitness / approved cargo operating manual", never a memorised universal number.

Topic Clusters — Tap to Recall

Container & Bulk Operations
Q1. Container vessel common problems
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Four clusters: seakeeping (parametric/synchronous rolling, GM variation, whipping); cargo (lashing/stack collapse, VGM SOLAS VI/2 since 1 Jul 2016, mis-declared DG fires); fire-fighting limitation (shielded seat; II-2/10.7 mist lances/monitors, builds ≥2016); machinery/ops (high-load plants, ECA changeovers, bridge visibility V/22). Casualties: APL China, ONE Apus, Maersk Honam, MOL Comfort.
Q5. Onboard IMSBC test (can test)
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
IMSBC Sec. 8.4: half-fill ~0.5–1 L can, strike on hard surface from ~0.2 m, 25 times at 1–2 s intervals. Free moisture/fluid state → do NOT load; lab test required. Cannot clear a cargo — only the lab does (moisture cert ≤7 days; TML cert ≤6 months; TML = 90% FMP). Groups: A liquefy, B chemical, C neither.
Chemical Tankers (IBC)
Q2. Type 1 vs Type 2 chemical tankers
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
IBC Ch. 2: survivability graded to hazard. Type 1: B/5 (≤11.5 m) side, B/15 (≤6 m) bottom, tank ≤1,250 m³. Type 2: ≥760 mm from shell, B/15 bottom, tank ≤3,000 m³. Type 3: no special location rule. Assignment: Ch. 17 column (e); certified via Certificate of Fitness. Higher type may carry lower-type cargo, never the reverse.
Q3. Chemicals carried & reference in doubt
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Families: petrochemicals/aromatics, alcohols & glycols, acids & caustics, veg/animal oils, additives/esters, molten sulphur/asphalt. Lookup chain in order: IBC Ch. 17/18 line → Certificate of Fitness list → P&A Manual → SDS → MEPC.2/Circ for unlisted products. Not listed + not assessed = no carriage.
Q4. Verifying first-time tank cleaning
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Three layers: COVERAGE — shadow diagrams per machine, drops/rotation/pressure per P&A Manual & maker data; PROCESS — monitor rotation, pressure/temp, continuous stripping, logged sequence; RESULT — permit-entry inspection of shadows/sumps/coils + surveyor wall-wash (chlorides, hydrocarbons, permanganate time, appearance) against charterer spec. Limits are commercial, not statutory.
Gas Carriers (IGC)
Q6. LPG reliquefaction & relief settings
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Fully refrigerated at atmospheric b.p.: propane −42 °C, n-butane −0.5 °C. Cycle: compress BOG → condense vs seawater → expand/return via spray. Watch: incondensables (purge), liquid carry-over, oil. MARVS & pump discharge = ship-specific certificate/manual values; ≥2 PRVs per tank, fire-case sized (IGC Ch. 8).
Q7. Reliquefaction plant types
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Direct: single-stage (butane), two-stage + intercooler (propane/ammonia), cascade — cargo condensed against closed refrigerant loop (ethylene −104 °C). Indirect: cargo never compressed — polymerising cargoes. Selection = condensing temp vs cooling medium + cargo chemistry. Fitted plant named in Certificate of Fitness/cargo manual.
Q8. LNG carriage temp/pressure & maintenance
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
−162 °C, near-atmospheric. Auto-refrigeration: boiling liquid — heat ingress leaves as latent heat; temperature control IS pressure control. BOG destinations: fuel (DF/ME-GI/X-DF or GCU), reliquefaction where fitted, bounded pressure rise. BOR ~0.10–0.15%/day design [ship-specific]; N₂-swept interbarrier spaces; filling/sloshing limits per loading manual.
Q9. LNG containment types & materials
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Membrane: Mark III 1.2 mm 304L corrugated / NO96 0.7 mm Invar (36% Ni, near-zero α) — COMPLETE secondary barrier. Type B Moss: Al 5083 / 9% Ni sphere, leak-before-failure analysis → PARTIAL barrier (drip tray). Type C: pressure vessels, no barrier — bunker/small-scale. Root logic: no ductile–brittle transition at −162 °C (IGC Ch. 6); 15-day basis (4.7).
