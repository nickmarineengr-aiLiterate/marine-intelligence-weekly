## 1. THE SITTING, AND WHY IT IS THE HARDEST DIRECTION OF PULL

February 2024 is an **early-2024** sitting. Every solved donor available to it is a **2025 or
2026** answer. The donor is therefore always a *later* answer pulled *backwards*, and the
standing rule in `DESKTOP_QP_ALLOCATION_2024.md` §6.3 applies to all nine questions:

> Any currency correction a donor's author made *because their sitting was later* must be
> **reversed**, not inherited.

This paper has a second, sharper edge. February 2024 falls in a **five-week window** between
two IMO boundaries:

| Boundary | Date | Position at this sitting |
|---|---|---|
| 33rd IMO Assembly resolutions adopted | **6 December 2023** | **BEFORE** the sitting — the `A.11xx(33)` editions are operative |
| **MEPC 81** | **18–22 March 2024** | **AFTER** the sitting — nothing from MEPC 81 exists yet |
| MEPC 82 | 30 Sep – 4 Oct 2024 | AFTER |
| 34th Assembly | 3 December 2025 | AFTER |

**The most recent MEPC at this sitting is MEPC 80 (3–7 July 2023).** This single fact governs
Q5 and constrains Q7. It was established from IMO's published meeting record, not assumed.

### 3.1 Q6 — EXACT stem, 28-month backward pull

`QP2606-Q6` (June 2026) and `QP2402-Q6` print **character-identical** stems. This is the

### 3.2 Q2 — the donor the intake missed

`QP2509-Q7` read the **2001 Bunkers Convention and the 1992 CLC in full**. Both instruments
were long in force at February 2024 (Bunkers in force 21 November 2008; CLC 92 in force 1996)
and **no amendment boundary sits near this sitting**, so the substantive content transfers
with the lowest temporal risk in the paper.

**The stems are NOT identical.** QP2509 sets four limbs — pollution damage *and exclusions*
(4), time limits (4), liability *and limitation* (4), compulsory insurance (4).
QP2402 sets four **defined terms** — pollution damage, bunker oil, time limits for claims,
exclusions — against an undifferentiated 16, and asks additionally for **background and
relevance**. **"Bunker oil" is a definition QP2509 was never asked for**, and compulsory
insurance is not a printed limb here. The answer is re-shaped to the printed question, not
copied.

**Neither treaty text is held in the corpus.** The substantive content is therefore carried as
`INTERNAL_REUSE_VERIFIED` from `QP2509-Q7`, whose own verification records reading both texts
in full, and is stated at the level that verification established. This is recorded as an
accepted limitation, not disguised as a primary reading.
