## 2. THE 34TH ASSEMBLY — SHARPER THAN "DECEMBER 2025"

The QP2509 incident (`A.1207(34)` carried into a September 2025 sitting) established the
Assembly boundary. For this paper it needs stating more precisely, because the naive form of
the rule is *"December 2025 is after November 2025"* and that is not quite what happened.

**The 34th Assembly convened on 24 November 2025 — inside the sitting month — and adopted its
resolutions at the close of the session, through 3 December 2025.**

The exact calendar date of this examination within November 2025 is **not recorded on the
source copy**. The boundary is nevertheless unambiguous, because adoption occurred at the end
of the session, not at its opening: `A.1207(34)` is dated **3 December 2025**.

**Nothing in the `A.12xx(34)` family may be treated as existing at this sitting.** That
includes, on the Assembly's own record of 22 resolutions:

- the **2025** Survey Guidelines under the Harmonized System of Survey and Certification;
- the **2025** Procedures for Port State Control;
- the Code on Alerts and Indicators, **2025**;
- the **2025** Non-exhaustive list of obligations relevant to the III Code;
- the second-cycle IMO Member State Audit Scheme framework, the Integrated IMO Identification
  Number Scheme, and the new Capacity Development Strategy.

**Where an Assembly-level instrument is needed, the 33rd Assembly edition of 6 December 2023
is the operative one** — the corpus already holds `A.1187(33)` primary-verified on that basis
(`QP2403-Q7`).

### Why this bites on Q5 specifically

`QP2511-Q5` (Enhanced Survey Programme) is donated by `QP2603-Q6`, sat **March 2026**. A March
2026 answer may legitimately cite the **2025** HSSC Survey Guidelines. At November 2025 that
citation is a **future instrument**. This is the exact backwards-regression this document
exists to catch, and it is invisible to any check that only asks whether the donor was correct
for the donor.

## 3. THE MERCHANT SHIPPING ACT 2025 BOUNDARY

Established **repository-first** and primary-verified in the corpus: the `QP2607/Q7`
verification record read the Gazette text **and** the commencement notification **S.O. 1244(E)**
directly.

```
Bill introduced (Lok Sabha)  10 December 2024
Passed                        6 and 11 August 2025
Assent                        18 August 2025      -> Act No. 24 of 2025
COMMENCEMENT                  15 March 2026        <-- the operative line
Repeal of the 1958 Act        s.324(1)
```

**At November 2025 the Act existed and had received assent, but had not commenced.** Assent is
not commencement. The **Merchant Shipping Act 1958** is the operative Indian statute for this
paper, in full.

This is a **backwards** trap of the most dangerous kind: the modern answer is coherent,
confident and wrong for the sitting.

Engaged on **Q8** (Indian ship recycling) and available on **Q3** (Indian health certificates
and issuing authority).

## 6. WHAT MUST NOT ENTER ANY ANSWER ON THIS PAPER

- Any `A.12xx(34)` resolution, or any **2025** edition of an Assembly instrument.
- The **Merchant Shipping Act 2025** as operative law.
- FAL 50 (March 2026) outcomes; FAL 49 items described as **adopted** or **in force**.
- The IMO Net-Zero Framework described as **adopted** or **in force**.
- Any donor sentence dated to a 2026 sitting — "recently", "currently", "this year".
- Any internal `Q`-reference inherited from a donor: `QP2508-Q7` carries **"see Q8"**,
  **"Q8 of this paper"** and **"See Q5"**, which point at QP2508's own paper. On QP2511, Q8 is
  the Hong Kong Convention — an unrelated question. This is the QP2509 defect exactly, and it
  is present in the forward donor, not the backward ones.
