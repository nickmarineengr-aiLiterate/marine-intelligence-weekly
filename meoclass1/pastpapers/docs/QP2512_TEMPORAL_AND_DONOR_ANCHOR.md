## 2. THE 34TH ASSEMBLY — WHY DECEMBER IS HARDER THAN NOVEMBER, AND WHY IT STILL RESOLVES

For `QP2511` the boundary was awkward but decidable: the Assembly *convened* inside the sitting
month yet *adopted* at the close, so a November paper is cleanly before adoption. **December is
the one month where a sitting can fall on either side of 3 December 2025.** Establishing which
is the first task of this paper.

### The examination date is NOT printed

The source copy was read back in full this session — text layer plus both pages rendered at
150 dpi. It prints **`DECEMBER 2025`** and the serial **`EM – 2512`**. **No day-level
examination date appears anywhere on the paper.** The governed convention when the exact date
cannot be established is to treat the `A.12xx(34)` family as **excluded rather than assumed
available** (`CURRENT_STATUS.md` §7).

### But the exclusion here is REASONED, not merely defaulted

Defaulting would leave the question open. It is not open, and the reason is a document date
rather than an adoption date:

```
34th Assembly session          24 November – 3 December 2025
A.1206(34) ADOPTED             3 December 2025
A 34/Res.1206 ISSUED           5 December 2025      <-- the document date
```

Verified from the resolution's own cover: `A 34/Res.1206`, dated **5 December 2025**,
*"adopted on 3 December 2025"*. **The Organization had not circulated the instrument until
5 December.** An examination sat at any point in December 2025 is therefore being sat against a
body of law in which the 34th Assembly texts either did not yet exist or had existed for a
matter of days and had not been issued. **No `A.12xx(34)` resolution may appear in any answer
on this paper**, and the conclusion does not depend on pinning the sitting to a day.

### 2.1 The trap this paper actually carries — a re-issued instrument with THREE editions

`WORKFLOW_LESSONS.md` predicted that the practical consequence of an Assembly boundary is *"a
whole family of instruments, not one resolution"*, and named the Procedures for Port State
Control as one of them. On this paper that prediction lands, and it lands with an extra step
that a two-edition mental model gets wrong:

| Edition | Resolution | Adopted | Revokes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procedures for PSC, **2021** | `A.1155(32)` | 15 December 2021 | `A.1138(31)` and predecessors |
| Procedures for PSC, **2023** | **`A.1185(33)`** | **6 December 2023** | `A.1155(32)` |
| Procedures for PSC, **2025** | `A.1206(34)` | 3 December 2025 | `A.1185(33)` |

Both revocations were read from the resolutions' own operative paragraph 4. **The operative
edition at a December 2025 sitting is `A.1185(33)`.**

Two distinct ways to get this wrong, and this paper is exposed to both:

- **Forwards.** `QP2606-Q2` (June 2026, Port State Control and the Right of Appeal) is built
  **entirely on `A.1206(34)`** — its own verification record states that the superseded-edition
  trap was caught and that `A.1206(34)` is operative *for June 2026*. That is correct for the
  donor and **wrong for this target**. `QP2606-Q2` is the natural support for `QP2512-Q8`'s PSC
  limbs and every citation drawn from it must be re-anchored onto `A.1185(33)`.
- **Backwards.** Reaching past `A.1185(33)` to `A.1155(32)` on the reasoning *"the 2021
  Procedures were current before the 2025 ones"* skips an entire edition. `A.1155(32)` was
  revoked two years before this sitting.

`A.1185(33)` was obtained from the IMO resolutions CDN and read this session. Chapter 3
(contravention and detention), §3.5 guidance for detention, §3.7 rectification and release,
Chapter 4 reporting and Appendix 2 (detainable deficiencies) are the load-bearing sections for
`QP2512-Q8`.

PREMATURE premise"* is live on both: a donor may reach the correct 1958-Act conclusion while
*reasoning* from the 2025 Act's repeal provision. Check the premise separately from the
conclusion.

### Q1 — the compressed stem, now uncompressed

`QP2512-Q1`'s printed stem is near-identical to `QP2601-Q6` (January 2026): four demands — WHO
role, national agencies, **certificates issued to a vessel**, **who may issue them in India**
plus validity and extension. `QP2511-Q3` (November 2025) is the *compressed* variant that folds
the issuing-authority demand into *"the different certificates in India"*.

`WORKFLOW_LESSONS.md` *"A compressed stem is a CHANGED TASK, not a shorter one"* was written
about exactly this pair, in the other direction. **Here the compression is being undone.**
`QP2511-Q3` remains the preferred donor because it is one month away, requires no backwards
pull, and was itself authored from `QP2601-Q6` — but the **issuing-authority limb must be

A November answer covers December only if that limb is explicit.

### Q9 — exact, and the forward donor is the one to sweep

`QP2512-Q9` is **character-identical** to both `QP2509-Q4` (September 2025) and `QP2404-Q3`

### Q8 — a composite, and the only question with a backwards limb

must be re-anchored onto `A.1185(33)`** — see §2.1. This is the single highest-risk adaptation

## 6. WHAT MUST NOT ENTER ANY ANSWER ON THIS PAPER

- Any `A.12xx(34)` resolution — in particular **`A.1206(34)`**, and any **2025** edition of an
  Assembly instrument.
- **`A.1155(32)`** as the current Procedures for Port State Control. It was revoked in 2023.
- The **Merchant Shipping Act 2025** as operative law, *and equally* any reasoning that depends
  on its repeal provision having taken effect.
- The IMO Net-Zero Framework described as **adopted** or **in force**.
- FAL 50 (March 2026) outcomes; FAL 49 items described as adopted or in force.
- Any donor sentence dated to a 2026 sitting — *"recently"*, *"currently"*, *"this year"*.
- Any internal `Q`-reference inherited from a donor. **Sweep the forward donors as hard as the
  backward one** — `QP2509-Q4` and `QP2511-Q3`/`Q4` are forward and are not exempt.
- Any authoring-date reference (2026 months) on a candidate-facing surface.
