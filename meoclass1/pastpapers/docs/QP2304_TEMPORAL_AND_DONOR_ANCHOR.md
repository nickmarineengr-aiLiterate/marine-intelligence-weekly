## 3. THE THREE BOUNDARIES THAT ACTUALLY BITE

### 3.1 The IMSBC 06-21 boundary — adopted, not in force, voluntarily applicable

> **`MSC.500(105)` was adopted 28 April 2022 and enters into force 1 December 2023. This paper was
> sat in April 2023 — inside the window between adoption and entry into force, and inside the
> voluntary-application window that opened 1 January 2023.**

Read from the resolution itself:

| Operative clause | Text |
|---|---|
| 1 ADOPTS | amendments to the IMSBC Code |
| 2 DETERMINES | deemed accepted **1 June 2023** |
| 3 INVITES … to note | shall **enter into force 1 December 2023** |
| 4 **AGREES** | Contracting Governments **may apply … in whole or in part on a voluntary basis as from 1 January 2023** |

**Consequence honoured in Q1.** The mandatory carriage requirement at this sitting is the Code as
amended by **Amendment 05-19**. Amendment 06-21 is described as *adopted and open to voluntary
application*, never as *in force*, and no 06-21 change is presented as a requirement. This is the
`adopted ≠ accepted ≠ in force` distinction landing precisely inside the sitting window, and it is
the single most examinable temporal fact on this paper.

**It also protects the answer.** The two bauxite schedules a candidate must know — `BAUXITE`
(Group C) and `BAUXITE FINES` (Group A) — were introduced by **05-19**, which *is* mandatory here.
So the substantive answer is unaffected by which side of 1 December 2023 the reader stands on. The
boundary is stated because it is true, not because the marks depend on it.

### 3.2 The 33rd Assembly is unambiguously future — unlike QP2312

The 33rd Assembly adopted `A.1185(33)`, `A.1186(33)` and `A.1187(33)` on **6 December 2023 — eight

`known_traps.md` Entry 22 records the chain `A.1140(31)` → `A.1156(32)` → `A.1186(33)` →

`DESKTOP_QP_ALLOCATION_2023.md` §4 predicted **7 exact/near, 0 family, 7 / 9 with a donor**, and §5
predicted the donors would be *"concentrated in `QP2509` and `QP2503`"*. That table was recomputed

**Recomputed against 270 built questions across 30 papers — the 252 questions in 28 papers on the
baseline, plus `QP2301` and `QP2312` at nine each on their own branches. Result: 9 / 9 with a
donor.** Nine exact or near, zero family-only, **zero fresh-research questions.**

**The prediction was a lower bound produced by a tooling defect, and the defect is now proved.**
The 2023 intake extractor reads a question only where the printed marker is followed by a space.
This paper prints **`Q6.Recent`** and **`Q8.a)`** with no space after the point:

```
regex  Q\d\.     → Q1 Q2 Q3 Q4 Q5 Q6 Q7 Q8 Q9     (9 — the true count)
regex  Q\d\.\s   → Q1 Q2 Q3 Q4 Q5    Q7    Q9     (7 — what the extractor saw)
```

The two questions the extractor could not see are **exactly Q6 and Q8** — and both turn out to have
**character-identical donors** (`QP2502-Q8` and `QP2509-Q8`). The readiness figure was therefore
never "seven of nine questions have donors"; it was "all seven questions I could see have donors,
and two are invisible to me". **`DESKTOP_QP_ALLOCATION_2023.md` §1.3 already warned that an
automated extractor under-reads the 2023 papers. This is the mechanism, demonstrated.**

**Referred:** §4 of the allocation board should be recomputed from a manual question count for the
five unstarted 2023 papers, because any paper whose printed markers omit a space carries the same
understated readiness. This branch did not edit the board.

Derived from the §6 table: **4 character-identical** stems, **5 near** (wording, printed marks, or a

### 8.1 The SOLAS base is later than the sitting, and the direction was checked

**The corpus SOLAS base is the Consolidated Edition 2024**, incorporating amendments in effect
1 July 2024 — **fifteen months after this sitting.** The provisions consumed are **II-1/3-1** and
**chapter I regulations 8, 9 and 10**. Each was checked against the corpus amendment register and
the filed post-2024 amendment resolutions (`MSC.520(106)`, `MSC.532(107)`, `MSC.550(108)`,
`MSC.557(108)`): **none touches II-1/3-1 or chapter I**, and II-1/3-1 has been unamended since it
entered into force on 1 July 2002. The 2024 text therefore states the April 2023 position for both.
Recorded because the direction of travel must be checked on every consumption, not assumed.

### 8.2 A transcription artefact shared by the source and the corpus — recorded, not corrected

The exam paper prints **`SOLAS ch.ll-1`**, using two lowercase L's for the Roman numeral II. The
corpus copy of SOLAS renders the cross-reference inside II-1/3-1 as **`regulation Xl-1/1`**, with a
lowercase L for the second I of XI. **Both are text-layer artefacts of the same class, in two
independent documents.** Neither is corrected. The exam's form is preserved in `text_verbatim` as
the house rule requires; the corpus's form is noted here so that a future reader does not treat it
as a corpus defect. The correct references are **chapter II-1** and **regulation XI-1/1**.
