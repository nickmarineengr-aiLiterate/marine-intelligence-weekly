### 2.2 The two instruments that turn on 1 January 2023 — the live edge of this paper

Both entered into force **within days of the sitting**, and both are therefore stated as *newly*
applicable rather than as settled practice.

1. **Cybutryne under the AFS Convention** (`MEPC.331(76)`, in force **1 January 2023**) — bears
   directly on **Q2**. A January 2023 answer must include cybutryne, because it is in force; but
   the compliance machinery is at its very beginning. The **60-month outer limit** for removing or
   over-coating a cybutryne system had only just started to run, and the transitional arrangements
   for the amended form of the **International Anti-fouling System Certificate** were at their
   widest, so a ship could legitimately still hold the older certificate form.
2. **EEXI and the operational CII** under revised Annex VI chapter 4 — bears on **Q1**. Applicable
   from 1 January 2023, but **EEXI is verified at the first annual, intermediate or renewal IAPP
   survey on or after that date**, and **2023 is the first CII data year, so the first ratings are
   not assigned until 2024**. At a January 2023 sitting these are *incoming obligations being
   implemented*, not a mature regime, and Q1 says so.
