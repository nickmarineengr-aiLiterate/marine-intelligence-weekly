| Instrument | Class | Note |
|---|---|---|

> **NEW STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENT FOR THE CORPUS.** Every prior paper demanded MARPOL at
> **Annex/regulation** level (Annex VI reg 14, Annex I, and so on). This question demands the
> **Articles** of the parent Convention, down to the third level of sub-paragraph. A corpus
> whose MARPOL object model stops at "Annex VI, regulation 14" cannot resolve
> `MARPOL-Articles-16-2-f-iii`, and this question needs exactly that.

## 4. Corpus temporal model — the live-list pattern reaches a FOURTH instance

March recorded that a **live external list is not a document state** and handed the requirement
to the corpus team as an architectural need. April supplies a fourth instance.

> **National and regional biofouling arrival requirements.** Several States operate their own
> biofouling standards for arriving ships. These are neither editions nor amendment states;
> they are continuously revised third-party regimes whose *current* value is operationally
> load-bearing. **The answer reproduces none of them and quotes no threshold**, describing only
> that they exist and must be checked before arrival.

The pattern now stands at **four instances across four papers**:

| Instance | Paper |
|---|---|
| Authorised Indian ports for ship sanitation certificates | QP2601 Q6 |
| Joint War Committee Listed Areas | QP2603 Q3 |
| EU-approved ship recycling facilities | QP2603 Q9 |
| **National / regional biofouling arrival requirements** | **QP2604 Q5** |

**A fifth, and a different shape:** the **United Nations treaty status record** used for Q7 is
also a live external register, but its *historical* entries — India signed 10 December 1982,
ratified 29 June 1995 — are **stable and citable**. This is a useful distinction for the corpus
model: a live register may still have **immutable historical facts**, and those are safe to
hold as corpus objects even when the register's current state is not.

**Not solved in QP. No workaround was invented.**

### A second structural requirement, new from April

**MARPOL object identity must reach the Articles of the parent Convention, not only the
Annexes,** and must resolve to the third level of sub-paragraph — `16(2)(f)(iii)` carries a
different rule from `16(2)(f)(i)`, and the whole of Q1 turns on that difference.
