## 2. WHAT THE EXISTING PACKAGES ACTUALLY DELIVERED

Two packages applied, and both changed the answer rather than decorating it.

### 2.1 `salvage` → Q4

`ART14_VS_SCOPIC.md` confirmed the three percentages the question turns on and which legal layer each
belongs to: **25 %** SCOPIC standard bonus (contract), **30 %** Article 14.2 standard uplift
(Convention), **100 %** Article 14.2 absolute ceiling on the total increase (Convention). Q4 states
all three correctly and attributes each to the right layer, so the classic merged-percentage trap
does not fire.

**But the package could not settle the clause itself.** `SALVAGE_INSTRUMENT_REGISTER.md` records only
*"1999; SCOPIC 2020 current series"* — no effective date and no clause text. The published SCOPIC 2020
clause was therefore read at source, and it both confirmed and falsified:

| Claim | Outcome |
|---|---|
| SCOPIC 2020 effective 1 January 2020 | **CONFIRMED** — the clause's own printed revision list ends `1.1.2020` |
| 25 % standard bonus on tariff rates | **CONFIRMED** — clause 5(iv) |
| Initial security within 2 working days | **CONFIRMED** — clause 3(i), excluding weekends and Lloyd's holidays |
| Article 13 award discounted by 25 % of the difference | **CONFIRMED** — clause 7 |
| *"Shipowner's Casualty Representative"* | **FALSIFIED** — clause 12 defines the **Special** Casualty Representative |
| The SCR *"attends"* | **FALSIFIED** — clause 12: the owners *"may at their sole option appoint"* one |

**`TSCR-5` (duplicate object IDs) did not bite.** No object ID was consumed from the salvage package;
the boundary document was used as a comparison table and the clause identity was resolved from
content, as the standing instruction requires.

### 2.2 `general-average` → Q6

`YAR_DEFINITIONS.json` supplied Rule A, Rule C and **Rule D** verbatim. Q6's Rule D was already
correct in substance and the defective gloss was **not** followed — see §3. The verbatim text
nevertheless improved it: the answer carried *"without prejudice to any remedies"* and the Rule says
*"any remedies **or defences** which may be open **against or to** that party"*. The defences half and
the two-way character are now restored, which is the residue the New Jason Clause exists to close —
the same point `QP2407-Q8` turned on.

No duplicate object IDs exist in this package (checked: 5/5 unique in both
`YAR_DEFINITIONS.json` and `YAR_SEQUENCE.json`).

### 2.3 The three packages that did not apply

`casualty/`, `wreck-removal/` and `contract-of-carriage/` were fit-tested and **none applies to any
question on this paper**. `casualty/` was tested explicitly rather than dismissed on its name, and its
instrument register confirms it holds SOLAS Chapter V, COLREGs 1972 and the SAR Convention 1979.
