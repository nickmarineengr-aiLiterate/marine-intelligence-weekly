### 3.1 The one boundary that is genuinely different in April 2025

**The Mediterranean Sea ECA sulphur limit does not bite until 1 May 2025 — the day after
this sitting month closes.**

MEPC.361(79) entered into force 1 May 2024 and inserted the Mediterranean Sea ECA at
regulation 14.3.5. But the corpus `amendment-register.json` records the resolution's own
operative paragraph 4, which applies regulation 14.7: ships operating in the Med ECA are
**exempt from regulations 14.4 and 14.6, and from 14.5 insofar as it relates to 14.4, until
1 May 2025 (printed date)**.

So at **every** day of April 2025 the Mediterranean is a designated ECA whose **0.10 % m/m
fuel-oil sulphur limit is not yet in effect**. This is established here for the first time —
it is not inherited from the January, February or March 2025 anchors, none of which had to
engage it — and it is **day-independent within April**, because the exemption runs to the end
of the month.

No QP2504 question turns on it. It is recorded because a donor pulled forward from a later
sitting could import "the Med ECA 0.10 % limit applies" as though it were true in April 2025,
and it was not.

### 6.3 Q9 — the tool's preferred donor is a POST-SITTING paper and is refused

`donor_readiness` ranked **`QP2510-Q5` (October 2025)** above `QP2403-Q5` (March 2024) because
both stems are exact and October 2025 is six months from the sitting where March 2024 is
thirteen. **The ranking function measures the *size* of the temporal gap and is blind to its
*sign*.** That is correct for its job and wrong for this decision.

`DESKTOP_QP_PRODUCTION_PLAYBOOK.md` §8 states it directly: **a later solved donor does not
transfer later law backwards.** QP2510 is a **six-month post-sitting** paper. Its cyber answer
was authored against October 2025 law and every sitting-relative phrase in it — "the current
edition", "recently issued" — is anchored to a month this paper has not reached.

than the sitting and therefore incapable of importing later law. `QP2510-Q5` was **read for

QP2510-Q5 and absent from QP2403-Q5 was treated as **suspected post-sitting content** and
re-verified against the April 2025 position before use; nothing survived that test that was
not independently establishable.

This is the first refusal of a tool-preferred donor in Batch 2 and it is recorded as a
workflow finding: **`donor_readiness` should not be read as an authorisation where the
candidate's sitting index exceeds the target's.**
