## 1. WHY THIS PAPER IS TEMPORALLY UNUSUAL

QP2406 is the **earliest sitting in the entire MIW solved set**. Every other solved paper
sits later in time, so every donor available to this paper was authored for a **later**
sitting and had to be **reversed** rather than inherited. That is the opposite of the
normal MIW case, where a donor pre-dates its recipient and cannot drag later law backwards.

The consequence is a standing rule for this paper, applied on every question:

> **A later donor can import law that did not exist at this sitting. No donor statement was
> inherited. Every sitting-relative statement was re-derived from the June 2024 position and
> the re-derivation is recorded in the question's `temporal_review`.**

There is exactly **one exception**, and it is worth naming because it is the only donor on
the paper that is temporally safe by construction: **QP2403-Q9 (March 2024)**, which
pre-dates this sitting by three months and supplies limbs (b) and (c) of Q6.

### 4.1 The two inversion traps — the corpus register is written for *current* law

The MIW corpus is maintained for the **present**. For a 2024 sitting, two of its rankings
are **inverted** and following the register uncritically would produce a wrong answer:

1. **SEEMP.** The register marks **MEPC.346(78)** as *superseded — do not cite as current*.
   At June 2024 it is the **operative** guideline and MEPC.395(82) does not yet exist.
   *(Checked on Q4; not engaged, because no question on this paper turns on SEEMP.)*
2. **PSC Assembly edition.** The register's newest edition is the 34th Assembly revision.
   At this sitting **`A.1185(33)` is operative** and the later edition is prohibited.
   *(Engaged directly on Q3, and resolved in the answer's favour.)*

A third, of the same shape, is the **ratification figure** for MLC 2006: the corpus records
110 ratifications and >96 % of world tonnage **as of September 2025**, fifteen months after
this sitting. That figure is **deliberately not carried back** — Q6 states that ratification
is very wide and gives no number.
