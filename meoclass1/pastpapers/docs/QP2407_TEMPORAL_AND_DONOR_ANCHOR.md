## 1. WHY THIS PAPER'S TEMPORAL SHAPE IS UNUSUAL

Every 2024 paper solved before this one carried the systemic warning recorded in
`DESKTOP_QP_ALLOCATION_2024.md` §6.3: *every donor available to a 2024 paper is a 2025 or
2026 answer, pulled backwards, so every currency correction made for the later sitting must
be reversed rather than inherited.*

**QP2407 is the first 2024 paper for which that is not the dominant case.** Its single
accepted donor, `QP2402-Q1`, is a **February 2024** answer — five months *earlier* than this
sitting. The donor therefore cannot import later law; the risk inverts, and the question to
ask of it is the opposite one:

> **A donor that pre-dates the sitting cannot contain amendments that arrived between the two
> dates.** The February line must be carried forward and re-checked for anything that changed
> between February and July 2024, not stripped of anything later.

That inversion is recorded here because getting it the wrong way round is the specific
failure this anchor exists to prevent. The rejected alternate donor `QP2502-Q1` (February
2025) travels the other way and is dealt with at §5.2.

## 3. THE MID-2024 BOUNDARY — THE FINDING THIS ANCHOR EXISTS FOR

The batch instruction asked specifically for anything that changed **after June but before
August 2024**. One boundary was found, it is verified at source, and it is load-bearing.

> ### SOLAS: MSC.521(106) entered into force **1 July 2024**
>
> The IMO SOLAS **Consolidated Edition 2024** states on its own title page that it
> incorporates *"all amendments in effect on 1 July 2024"*, and its foreword lists
> **MSC.521(106)** — the November 2022 amendments — as entering into force **1 July 2024**,
> the last set it carries.

The consequence is a clean three-way split across the three mid-2024 papers, and it runs the
*same* way for both Batch 3 papers:

| Sitting | Position of MSC.521(106) | Operative consolidated edition |
|---|---|---|
| **QP2406 — June 2024** | **adopted, NOT in force** | the previous consolidated edition |
| **QP2407 — July 2024** | **IN FORCE** | **Consolidated Edition 2024** |
| **QP2408 — August 2024** | **IN FORCE** | **Consolidated Edition 2024** |

The boundary is unambiguous even though **the printed paper gives no day within July**: 1
July precedes every possible July sitting date, so the amendments are in force for the whole
month whichever day the examination fell on. The same reasoning, run the other way, is what
kept them out of the June paper. **No day-level assumption is made or needed.**

This is why the QP2406 anchor recorded the 2024 consolidated edition as *future* and this one
records it as *operative*. The two statements are both correct and they are not in conflict.

**Engagement:** the boundary is live on **Q5**, which is a pure SOLAS chapter XI-1 question.
Whether MSC.521(106) itself amends chapter XI-1 is a question for Q5's own verification
record; the edition question is settled here, the amendment's content is not.

### 3.1 July → August 2024: searched, and nil

No instrument was found entering into force, being adopted, or being revoked between the
July and the August 2024 sittings. MEPC 81 (March 2024) and MSC 108 (May 2024) both precede
the pair; MEPC 82 (30 September – 4 October 2024) follows both. **This is recorded as a
searched nil, not an assumed nil** — the QP2408 anchor re-runs it independently rather than
inheriting this line.

### 4.1 The two decisive exclusions

**Q4 — the GHG question.** The stem asks for *"the IMO's revised strategy"*, its short-,
mid- and long-term goals, and the impact on industry. At July 2024 the answer is
**MEPC.377(80)** and nothing later. Everything the industry now associates with the mid-term
measures — the approved basket, the Net-Zero Framework, the GFI mechanism, the pricing
element — belongs to **2025**. This question has **no donor**, so there is no donor text to
strip; the risk is instead that present-day knowledge leaks in unmarked. The 2023 Strategy's
own *levels of ambition* and *indicative checkpoints* for 2030 and 2040 are the correct
content, and they are read at source.

**Q6 — the MLC question.** The stem asks for flag State and port State obligations under
MLC 2006. The **2022 amendments enter into force 23 December 2024, five months after this
sitting**, so the operative edition is the Convention as amended through **2018**. The corpus
holds `MLC-2022-amendments.pdf` in the same folder as the base text, which is precisely how
this goes wrong: the later file is present, readable and *inapplicable*. It must be read only
to know what to exclude. This is the same trap the QP2410 anchor recorded for October 2024
and it is confirmed live here.

### 5.1 Why the board's figures were not used

`DESKTOP_QP_ALLOCATION_BOARD.md` §5 held QP2407 back at *"1/9, reach 0, 1 HIGH flag"*, and
the older `DESKTOP_QP_ALLOCATION_2024.md` §3 recorded it at *"0/9"*. Neither figure was
carried forward. Donor readiness was **recomputed from the actual built set** at the Batch 3
baseline — **234 of 252 questions built across 26 solved papers** — using
`recurrence_model.donor_readiness` through `build_reuse_map`, the same derivation the product
uses, and then **re-adjudicated by reading both printed stems**.

A second, independent sweep was run so that the family model was not the only witness: every
QP2407 stem was compared against all 234 built stems on word-token sequence ratio *and*
Jaccard overlap. That sweep is what makes a zero defensible — it can and does fire elsewhere
(it surfaced three near-identical candidates for `QP2408-Q3`), so a nil result here means
"nothing is there", not "the filter is broken".

### 5.3 The rejected alternate, and why the rule matters

`QP2502-Q1` (February 2025) is *equally* exact — the same byte-identical stem. It was
rejected because it is **later**, and a later donor on a 2024 paper is the systemic risk
recorded at `DESKTOP_QP_ALLOCATION_2024.md` §6.3. Where two donors are equally exact, the
one that cannot import later law is preferred. `QP2502-Q1` is itself recorded as
`reused_from: QP2402-Q1`, so preferring the February 2024 answer also goes to the head of
the family rather than to a copy of it.

`QP2502-Q1` **is** read during authoring — as a check on the donor, never as a source. Any
statement present in the 2025 answer but absent from the 2024 one is treated as a candidate
post-sitting import and must be justified from the July 2024 position or dropped.
