## 1. THE SITTING DATE AND WHAT IT DECIDES

The source copy prints `DECEMBER 2024` and `Sr. No. EM - 2412`. It prints **no day of the month**.
That matters exactly once, at §1.4, and is handled there rather than guessed.

### 1.1 The 33rd IMO Assembly — adopted 6 December 2023

The 33rd Assembly adopted its resolutions on **6 December 2023**. December 2024 falls **after** that
boundary and **before** the 34th Assembly (**3 December 2025**). Therefore, for this sitting:

- `A.1186(33)` — Survey Guidelines under the HSSC, 2023 — **is the operative edition** (Q4).
- `A.1187(33)` — 2023 Non-exhaustive list of obligations — **is the operative list** (Q9).
- `A.1185(33)` — Procedures for Port State Control, 2023 — **is the operative PSC procedure** (Q3, Q9).
- **No `A.12xx(34)` instrument may appear anywhere in this paper.**

An Assembly boundary is the **adoption date**, not the meeting month.

### 1.2 The Indian statute boundary

The **Merchant Shipping Act 1958** governs throughout. The MS Act 2025 commenced **15 March 2026**
and must not appear. For Q7 the companion statutes at this sitting are the **Indian Carriage of Goods
by Sea Act 1925** (as amended in 1993 to give effect to the Hague-Visby Rules) and the **Indian Bills
of Lading Act 1856**. Their 2025 replacements did not exist at this sitting.

### 1.3 The four boundaries that moved *during* 2024 — the substance of this anchor

The intake recorded **zero temporal flags** for QP2412 and the allocation carried that figure forward.
**That figure is wrong, and this is the principal finding of this anchor.** Four instruments moved
inside 2024 itself, three of them within ninety days of the sitting:

| Boundary | Date | Question | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MEPC.395(82)**, 2024 SEEMP Guidelines | adopted **4 Oct 2024** | **Q8** | Revokes MEPC.346(78). Operative at this sitting by two months |
| **MLC 2006, 2022 amendments** | in force **23 Dec 2024** | **Q6** | Lands *inside the sitting month*. See §1.4 |
| **Paris MoU 2023 performance lists** | applied from **1 Jul 2024** | **Q3** | The White/Grey/Black list in force at this sitting |
| **MEPC.388(81)** amending the 2022 SEEMP Guidelines | **Mar 2024** | **Q8** | Superseded in turn by MEPC.395(82); recorded so the chain is complete |

A January 2024 treatment of Q8 would cite MEPC.346(78) and be **eleven months stale by December**.
This is the concrete refutation of the standing instruction's warning that "QP2401 January treatment
[must not be assumed to] remain valid eleven months later" — on this paper it demonstrably does not.

### 1.4 The MLC boundary lands inside the sitting month — how it is handled

The **2022 amendments to MLC, 2006** entered into force on **23 December 2024**. The source copy
prints no examination day, so **it cannot be established from the source whether the paper was sat
before or after 23 December.**

**This is not treated as a blocker and no date is assumed.** The answer to Q6 is authored so that it
is correct **either way**: the amendments are presented as *adopted at the 110th International Labour
Conference in June 2022 and entering into force on 23 December 2024* — that is, **at this very
sitting** — and both positions are stated expressly. The distinction *adopted / approved / in force*
is the thing the question rewards, and this sitting happens to sit exactly on the line, which makes it
the sharpest available point rather than a difficulty. Recorded under §20 of the playbook as a
boundary **established** (the 23 December date is primary and certain) with a **residual unknown**
(the examination day) that the answer is written to survive.

A second MLC point is sharper here than in most material: the **2014 amendments entered into force
18 January 2017** and added **two** matters to Appendix A5-I. At this sitting the list is therefore
**16 matters, not 14**. Almost all secondary material — including the ILO's own 2015 FAQ — still says
fourteen. Recorded as the major trap on Q6.

## 4. PER-QUESTION SITTING POSITION

### Q1 — UN Sustainable Development Goals · **CONFIRMED / LOW**
Agenda 2030 (`A/RES/70/1`, 25 Sept 2015; effective 1 Jan 2016; 17 Goals, 169 targets) is unmoved.
The maritime limb must be anchored: the **2023 IMO GHG Strategy** (`MEPC.377(80)`, July 2023) is the
operative strategy at this sitting — **not** the 2018 Initial Strategy, which it superseded. The
mid-term measures were still **under development** at MEPC 82 and must not be described as adopted.

### Q2 — IMO Noise Code · **STABLE / LOW**
`MSC.337(91)`, adopted 30 Nov 2012; mandatory via SOLAS `II-1/3-12` (`MSC.338(91)`) from **1 July 2014**.
The Code is **unamended** at this sitting. No 2024 movement. The only date discipline needed is not to
describe it as applying to existing ships.

### Q3 — Paris MoU New Inspection Regime · **CONFIRMED / MEDIUM**
NIR in force **1 January 2011** and structurally unchanged at this sitting. Two things *are*
sitting-relative and must be stated at the December 2024 value: the operative performance lists are
the **2023 lists, applied from 1 July 2024**; and the **Russian Federation is suspended** from the
Paris MoU. The revised Ship Risk Profile aligned to the recast EU PSC Directive is a **future**
instrument (Directive applies 2027) and must not be described as operative.

### Q4 — Classification societies and surveys · **CONFIRMED / MEDIUM after correction**
See §3.1. HSSC edition is `A.1186(33)`. `A.1207(34)` must appear **nowhere** except as an express
warning. Risk raised from the intake's LOW because the frozen donor pointer is contaminated.

### Q5 — Root cause analysis, active and latent failures · **STABLE / LOW**
Reason's organisational accident model and the proximate/root distinction are not date-sensitive.
The regulatory hook — the **Casualty Investigation Code**, `MSC.255(84)`, mandatory from 1 Jan 2010 —
is unmoved. Nothing in 2024 touches this question.

### Q6 — MLC 2006 structure and the DMLC · **CONFIRMED / HIGH**
The single highest-risk question on the paper, and the intake rated it LOW. See §1.4. Amendment
chain to be stated at the December 2024 position: 2014 amendments in force **18 Jan 2017** (A5-I
becomes **16** matters); 2016 amendments in force 8 Jan 2019; 2018 amendments in force 26 Dec 2020;
**2022 amendments in force 23 December 2024 — at this sitting**.

### Q7 — Carrier responsibility and defences · **CONFIRMED / LOW**
Hague-Visby, Hamburg and Rotterdam Rules are unmoved. **The Rotterdam Rules are NOT in force** at this
sitting and must be described as such. Indian statutes: **COGSA 1925** (as amended 1993) and the
**Bills of Lading Act 1856**; their 2025 replacements must not appear.

### Q8 — SEEMP four-step cycle · **CONFIRMED / HIGH**
See §1.3. Operative guidelines are **`MEPC.395(82)`, adopted 4 October 2024**, revoking `MEPC.346(78)`.
SEEMP sits at **MARPOL Annex VI regulation 26** — *not* regulation 22, which is the pre-2021

1 Jan 2023, so at this sitting **2023 is the first full CII data year and the first ratings have been
issued** — a December 2024 statement that a January 2024 answer could not make.

### Q9 — III Code · **CONFIRMED / LOW**
