### 3.1 Q2 — the ammonia boundary, and how November differs from April

The April 2024 donor (`QP2404-Q2`) establishes the load-bearing proposition as an **absence**:
no IMO instrument governed ammonia as a marine fuel; approval ran through **SOLAS II-1/55
alternative design and arrangements** with class rules carrying the detail.

**That absence still holds in November 2024, and it is established by the same dated evidence:**

- The **Interim guidelines for the safety of ships using ammonia as fuel** were **approved at
  MSC 109, 2–6 December 2024** and **issued 26 February 2025**. Both dates are **after** this
  sitting.

But the window **April → November 2024** is not empty, and the answer is sharpened rather than
copied. At November the IMO work programme has moved further along than in April: the
sub-committee stage of the draft interim guidelines had run its course during 2024 and the
instrument was **awaiting approval by the Maritime Safety Committee**, which had not yet
happened. The November answer therefore says *"finalised at sub-committee level and awaiting
Committee approval"* where the April answer said *"principles agreed, guidelines not finalised"* —
a genuine re-anchoring, and one that still lands on the same verdict: **nothing in force, nothing
approved, SOLAS II-1/55 is the route.**

The precise September-2024 sub-committee session identity is **not** asserted, because no held
source establishes it. The *absence* is what the answer depends on, and that is established from
the MSC 109 approval date. Recorded as a `B_CURRENCY_CHECK`.
