**Corrections to the PROD01 / Stage B text**

- The lubricator is described as mounted on each cylinder's hydraulic cylinder unit and actuated by the engine's
  300 bar hydraulic (system) oil. This replaces "common-rail servo-oil-driven" and "driven electrically".
- **The accumulator is removed from the ME-engine layout.** The maker's ME arrangement (project guide Fig.
  9.02.02c) shows none. Lubricator accumulators are documented for MC/MC-C Alpha lubricators only (SL2016-632), and
  this is now stated in the Study Guide. This supersedes the Stage B placement-neutral wording in section 8, and
  resolves the Stage B `unresolved` item.
- The supply side follows project guide Fig. 9.01.01: two BN grades, the ACOS2 valve, a filter, a heating tank and a
  heat-traced line. This replaces the generic "heated service tank with low-level cut-out".
- Injection is intermittent "for instance every 2, 4 or 8 engine revolutions" and is not tied to low load. Below
  the part-load breakpoint the feed is proportional to engine speed (SL2023-737 App. I).
- **The feed-rate factor (formerly ACC) applies only to high-sulphur fuel.** On low-sulphur fuel and gas, the
  minimum feed-rate setting governs (SL2023-737, June 2023). The PROD01 text presented ACC x S% as the general rule,
  from SL2013-571, which is now superseded.
- The WinGD changeover unit is named **iCAT** (Integrated Cylinder lubricant Auto Transfer). It is optional, per the
  WinGD X72DF-1.1 MIM and X82DF OM. This resolves the earlier `unresolved` item.
- ACC is given with both of the maker's expansions (Adaptive / Adaptable Cylinder oil Control).

**Additions**

- ACOS2 is added as the standard switching system, and ACOM Mk III as the optional mixing system. The Mk III
  designation is printed on project guide Fig. 9.01.02.
- The dual-fuel oil strategy for the named engine is added.
- A dated table of current maker guidance is added to the Study Guide.
- The WinGD Pulse Jet is added as a separate-maker alternative.
- Six original diagrams, D1-D6, are hosted beside the page under `figures/`. D1 replaces the Stage B sketch under the
