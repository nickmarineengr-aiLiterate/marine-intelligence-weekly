## 8. QP2609-RELEASE STAGE B CORRECTION (22 September 2026)

**PROD01 was wrong on the Rules.** The Merchant Shipping (Marine Incident and Emergency Response) Rules, 2026 were
**notified** as G.S.R. 354(E), Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II s.3(i) No.320, dated 12 May 2026, made under
s.230 read with s.228(4) of the 2025 Act, and are in force from publication (r.1(2)). Read in full in the Gazette text
(eGazette 272543.pdf, sha256 1a0bb49288f21fc53cd6e6514331088a06017cb77624ad86e99ba1949e90ade5; the DGMA listing copy is
byte-identical). The notified Rules contain six rules: definitions (r.2), duties and powers of the nodal authority incl.
directions, intervention and the Director-General's coordinating role (r.3), response where the primary response party
delays or cannot act (r.4), cost recovery from the ship owner (r.5), records and audit (r.6). They do **not** contain the
draft's 24-hour reporting rule or its 'competent authority' definition, and do not themselves appoint a nodal authority
(r.2(1)(f) refers to one appointed under s.225). All five learner modes were corrected. The s.225 appointment remains
unresolved: no notification located. Residual: e-Gazette upload/signature dated 14 May 2026.
