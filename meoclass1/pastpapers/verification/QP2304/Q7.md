## 5. Verification decisions taken

1. **Define first, compare second, in every limb.** The examiner's command says so and the marks
   follow it.
2. **Give limb (c) two sections.** It prints **two** demands — *liability of Ship owner* **and**
   *Limitations of Liability* — where the other limbs print one, and the limitation half is where
   the structural difference lies. Giving it a single section would have buried the best point in
   the question.
3. **Make the absence of a Bunker limit the headline of that section.** *"The Bunker Convention sets
   NO figure at all"* — article 6 merely preserves limitation under the applicable regime. Three
   consequences follow and are stated: the limit is tonnage-based under a **general** convention;
   the bunker claim **shares one fund** with every other claim from the incident; and there is **no
   second tier**. A candidate who quotes "the Bunker Convention limit" has missed the entire point.
4. **State the channelling difference in one reversible-proof line.** *CLC funnels liability in;
   Bunkers spreads it out.* This is the difference most often stated backwards.
5. **Draw out the asymmetry inside the Bunker Convention.** Liability under article 3 is **wide** —
   owner, bareboat charterer, manager, operator, jointly and severally — but the **duty to insure
   under article 7 falls on the registered owner alone**. It reads as an inconsistency and is
   deliberate, and noticing it is a discriminating point.
6. **Keep exclusions and defences apart.** Limb (a) asks for **exclusions** — what the Convention
   does not apply to. The shipowner's three **defences** are answers to a claim the Convention does
   apply to, and belong in limb (c). Merging them is common and loses marks in both limbs.
7. **Answer limb (b) by saying the limits are identical.** That *is* the answer, and it should be
   stated plainly rather than padded. The HNS contrast is added to show breadth.
8. **Reproduce no LLMC table and no currency conversion.** The Convention has no limit of its own,
   the comparison is structural, and SDR rates move daily with no held source fixing one at this
   sitting.
9. **Assert no party status and no count of States party** for either convention. Both are status
   facts that date and no printed limb requires either.
