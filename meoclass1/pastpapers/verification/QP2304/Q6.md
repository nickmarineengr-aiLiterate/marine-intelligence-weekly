## 5. Verification decisions taken

1. **Discard the donor's opening rather than adapt it.** Adapting a paragraph that describes a
   mature regime into one describing a three-month-old regime invites exactly the residue that a
   sweep is meant to catch. It was rewritten from the corpus position.
2. **Attach every device to a named loss.** The opening names the loss mechanisms — axial,
   rotational, frictional and sectional, tip and hub vortex, and non-uniform inflow — so each limb
   can be presented as attacking one of them. A candidate who knows the losses can answer any
   version of this question, including ones naming devices he has never met.
3. **Show that the four limbs ARE the three efficiency terms.** Quasi-propulsive efficiency is the
   product of open-water, hull and relative-rotative efficiency; limb (d) improves the first,
   limb (a) the second, and limbs (b) and (c) the third. Presented as a small table. This turns a
   catalogue into a system and is the discriminating structural move of the answer.
4. **Use the same four-part shape in every limb** — problem, principle, forms, gain and limits — so
   the four limbs are visibly equal and the marker can find each element.
5. **Acknowledge that the categories are not watertight.** A duct with internal stator fins belongs
   to both limb (a) and limb (b). Saying so shows understanding; forcing it into one limb does not.
6. **Give no single precise efficiency percentage.** Every figure is an **indicative range with its
   condition attached** — full-form or fine, laden or ballast, design speed or slow steaming.
   Published gains are obtained on one hull at one draught and speed and do not transfer. This is
   the commonest way the subject is answered badly and the restraint is stated in the study notes as
   well as observed in the answer.
7. **Name no proprietary device or manufacturer.** Generic type and principle earn the marks;
   product names date and invite error.
8. **State no CII band, reduction factor or EEXI reference-line figure.** The question does not
   require them, and at a sitting where no ship had been rated a rating discussion would be
   premature as well as unnecessary.
9. **Close on the cautions that stop the answer reading as a brochure** — matching by CFD and model
   testing, the moved operating profile under slow steaming, non-additive interaction, hull and
   propeller husbandry first, verification against a measured baseline on the ISO 19030 approach,
   and the class and structural consequences of a welded appendage in cavitating flow.
