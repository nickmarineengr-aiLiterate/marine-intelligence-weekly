## 2. The one hard instrument — obtained and read

This question sits **substantially outside the regulatory corpus**, exactly as the plan predicted.
It nonetheless has **one dated, mandatory, citable instrument**, and it was read rather than
summarised.

**PRIMARY: resolution FAL.14(46)**, *Amendments to the Annex to the Convention on Facilitation of
International Maritime Traffic, 1965*, **adopted 13 May 2022**, obtained from the IMO resolutions
CDN (50 pages).

| Element | Verbatim / verified |
|---|---|
| Entry into force | Operative ¶2 determines entry into force **1 January 2024** under article VII(2)(b) tacit acceptance, unless one third of Contracting Governments objected before 1 October 2023 |
| The obligation | **Standard 1.3quin** — *"Public authorities shall introduce arrangements to enable the provision of all the information required in accordance with Standard 2.1 to a 'single window' by electronic means."* |
| The definition | *"Single window. An environment that allows for the submission or provision of standardized and harmonized information and declarations to a single entry point, typically by electronic means."* |
| The cap | **Standard 2.1bis** — public authorities *"shall not require more than the information shown in appendix 1"* |

**In the FAL Convention a "Standard" is mandatory and a "Recommended Practice" is not.** The
Maritime Single Window obligation was therefore **live two months before this sitting**, and it is
the single fact that lifts this answer above a general technology essay.

## 4. Temporal review — **CONFIRMED, MODERATE**

The intake recorded `STABLE / LOW`. Checked rather than trusted. The **topic** is stable; the
**instrument** was not, and the plan correctly flagged this as a research instruction rather than a
flag correction.

- **FAL.14(46) took effect 1 January 2024 — two months before this sitting.** The Model Answer
  therefore uses the Single Window obligation **in the present tense**.
- This is the **mirror image of the usual temporal error**. The normal risk is importing later law;
  here the risk is writing on pre-2024 material and describing the Single Window as *forthcoming*,
  which would be wrong at this sitting.
- **MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3/Rev.2** is the operative cyber edition, carried from the Q5 record. **Rev.3 of
  4 April 2025 must not be used here** and does not appear.
- For **QP2510**: the Single Window obligation is equally in force and needs no change; the cyber
  cross-reference must move to **Rev.3**.

## 5. Limitations accepted — and one deliberate omission

1. **No percentage figures appear anywhere in the answer, for any benefit.** Published claims for
   fuel or emissions savings from analytics vary by an order of magnitude, are specific to hull,
   trade and baseline, and originate largely from vendors. **This is a deliberate decision, recorded
   so that a later editor does not "improve" the answer by adding them.** `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION`.
2. **MARPOL Annex VI was not read** in a licensed consolidated edition for this question. The
   efficiency-rating framing at route step 3 is therefore expressed **with no regulation number and
   no date**. `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION`.
3. **Entry into force is stated from the resolution's own tacit-acceptance mechanism**, which states
   the condition rather than the outcome. Confirm from IMO status information before publication.
   `B_CURRENCY_CHECK`.
4. No claim is made about the number of ships fitted with performance monitoring, the size of the
   maritime analytics market, or any commercial platform.
