## 5. Temporal review — **CORRECTED, HIGH**

See §3 for the proof. In summary:

- **Intake said `STABLE / LOW`. It is wrong.**
- Operative at March 2024: the Convention **as amended in 2014, 2016 and 2018**.
- The **2022 amendments were not in force** and appear nowhere in the Model Answer.
- Six specific 2022 provisions were identified and excluded by direct textual comparison, not by
  recollection.
- Nothing later than the sitting appears in the Model Answer. The 2022 position is described in the
  **Study Guide only**, expressly labelled as later law and as the exclusion list.

## 6. Limitations accepted

1. **Entry-into-force dates.** The consolidated text records the Conference sessions at which
   amendments were *approved*, not the dates they took *effect*. The dates used in the Study Guide
   framing come from the corpus temporal record. **Nothing in the answer depends on them**: the
   material fact — which consolidation is operative at March 2024 — is established from the texts
   themselves. `B_CURRENCY_CHECK`.
2. **Ratification count** at the sitting date was not established and is **not asserted anywhere**.
3. **COVID-19 crew change crisis** is used qualitatively in limb (b). **No figure is given**,
   because no primary source for one was consulted. `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION`.
4. **"Recognized organizations are paid by the shipowner they inspect"** is a statement of
   commercial reality, not of a rule — Reg 5.1.2 authorises delegation and says nothing about
   payment. It is offered as evaluation, which is what limb (b) asks for, and flagged as judgement.
   `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION`.
