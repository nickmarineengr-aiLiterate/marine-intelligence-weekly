## 3. "Open the source" — three errors caught in one summary

An automated summary of **this same resolution** was consulted during research and was checked
line by line against the text. It was **wrong on three counts**, all of them in limb (a), which
is the limb the examiner is counting:

| Summary asserted | The resolution actually says |
|---|---|
| "Ballast water management records where applicable" | Not present. The BWM Record Book is a BWM Convention document, not MARPOL, and annex 2.1 does not list it. |
| "Engine maintenance and fuel consumption logs" | Not present. What is listed is the **Record Book of Engine Parameters** (NOx Technical Code 6.2.2.7) and the **Record of Fuel Oil Changeover** (Annex VI 14.6) — different documents. |
| "Waste Reception Facilities Record documentation" | Not present as a covered record book. Reception-facility **receipts** appear at annex 4.3.11, as attachments to an entry — a different thing entirely. |
| (omitted) | **Cargo Record Book** (Annex II 15.1), **Ozone-depleting Substances Record Book** (Annex VI 12.6), and **recording of tier and on/off status of marine diesel engines** (Annex VI 13.5.3) were all missing. |

**Eighth consecutive paper on which reading the instrument caught something a summary got wrong.**
A candidate answering limb (a) from that summary would have scored close to zero on it.

## 5. Temporal review — **STABLE, and confirmed rather than assumed**

Intake recorded `STABLE / LOW`. **That is correct here**, and it was re-established rather than
trusted, because the QP2508-Q3 precedent and this paper's own Q5 both show the flag can be wrong.

- MEPC.312(74) was adopted **17 May 2019** and **has not been revised**.
- The enabling MARPOL amendments entered into force **1 October 2020** — three and a half years
  before this sitting. The whole subject matter was fully operative in March 2024.
- **One moving part checked and found not load-bearing:** the resolution cross-refers to the
  *Procedures for port State control* (A.1119(30), as amended). The Procedures are re-adopted
  periodically. The answer reproduces the resolution's own reference rather than restating a
  current edition, so no claim is exposed.
- **One residual recorded as B_CURRENCY_CHECK:** MARPOL **Annex VI was revised by MEPC.328(76)**
  after MEPC.312(74) was adopted. The Annex VI regulation numbers in limb (a) — 12.6, 13.5.3,
  14.6 — are stated **exactly as MEPC.312(74) prints them**, which is the correct thing to do for
  a question that says "as laid down in resolution MEPC.312(74)", but they should be confirmed
  against a consolidated Annex VI before publication.
