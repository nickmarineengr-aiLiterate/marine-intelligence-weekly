### The two later printings were read and deliberately rejected as donor

`QP2404-Q5` (April 2024) and `QP2409-Q8` (September 2024) print the same limb. `QP2404-Q5`'s own
temporal record describes cybutryne as having been in force *"fifteen months before this sitting"*.
**At June 2023 it is five.** Taking either as donor would have imported a market maturity that did
not yet exist.

### The governing delta is the MARKS, not the wording

In the donor this material is **one limb worth roughly eight marks**, alongside a limb a) on the
Convention and on the documents and records demonstrating compliance. **This paper prints only the
coating limb, and gives it all sixteen marks.**

Consequently: the Convention is compressed to **framing** (four lines), the four types are treated
at roughly **double the donor's depth**, and a **comparison section** is added that the eight-mark
donor limb has no room for. **Reproducing the January answer would spend half a sixteen-mark
question on material the examiner did not ask for**, and that risk is recorded because an
exact-recurrence relation actively invites it.

## 4. TEMPORAL VERIFICATION — JUNE 2023

**Result: CONFIRMED · risk MEDIUM.**

1. **The risk runs backwards.** The danger is omitting the cybutryne controls, in force since
   **1 January 2023**, not importing something later.
2. **The elapsed interval was recalculated for this sitting and not inherited.** January's donor
   correctly says *days*; April 2024 correctly says *fifteen months*. **Neither is true here — about
   five months is.** The description of market maturity differs accordingly: the first removal and
   sealer-coat decisions have now been taken in practice, which was not so in January.
3. **The boundary is day-independent** — controls took effect 1 January and the sitting is June, so
   no reasoning about the examination day is needed. (The January printing had to reason about it
   explicitly; this one does not.)
4. **Guidelines edition checked** — MEPC.358(78), adopted June 2022.
5. **Deliberately absent, all future:** MEPC 80 (**3–7 July 2023 — one month after this sitting**)
   and the 2023 IMO GHG Strategy; the 33rd Assembly (6 December 2023); the Hong Kong Convention, not
   in force, and so not mentioned in connection with coating removal.
6. **The energy-efficiency link was re-anchored.** The operational carbon-intensity regime entered
   force 1 January 2023, so at this sitting it is in its **first data year and no ship has yet been
   rated**. The answer states the mechanism and claims **no rating experience and no distribution**.
7. **Merchant Shipping Act, 1958** governs the paper; not engaged here.
