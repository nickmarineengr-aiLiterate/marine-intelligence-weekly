## 4. TSCR-3 IS ENGAGED BY THIS QUESTION AND IS DECLINED

**This is the most important True Source finding on the paper.**

The corpus amendment register at
`true-source/03-imo-instruments/MARPOL-Annex-VI/amendment-register.json` records:

```json
{"resolution": "MEPC.328(76)", "adopted": "2021-06-17", "entryIntoForce": "2023-11-01"}
```

**That date is wrong. The correct date is 1 November 2022 — and this paper sits inside the window
the error corrupts.**

Consuming `2023-11-01` would place a June 2023 sitting **before** the revised Annex VI entered into
force. The answer would then have to say that the SEEMP is required by **regulation 22**, that
chapter 4 had not been renumbered, and that **EEXI and CII were not yet applicable** — three wrong
statements, one of which **inverts the substance of this very question**.

**The corpus figure is declined**, and it is refuted **from inside the corpus by primary source**.
The preamble of MEPC.346(78), read directly this session, states that MEPC.328(76) — the 2021
revised MARPOL Annex VI — *"will enter into force on 1 November 2022"*.

**No corpus file was touched. TSCR-3 remains OPEN and is re-reported, not repaired.**

**TSCR-4 is not engaged** — it concerns `MEPC.376(80)`, a July 2023 instrument, future at this
sitting.
