## 5. THE BOUNDARY BETWEEN CODE AND PRACTICE

**The ISM Code requires that procedures exist; it does not prescribe their content.** Section 8 says
nothing whatever about media handling, next-of-kin contact or the specification of communication
equipment. The four measure sets are therefore **engineering and management judgement expressed as
good practice consistent with the Code**, and the answer **states that distinction openly** rather
than dressing practice as regulation.
