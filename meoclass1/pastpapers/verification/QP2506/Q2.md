## Reasoning recorded

- **The two named terms get their own heading.** The examiner numbers them (i) and (ii); defined in
  passing inside a narrative they are not credited.
- **They are contrasted, not merely defined** - accession is one step, signature-subject-to-
  ratification is two, and both reach the same destination.
- **Convention entry into force and amendment entry into force are kept separate.** Threshold plus
  waiting period for the first; tacit acceptance for the second.
- **The answer ends on domestic effect.** International entry into force does not bind the ship; the
  flag State's implementing legislation does - in India at this sitting, the Merchant Shipping Act
  1958 and the rules under it. This is what ties the question to **Q9**.
