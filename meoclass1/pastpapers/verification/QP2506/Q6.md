## Reasoning recorded

- **Limb (b) is a Rule VII question and Rule VII is unusually explicit**, which is why the answer
  rests on the rule text rather than on case law. The conditions are cumulative: **ashore**, **in a
  position of peril**, an **actual intention to refloat**, for the **common safety**, and **at the
  risk of such damage**.
- **The corollary is where the marks are lost.** Damage to machinery worked hard on a ship that is
  *afloat* is not allowable - it is ordinary use of the ship, however hard, and therefore not
  extraordinary. Limb (a)'s "essential features" and limb (b)'s answer are the same test applied
  twice, which is why the two limbs must be written as one argument.
