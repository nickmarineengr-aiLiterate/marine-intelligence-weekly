Limb (b) is the engineer's limb: what the guidance says, what can actually be done to a ship, and what the Chief Engineer's part in it is. **Freshness risk.** MEDIUM - the science is stable but the instrument moved before the sitting and is expected to move again after it. The perishable content is the EDITION: the stem names the 2014 circular and the revised circular governs at the sitting, so the answer must name both and confuse neither. The second perishable element is the status of the Experience Building Phase, which is running at this sitting and about which nothing may be asserted.

**Jurisdiction risk.** LOW. The subject is international and entirely recommendatory; no Indian statute is engaged and none is cited. Some coastal States and ports operate voluntary incentive schemes, which are mentioned as a category rather than named, since none is held.

**Technical ambiguities adjudicated.**

- Whether URN is regulated - it is not; the regime is wholly recommendatory at this sitting
- Which source dominates - propeller cavitation above inception speed, machinery-borne noise below it, so the answer depends on the operating condition and must say so
- Whether noise is 'pollution' in law - it is capable of being so, because UNCLOS defines pollution by reference to the introduction of ENERGY
- Whether quieting conflicts with efficiency - largely it does not; most measures reduce both fuel and noise, and that overlap is the practical argument
- Whether the effects on marine life are a single phenomenon - they are not; masking, behavioural change, physiological stress and injury are distinct mechanisms with different thresholds
- Whether a Chief Engineer can influence design measures - generally not on an existing ship, which is why the answer separates design from operational and maintenance measures
- Whether hull fouling increases noise - it does, through added roughness and disturbed inflow to the propeller, which is also why it is a maintenance rather than a design measure

**Target answer shape.** Six numbered sections under two limb headings, the first limb heading reproducing the printed anomaly by carrying no letter of its own. Limb (a): concept and measurement, then sources as a table because three sources with different speed dependence is exactly what a table renders, then effects as four named mechanisms. Limb (b): the framework with the recommendatory status stated first and hard, then design measures, then operational and maintenance measures closing on the Chief Engineer's role expressed through the SEEMP and the SMS.
