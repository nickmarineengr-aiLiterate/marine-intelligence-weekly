**Technical ambiguities adjudicated.**

- Whether vetting is mandatory - it is not; it is voluntary in law and enforced by the market
- Whether OCIMF approves or rejects ships - it does neither; it operates an information programme and each recipient member applies its own criteria
- Whether a vetting inspection is a survey - it is not; nothing is certified and no statutory consequence follows

- Whether the human-factors tool only records failures - it does not; it can record positive observations where performance exceeds expectation
- Whether data analytics is neutral - it is not; it inherits the quality of its inputs and risk-based targeting creates blind spots wherever the model treats something as low risk
- Whether remote inspection replaces attendance - it does not; a tablet does not open a crankcase
