## 5. SOURCES CONSULTED

- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: MLC 2006, Regulation 4.5 (Social security), from the ILO Compendium held in the MIW True Source corpus. Purpose: to ensure that measures are taken with a view to providing seafarers with access to social security protection. Paragraph 1 - each Member shall ensure that all seafarers and, to the extent provided for in its national law, their dependants have access to social security protection in accordance with the Code. Paragraph 2 - each Member undertakes to take steps, according to its national circumstances, individually and through international cooperation, to achieve progressively comprehensive social security protection. Paragraph 3 - seafarers subject to its social security legislation, and their dependants to the extent provided in national law, are entitled to benefit from protection no less favourable than that enjoyed by shoreworkers.
