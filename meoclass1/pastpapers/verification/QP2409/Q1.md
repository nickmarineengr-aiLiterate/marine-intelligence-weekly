## 5. SOURCES CONSULTED

- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: STCW Convention, regulation VIII/1 (Fitness for duty), STCW Consolidated Edition 2017, MIW True Source corpus. Paragraph 1 requires each Administration, for the purpose of preventing fatigue, to (.1) establish and enforce rest periods for watchkeeping personnel and those whose duties involve designated safety, security and prevention of pollution duties in accordance with section A-VIII/1, and (.2) require that watch systems are so arranged that the efficiency of all watchkeeping personnel is not impaired by fatigue and that duties are so organized that the first watch at the commencement of a voyage and subsequent relieving watches are sufficiently rested and otherwise fit for duty. Paragraph 2 requires adequate measures against drug and alcohol abuse in accordance with section A-VIII/1, taking into account the guidance in section B-VIII/1.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: STCW Code section A-VIII/1, paragraphs 1 to 3. Administrations shall take account of the danger posed by fatigue. Persons assigned duty as officer in charge of a watch or as a rating forming part of a watch, and those whose duties involve designated safety, prevention of pollution and security duties, shall be provided with a rest period of not less than 10 hours in any 24-hour period and 77 hours in any 7-day period. The hours of rest may be divided into no more than two periods, one of which shall be at least 6 hours, and the interval between consecutive periods of rest shall not exceed 14 hours.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: STCW Code section A-VIII/1, paragraph 4. The rest requirements in paragraphs 2 and 3 need not be maintained in the case of an emergency or in other overriding operational conditions. Musters, fire-fighting and lifeboat drills, and drills prescribed by national laws and regulations and by international instruments, shall be conducted in a manner that minimizes the disturbance of rest periods and does not induce fatigue.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: STCW Code section A-VIII/1, paragraphs 5 to 7. Watch schedules shall be posted where they are easily accessible, established in a standardized format in the working language or languages of the ship and in English. A seafarer on call, such as when a machinery space is unattended, shall have an adequate compensatory rest period if the normal period of rest is disturbed by call-outs to work. Records of daily hours of rest shall be maintained in a standardized format, in the working language or languages of the ship and in English, to allow monitoring and verification; seafarers shall receive a copy of the records pertaining to them, endorsed by the master or a person authorized by the master and by the seafarers.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: STCW Code section A-VIII/1, paragraph 8 - the master's authority. Nothing in the section impairs the right of the master to require a seafarer to perform any hours of work necessary for the immediate safety of the ship, persons on board or cargo, or for the purpose of giving assistance to other ships or persons in distress at sea. The master may suspend the schedule of hours of rest and require a seafarer to perform any hours of work necessary until the normal situation has been restored; as soon as practicable after the normal situation has been restored, the master shall ensure that any seafarers who have performed work in a scheduled rest period are provided with an adequate period of rest.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: STCW Code section A-VIII/1, paragraph 9 - the exceptions regime. Parties may allow exceptions from the required hours of rest in paragraphs 2.2 and 3 provided that the rest period is not less than 70 hours in any 7-day period. Exceptions from the weekly rest period shall not be allowed for more than two consecutive weeks, and the intervals between two periods of exceptions on board shall not be less than twice the duration of the exception. The hours of rest in paragraph 2.1 may be divided into no more than three periods, one of which shall be at least 6 hours and neither of the other two less than one hour, with intervals between consecutive periods of rest not exceeding 14 hours, and exceptions shall not extend beyond two 24-hour periods in any 7-day period. Exceptions shall as far as possible take into account the guidance on prevention of fatigue in section B-VIII/1.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: STCW Code section A-VIII/1, paragraph 10 - the alcohol limit. Each Administration shall establish, for the purpose of preventing alcohol abuse, a limit of not greater than 0.05% blood alcohol level (BAC) or 0.25 mg/l alcohol in the breath, or a quantity of alcohol leading to such alcohol concentration, for masters, officers and other seafarers while performing designated safety, security and marine environmental duties.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED - READ AT SOURCE: STCW Convention, regulation VIII/2, which places the watchkeeping arrangements alongside fitness for duty in the same chapter, and requires the officer in charge of an engineering watch, under the direction of the chief engineer officer, to be immediately available and on call to attend the machinery spaces and, when required, to be physically present in the machinery space during periods of responsibility.
