## 5. SOURCES CONSULTED

- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY: IMO GreenVoyage2050 technology material on propulsion-improving devices - the taxonomy of devices that recover the axial and rotational losses inherent in propeller operation, and their division into pre-swirl, post-swirl and rudder-region devices.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY: published naval-architecture literature on the Grim vane wheel - patented by Otto Grim in 1966 as the "Leitrad"; a freely rotating wheel mounted aft of and coaxial with the propeller, of larger diameter, whose inner portion acts as a turbine extracting energy from the propeller slipstream and whose outer portion, standing outside the race, acts as a propeller converting that energy into additional thrust, with no mechanical connection to the shaft.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY: computational-fluid-dynamics studies of rudder bulbs and rudder thrust fins, establishing that propulsive gain is sensitive to bulb diameter and to fin span, chord and angle of attack, and that model-scale results do not transfer to full scale without a scale-effect correction.
- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT: the quoted gain ranges, the off-design and interaction cautions, and the acquisition reasoning in step 6. These are stated as engineering judgement and as ranges, not as verified figures for any hull.
