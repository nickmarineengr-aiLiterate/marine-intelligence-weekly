**Technical ambiguities identified before drafting:**

- 'Verification and validation' are used loosely in general engineering. Within GBS they have distinct roles - verification of the Tier IV rules against Tiers I and II at IMO, and validation of the individual ship through plan approval, survey and certification. The answer separates them explicitly and says which is which.
- The stem asks how GBS ensures 'environmental protection', which invites a drift into MARPOL. It does not mean that: environmental protection enters through the Tier I goal wording and through the structural-integrity and recycling functional requirements. The answer stays inside GBS.
