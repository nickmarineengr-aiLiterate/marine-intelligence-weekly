## 5. SOURCES CONSULTED

- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY - the International Convention on the Establishment of an International Fund for Compensation for Oil Pollution Damage, 1992 (the 1992 Fund Convention), establishing the 1992 Fund as the second tier above CLC 92. Not held in the MIW corpus; not read at source.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY - the Protocol of 2003 to the 1992 Fund Convention, establishing the Supplementary Fund as an optional third tier, in force since 2005. Not held; not read at source.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY - the funding mechanism: contributions are levied on any person who received in a calendar year more than 150,000 tonnes of contributing oil - crude oil and heavy fuel oil - carried by sea and received in ports or terminal installations in a State Party. Contributions are paid by oil receivers, not by governments and not by shipowners; States report oil receipts; the Assembly levies annual contributions according to need. For the Supplementary Fund, a minimum aggregate quantity is deemed to have been received in each Member State.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY - the compensation ceilings: the total payable by the 1992 Fund for any one incident, including the sum actually paid under CLC 92, is 203 million SDR; where three States Parties collectively received 600 million tonnes or more of contributing oil in the relevant year, that figure rises to 300,740,000 SDR. The total available under the Supplementary Fund, including the amounts payable under CLC 92 and the 1992 Fund, is 750 million SDR for any one incident. These figures are amendable by tacit acceptance and are flagged for currency check.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY - the admissibility criteria applied by the Funds, as set out in the IOPC Funds Claims Manual: an expense or loss must have been actually incurred; must relate to measures that are reasonable and justifiable; must be quantifiable and supported by documentary evidence; and must show a reasonable degree of proximity - a sufficiently close link of causation - between the contamination and the loss. Compensation for impairment of the environment other than loss of profit is limited to the costs of reasonable measures of reinstatement actually undertaken or to be undertaken. The Claims Manual is not held in the MIW corpus.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY - the scope boundary: the regime applies to persistent oil carried as cargo in bulk by a tanker, and to pollution damage in the territory, territorial sea and exclusive economic zone of a State Party, with preventive measures wherever taken. Non-persistent oil, bunkers spilled from non-tankers and hazardous and noxious substances fall outside it.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY - the 1971 Fund and its predecessor Convention ceased to operate and the 1971 Fund was wound up, well before this sitting. Only the 1992 Fund and the Supplementary Fund exist at the date of this paper.
- P3 INDUSTRY GUIDANCE - the voluntary industry agreements STOPIA 2006 and TOPIA 2006, under which tanker owners' P&I interests indemnify the 1992 Fund in respect of small tankers and indemnify the Supplementary Fund for a share of its payments. Named as the mechanism by which the burden is rebalanced between shipowners and oil receivers; no percentage or figure is asserted beyond what is stated in the answer.

That record read CLC 92 in full.
