## 4. Evidence gaps — recorded, not filled

⭐ **The evidence position on this question is the strongest on the paper, and the one gap is
precisely where limb (b)'s content lives.**

**What was READ — P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED.** The corpus holds the **official India Code PDF of Act
22 of 2017** and it was read directly this session. Sections **2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13,
14 and 17** come from the page: the nine named High Courts and the notification clause; the
territorial-waters limit and its extension proviso; the definition of *vessel*; the maritime
claims list including the *mutual insurance calls* limb; arrest in rem and the sister-ship
proviso; the **five maritime liens in their statutory order**; the **one-year and two-year**
extinction periods; the priority order in s.10; the wrongful-arrest security and the
**forty-five-day** auction provision; the exact two-subsection structure of **s.13**; and the
repeals.

**What was NOT read — and it is the delegated legislation.**

1. ⚠ **The rules made under s.16, prescribing the assessors' qualifications, were not read** and
   are not held. **No qualification list is asserted as statutory anywhere in this answer.** The
   professional categories given in §9 of the answer are stated expressly as what the statutory
   standard *contemplates*, never as what any rule prescribes. Flagged `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION`.
2. **Corpus open item RQ-34** — post-2017 amendments — carried forward unclosed.
3. **The s.2(1)(e) enumeration** is of historical form and is reproduced as printed.
4. **The Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 is not held.** Its ss.150, 151 and 285 are named **only
   because the 2017 Act names them**, and nothing about their content is asserted.
5. **The definition of *vessel* in s.2(1)(l) is reproduced only so far as it was read**; its
   closing limb is not completed and nothing is attributed to it that was not on the page.

**The finding that carries limb (b):** s.13(1) requires the Central Government to appoint by
notification a list of assessors with *"such qualifications and experience in admiralty and
maritime matters"* … *"in the manner as may be prescribed"*, and s.2(1)(j) defines *prescribed*
as **by rules made by the Central Government**. **The Act creates the mechanism and delegates
the criteria.** An answer that recites a statutory qualification list is describing delegated
legislation the statute does not print.
