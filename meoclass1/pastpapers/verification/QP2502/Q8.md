## 4. Evidence gaps — recorded, not filled

**The evidence position on this question differs sharply from the regulatory questions, and the
difference is stated in the answer.**

**What is engineering fact, and is stated as such.** The decomposition of quasi-propulsive
efficiency into open-water, hull and relative-rotative components; the wake and thrust-deduction
relationship; the classification of losses into axial, rotational, frictional and vortex
components; and the working principle of each device family. **These are not IMO inventions and
do not depend on any instrument.**

**What is flagged.**

1. ⚠ **EVERY QUANTIFIED GAIN IS INDICATIVE.** The percentage ranges come from reported industry
   practice and are strongly dependent on hull form, block coefficient, draught, speed,
   propeller loading and the quality of the baseline. **No figure is asserted as a fixed
   property of any device**, and the answer says so.
2. **NO MAKER'S DOCUMENT IS HELD.** The named devices are commercial products described **by
   working principle only**; no maker's technical publication, model-test report or performance
   guarantee was read, and **no maker's performance claim is adopted**.
3. **No model-test or CFD source is held.** The description of how a gain is demonstrated
   describes accepted practice and rests on no held document.
4. **The speed-power trial correction practice is described by effect**; no standard
   designation, revision or coefficient is asserted.
5. **The remark that free-rotating vane wheels proved structurally troublesome and were not
   widely adopted is engineering judgement about industry adoption**, not a cited finding.

**What is primary-verified.** The regulatory framing is anchored to the corpus's **frozen
canonical records** for regulations 22 and 24, which are **citation-ready**. That is what makes
the innovative-technology point verifiable rather than asserted: **regulation 22.1 makes the
EEDI technical file the basis on which the attained value is verified**, so a credited gain must
be evidenced there.
