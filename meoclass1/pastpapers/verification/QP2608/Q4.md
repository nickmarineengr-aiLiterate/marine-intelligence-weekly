## 2. Statement-type separation

- **Statutory (Indian):** deviation, excuses, warranties, maritime perils, contribution/double
  insurance, general average loss.
- **Statutory (foreign, contrast only):** UK Insurance Act 2015 — expressly excluded from applying.
- **Market wording:** the war risk exclusion and buy-back, listed areas, cancellation and automatic
  termination. Named by identity only; **no clause quoted, no clause number, no edition year**.
- **Contractual:** the charterparty general average clause and any charterers' contribution
  provision.
- **Negative findings, stated in the answer:** the Act contains no War Risk Clause and no Charterers
  Contribution Clause.

## 4. Unresolved

1. Only s.66 of the Marine Insurance Act, 1963 is primary-verified in MIW work; ss.48, 51 and 35–43
   rest on the Act's scheme and on authoritative-secondary reproduction of the bare Act. India Code
   refuses automated retrieval — the fourth independent record of this corpus finding.
2. The war risk market wordings are commercial documents not held by MIW.
3. The Act contains no clause called a "Charterers Contribution Clause" — a **finding**, recorded as
   such and stated in the answer rather than papered over.
