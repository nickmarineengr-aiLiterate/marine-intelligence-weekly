context of maritime law? [8] (b) In what circumstances is cargo considered abandoned, and who

## 2. Statement-type separation

- **Indian statute, primary:** Customs Act 1962 ss.23(2) and 48.
- **Indian statute, primary via internal reuse:** COGSA 2025 and its Schedule, Bills of Lading Act
  2025 — read in the Gazette during `QP2510-Q2`, not re-read this session.
- **International instrument:** the Hague and Visby Protocols, spoken of **through the Indian
  Schedule** because MIW holds no licensed text of the international instruments.
- **Commercial practice:** the Merchant clause, the lien, the carrier's notice-and-sale sequence.
- **Doctrine:** the objective itself, and the three senses of abandonment.
