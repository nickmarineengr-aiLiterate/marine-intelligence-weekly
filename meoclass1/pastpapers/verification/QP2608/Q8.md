# QP2608 — Q8 verification record

**Question (verbatim).** (a) A particular shipping company has been regularly failing PSC inspections
reflecting poor safety management system implementation on the ship. If you have just joined one of
those ships as a Chief Engineer, underline the general procedures followed for flow of information
among ships personnel in the initial meeting, citing few examples for such repeated failures. If the
ship is having multinational crew, state how the process of effective communication to the team can
be approached by you and also how a certain instruction received by you, from shore office for
effective management of shipboard activities, can be best utilized? [10] (b) As Chief Engineer on
board stress the issues you will address for lack of motivation, differences in attitude and to
increase sense of competitiveness for better management and effective control? [6]

**Recurrence:** no host annotation. **Limb (b) is an exact recurrence of the second sentence of
`QP2512-Q4`** (December 2025), which prints it word for word; the same sentence appears at
`QP2103-Q7` and `QP2204-Q4` in the intelligence layer. In every prior instance it is preceded by a
