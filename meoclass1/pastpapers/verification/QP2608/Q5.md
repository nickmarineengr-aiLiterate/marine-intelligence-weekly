# QP2608 — Q5 verification record

**Question (verbatim).** The main propulsion machinery of the vessel in which you are a Chief
Engineer, suddenly fails while the vessel was on full ahead, with the main electrical power, however,
remaining intact. Under this scenario, explain systematically the following: (a) The reasons which
could have led to this failure. [5] (b) The actions which you are required to take if: (i) the
weather is calm; & [5] (ii) the weather is heavy [6]

**Recurrence:** no host annotation, and none in MIW's holdings. `QP2403-Q8` and `QP2510-Q8` are the
nearest solved neighbours — a main engine failing to answer bridge control at dock gates — but that
is a control failure under pilotage in confined water with a different set of demands.
**Reuse tier:** C. **Verified:** 2026-08-17.

## 2. Statement-type separation

- **Convention:** COLREGs not-under-command; SOLAS ch. IV/V urgency and distress; ISM elements 5
  and 8; SOLAS ch. II-1 at concept level.
- **Maker / installation:** the trip and slowdown schedule. The ship's own schedule is made the
  authority; **no universal parameter list is asserted**.
- **Ship-specific equipment:** bow thruster, CPP, shaft generator, sea anchor, emergency towing
  arrangement — each conditioned on the ship being so fitted.
- **Engineering and seamanship judgement:** the cause families, the diagnostic order and the whole
  heavy-weather response.
