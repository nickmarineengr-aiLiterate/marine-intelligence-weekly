# QP2608 — Q2 verification record

**Question (verbatim).** The vessel where you are posted as Chief Engineer, is about to enter a dry
dock. State the coordination and exchange of information necessary with the Master of the vessel for
entering the dock. Also, list the necessary preparations required along with the delegation of
responsibilities to the engineers of the vessel. Enlist the inspections and co-operations you will
make with the dry dock authorities for undocking of the vessel. [16]

## 2. Statement-type separation

- **Convention / Code:** ISM Code elements 5, 7, 8, 10 and SOLAS ch. I reg 11 — the framework only.
- **Class rule:** bottom survey requirement, and approval and attendance for repairs affecting class.
- **Ship's own document:** docking plan and shell expansion.
- **Dock's own rule:** entry conditions, hot work, fire watch, ship/dock division of duties.
- **Operational practice:** the entire docking, in-dock and undocking sequence.
