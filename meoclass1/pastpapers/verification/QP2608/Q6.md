# QP2608 — Q6 verification record

**Question (verbatim).** (a) What is the primary regulatory driver for installing scrubber systems in
modern shipping? [4] (b) A container ship operating on high-sulfur fuel oil (HSFO) paired with an
open-loop scrubber enters a designated zero-discharge port zone. Detail the operational switch the
crew must make, explain the compliance risks of failing to transition, and discuss the economic
trade-off between using compliant low-sulfur fuel (VLSFO) versus running a scrubber system. [12]

## 2. Statement-type separation

- **International instrument, PERMISSIVE:** MARPOL Annex VI regs 4, 14, 14.6, 14.7, 18 and the EGCS
  Guidelines. These permit the equivalent and set the discharge criteria and monitoring.
- **Local / regional, RESTRICTIVE:** the zero-discharge zone itself and the OSPAR regional ban. The
  answer states expressly that these are not MARPOL.
- **In-progress international work:** the PPR/GESAMP process, stated as at this sitting only.
- **Engineering fact:** the open-loop / hybrid distinction and the changeover mechanics.
- **Commercial reasoning:** the whole economic limb, stated as variables.
