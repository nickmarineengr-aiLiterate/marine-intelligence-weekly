## 0. THE FINDING AGAINST THE PRINTED QUESTION

**The question's premise does not meet the regulation it names.** Regulation 28.7 triggers a plan of
corrective actions where a ship is rated **D for THREE consecutive years, or E in any one year**.
The question posits **two** consecutive D ratings, which does not engage regulations 28.7 to 28.9.

This was checked before the answer was framed, against the Organization's own published material on
EEXI and CII, which states: *a ship rated D for three consecutive years, or E for one year, has to
submit a corrective action plan to show how the required index of C or above will be achieved.* It
is consistent with the verified regulation 28 record already carried on five MIW papers — `QP2504-Q6`
records the same trigger and used it to make a sitting-relative argument in April 2025.

**Handling.** Under the standing rule that examiner and source errors are preserved and never
silently normalised, `text_verbatim` records the question exactly as printed. The answer states the
correct trigger, places the ship one year short of it, and **still delivers the plan the examiner
asks for** — as the voluntary plan a prudent Chief Engineer builds after a second D, drafted in the
form regulation 28.8 will require. Nothing is refused and nothing is invented.
