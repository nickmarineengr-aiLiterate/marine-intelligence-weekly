## Sources actually used

- PRIMARY: MSC-MEPC.2/Circ.12/Rev.2, 'Revised Guidelines for Formal Safety Assessment (FSA) for use in the IMO rule-making process', 9 April 2018 - full text read. The title itself establishes that FSA is a tool for the IMO's <i>rule-making</i> process. Figure 1, the flow chart of the FSA methodology, gives the five steps as Step 1 Hazard identification, Step 2 Risk assessment, Step 3 Risk Control Options, Step 4 Cost-Benefit Assessment and Step 5 Decision-making recommendations, with Figure 4 giving Step 2 as Risk Analysis and Step 5 as Recommendations for Decision-Making. Section 10 requires an FSA report to state final recommendations ranked and justified in an auditable and traceable manner, to list the principal hazards, risks, costs and benefits, and to describe the sources, extent and magnitude of significant uncertainties. The Rev.2 amendments added a requirement that incident, near-miss and operational-failure data be reviewed objectively and their reliability, uncertainty and validity assessed and reported.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY: class-society regulatory notice of December 2025 on fire safety requirements for ro-ro, passenger and cargo ships - resolutions <b>MSC.550(108)</b> and <b>MSC.555(108)</b> were adopted at MSC 108 in May 2024 and <b>entered into force on 1 January 2026</b>. MSC.550(108) amends SOLAS chapter II-2 regulations 7 and 20; MSC.555(108) amends FSS Code chapters 7 and 9. Requirements include individually identifiable fixed fire detection in vehicle, special category and ro-ro spaces, combined smoke and heat detectors, alignment of detection sections with deluge sections, fixed detection on weather decks used for vehicles, an effective video monitoring system with at least seven days of replay available at a continuously manned control station, and A-60 insulation of special category and ro-ro space boundaries on passenger ships carrying more than 36 passengers. Application is to ships constructed on or after 1 January 2026, with existing ships in scope to comply not later than the first survey on or after 1 January 2028.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY: the same notice records the regulatory lineage - earlier amendments by resolutions MSC.365(93) and MSC.421(98) to SOLAS II-2/20; the EMSA <b>FIRESAFE I and FIRESAFE II</b> research studies analysing fire dynamics, system performance and operational factors in ro-ro spaces; and <b>MSC.1/Circ.1615</b>, Interim Guidelines for Minimizing the Incidence and Consequences of Fires in Ro-Ro Spaces and Special Category Spaces of New and Existing Ro-Ro Passenger Ships, into which key FIRESAFE outcomes were carried.
- P2 AUTHORITATIVE SECONDARY: IMO work-programme reporting that fire protection, detection and extinction arrangements in vehicle, special category and ro-ro spaces carrying new energy vehicles were prioritised for further amendment, with that further work still in development at the sitting date.
- INTERNAL REUSE VERIFIED: QP2607 Q1(a), which carries the FSA methodology and the same circular, verified in that paper's own verification record.
