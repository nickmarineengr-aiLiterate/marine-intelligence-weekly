## 9. ANSWER ROUTE AS BUILT

Archetype `evaluate`. The principal headings of the model answer ARE this route.

**1. What gender equality means at sea, and what the goal actually says** - the UN 2030 Agenda goal; access
independent of sex and a ship that is also a home; opportunity not outcome; balance is aspiration,
non-discrimination is law.

**2. Why gender balance is required** - limb **a.** - right; workforce; quality of selection; diversity of
perspective in risk management; retention; commercial expectation; better standards for everyone; and the
explicit caution against an unsourced statistic.

**3. What the International Maritime Organization does** - limb **b.** - programme work not regulation; women
in maritime programme; regional associations; fellowships and reserved places; a World Maritime Day theme;
parity written into ordinary programmes, with the GHG-SMART example read at source; and a statement of what is
deliberately not asserted.

**4. What the Government of India does** - limb **b.** - DG Shipping under the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958;
admission to training; certification and service without restriction; institute approval conditions as the
practical lever; training berths; MLC implementation; and again what is not asserted.

**5. The obligations that already bind the ship** - the MLC table: Article III(d), Article IV, Standard A3.1,
Guideline B4.3.1, Regulation 5.1.5 / Standard A5.1.5.

**6. As Chief Engineer - the physical ship** - limb **c.** - cabin and sanitary facilities; PPE in the full
range of sizes; lifting aids and tooling; medical provision and confidentiality; safety equipment sizing
checked in drills.

**7. As Chief Engineer - work, watches and training** - limb **c.** - allocation by rank and competence;
protective allocation named as exclusion; same responsibility and same standard; honest appraisal; deliberate
mentoring; inclusion in departmental decisions; workload visible in the rest-hour records.

**8. As Chief Engineer - behaviour, complaints and accountability** - limb **c.** - the standard set on day
one; the first incident dealt with; the complaint route made real; the complainant protected from
victimization; harassment treated as OSH; SMS reporting so the Company sees the pattern; reliefs briefed;
singling out avoided. Closes on *initiatives recruit, conditions retain*.
