## 2. WHAT THE QUESTION ASKS

Four independent short notes, equally weighted, approaching one subject from four directions: the regulatory
limit, a combustion-side reduction method, an exhaust-side reduction method, and the certification document
that fixes the engine's compliant condition. The examiner is testing breadth across a topic rather than depth
in one part of it.

**Mark allocation.** 16, printed as 4 + 4 + 4 + 4. Equal marks means equal writing time. The standing
temptation is to over-write limb A because it carries numbers and under-write limb D because it feels
administrative; that trade loses marks at both ends.

**Examiner expectation.** For limb A, the tier limits and where each applies, with the construction-date logic
correct. For limbs B and C, the **mechanism** - why the device reduces NOx - and its operating consequences,
not a hardware description. For limb D, the understanding that the file defines the engine's certified
condition and constrains what may be fitted and adjusted.

**Required answer components:** regulation 13's application and exclusions; major conversion; the three tier
limit sets with their construction-date triggers; the Tier III areas and construction dates; the logbook
recording requirement; the exceptions and shipyard exemption; the emulsion mechanism and its operating price;
the SCR chemistry, arrangement, temperature window, slip and consumables, with the EGR comparison; and the
Technical File's origin, approval, content and effect, with its evidence limit declared.

## 9. ANSWER ROUTE AS BUILT

Archetype `explain`. The principal headings of the model answer ARE this route.

**1. Regulation 13 - which engines are caught, and which are not** - limb **A**

- Marine diesel engines of more than 130 kW installed on a ship; major conversion on or after 1 January 2000
- Major conversion: replacement, additional engine, substantial modification, or MCR increased by more than 10%
- Excluded: emergency-only and lifeboat engines; domestic-trading ships under an alternative NOx measure;
  the Administration's pre-19 May 2005 exclusion

**2. Tier I, Tier II and Tier III - the limits, and what fixes which one applies** - limb **A**

- Total weighted NO2 in g/kWh as a function of rated speed n; the SHIP's construction date fixes the tier
- Tier I 17.0 / 45 n^-0.2 / 9.8; Tier II 14.4 / 44 n^-0.23 / 7.7; Tier III 3.4 / 9 n^-0.2 / 2.0
- Tier II global, met by engine design; Tier III needs SCR, EGR or a low-NOx gas process

**3. Where Tier III applies - the areas, and the logbook** - limb **A**

- Four areas; 1 January 2016 for the American areas, 1 January 2021 for the European ones
- Outside them the ship reverts to Tier II, which is why the engine is certified to both
- Tier and on/off status logged on entry, exit and change, with date, time and position
- Narrow exceptions; the temporary shipyard exemption; do not confuse NOx and SOx area lists

**4. Homogenizers for water emulsion** - limb **B**

- High-shear device producing a stable water-in-oil emulsion; water metered in ahead of it
- Water absorbs latent and sensible heat and adds mass, lowering peak flame temperature and thermal NO
- Micro-explosion improves atomisation; secondary fuel-treatment benefit
- Costs: calorific value per unit mass, corrosion and wear, ignition delay, cannot be stored, must be
  instantly bypassable

**5. Selective Catalytic Reduction** - limb **C**

- Urea or ammonia over a catalyst reduces NOx to nitrogen and water vapour
- Before the turbine on a two-stroke, after it on a four-stroke, for temperature reasons
- Temperature window, ammonium bisulphate, sulphur; dosing accuracy and ammonia slip
- Urea storage and heating; reactor space; catalyst fouling, poisoning, ageing and replacement
- Compared with EGR - no consumable but a scrubber, and it changes combustion directly

**6. The NOx Technical File** - limb **D**

- Regulation 13.8 applies the revised NOx Technical Code to certification, testing and measurement
- Prepared by the builder, approved by the Administration at certification, accompanies the engine for life
- Defines the certified condition: NOx-critical components and their permitted adjustment ranges
- Parameter check at survey; defeat devices prohibited under 13.9; the Approved Method file for pre-2000
  engines
- The evidence limit stated openly
