## 9. ANSWER ROUTE AS BUILT

Archetype `explain`. The route is the single canonical sequence; the model answer's principal
headings **are** these steps, and `validate_spec.py` enforces that correspondence.

1. **The principle, stated as a contrast**
   - additive manufacturing builds a part by adding material layer upon layer under the control of a digital model
   - contrast with subtractive - material removed - and formative - material forced into shape by a mould or die
   - consequence one: complexity is free, because the machine only follows a different path
   - consequence two: no tooling, so no minimum quantity - one part costs the same each as a hundred
   - consequence three: the part is a file, so inventory becomes digital and can be made where it is needed
2. **The working chain, from model to finished part**
   - digital model, from CAD or reverse-engineered by 3D scanning an existing component
   - tessellation to a surface mesh the machine software can process
   - orientation and support generation - orientation fixes the layer direction and therefore the weak direction
   - slicing into layers and conversion to a tool path
   - build, then removal from supports and build plate
   - post-processing - stress relief or heat treatment, hot isostatic pressing, machining of mating surfaces, finishing
   - inspection - dimensional, non-destructive examination, and witness coupons where required
3. **The process families a marine engineer will meet**
   - material extrusion - thermoplastic, cheap and tolerant, low strength; the realistic shipboard option
   - vat photopolymerisation - resin, high resolution but brittle; patterns and gauges
   - powder bed fusion - laser or electron beam melting of powder; the principal route to dense metal parts
   - directed energy deposition - powder or wire melted as deposited; large parts, cladding and REPAIR by build-up
   - binder jetting - binder into powder, then debind and sinter; also prints sand cores and moulds for castings
4. **Where it is actually used**
   - ASHORE - yard tooling, jigs and fixtures; patterns, cores and moulds for castings
   - ASHORE - obsolete and long-lead spares reverse-engineered from the worn part; prototyping of retrofits
   - ASHORE - repair rather than replacement by directed energy deposition or cold spray on shafts, liners, seats and wear rings
   - ON BOARD - polymer guards, brackets, covers, seals, cleats, tools, gauges and templates; temporary items honestly described as temporary
   - SUPPLY CHAIN - digital inventory as a qualified file, and distributed manufacturing near major repair ports
5. **Advantages, stated where they are real**
   - lead time, and the gain is largest where conventional supply is worst
   - no tooling and no minimum quantity, which is the normal marine spares case
   - inventory and capital - a file occupies no space and is not written off with the ship
   - design freedom - internal passages, weight reduction, part consolidation removing joints and fasteners
   - repair as an alternative to replacement
   - resilience and sustainability - shorter chains, less air freight, less material waste
   - and the limit: for a standard part from stock, printing is slower and dearer - it competes on availability
6. **Challenges - and the one that actually blocks adoption**
   - QUALIFICATION AND APPROVAL FIRST - material and part are created together, so material and process approval cannot be separated
   - material properties and anisotropy, residual stress, porosity and surface roughness, all reducing fatigue performance
   - post-processing is unavoidable and is part of the cost and lead time
   - size and build rate limits put large marine components outside the practical envelope
   - shipboard practicality - power, space, atmosphere, motion, powder and fume hazards, competence
   - intellectual property and liability when an OEM part is reverse-engineered and printed
   - cyber integrity of the file - an altered file is an altered part
   - management of change under the safety management system, especially for critical equipment
   - the balanced position - use it now for tooling, patterns, polymer items and repair; demand the same evidence as any other component elsewhere
