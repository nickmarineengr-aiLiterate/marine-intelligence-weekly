**Technical ambiguities identified before drafting:**

- 'Twin Skeg Design' as printed could mean a twin-skeg hull with two propellers, or a twin-skeg form with a single screw. The commercial meaning, and the one the question intends, is the twin-screw twin-skeg hull, and the answer says which reading it takes
