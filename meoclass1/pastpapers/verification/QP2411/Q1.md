## 7. VERIFICATION STATEMENT

FRESH RESEARCH, NO DONOR. This question sets no instrument and the answer does not invent one. NO IMO OR STATUTORY INSTRUMENT PRESCRIBES THE USE OF VR OR AR, AND NONE HAS BEEN NAMED TO GIVE THE ANSWER FALSE AUTHORITY. The one regulatory anchor that is genuinely available is the STCW simulator regime - the Convention permits training and assessment by simulator and sets standards for simulator performance and for the conduct of simulator-based training - and it is used only to make the single point that an immersive trainer is not automatically a recognised means of demonstrating competence. That statement is made at the level of the regime, not by quoting provisions, because MIW holds no licensed STCW Code text and the 2017 consolidated edition in the corpus carries no text layer.
