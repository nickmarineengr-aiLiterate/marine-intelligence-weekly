**Technical ambiguities identified before drafting:**

- 'Main offenses' is deliberately open. The 1988 Article 3 list has seven principal limbs plus attempt, abetting and threat; the answer gives all seven rather than a selection, because the examiner asked for the offences and a partial list looks like partial knowledge.
- 'Its 2005 Protocol' is printed in the singular. There are in fact TWO 2005 Protocols, one to the Convention and one to the Fixed Platforms Protocol. The answer treats the Convention Protocol as the subject, which is plainly what is meant, and mentions the fixed-platform companion once so the completeness is visible without derailing the answer.

It is included because the question asks how the amendments reflect evolving security concerns, and interdiction is the clearest evidence of that shift - but it is given its own step so it is not confused with the offence list.
