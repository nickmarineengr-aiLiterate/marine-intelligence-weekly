**Technical ambiguities identified before drafting:**

- 'The two short-term measures' is the crux and is genuinely capable of being misread. The Strategy defines short-term measures by TIMING - those finalized and agreed between 2018 and 2023 - which is a wider class. But the two measures 'approved for implementation' with a guideline layer of their own are EEXI and the operational carbon intensity rating, adopted together by MEPC.328(76). The answer identifies them expressly and says why EEDI and SEEMP are not the answer.
- 'With their guidelines' could be answered with a single sentence. It is the highest-value part of the question and is given its own step, naming the five CII guidelines by letter and resolution and the SEEMP guidelines by edition.
- 'Long-term goals' invites a list of long-term measures. There is none: the Strategy defines them only by timing, beyond 2030 and to be developed in the 2028 review. Inventing a list would be fabrication and the answer says so.
- 'Ambitions' is loose. The Strategy distinguishes LEVELS OF AMBITION from INDICATIVE CHECKPOINTS, and the two use different measures - intensity per transport work against total annual emissions. The answer separates them.
