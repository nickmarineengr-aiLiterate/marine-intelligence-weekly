**Technical ambiguities identified before drafting:**

- 'Define P&I insurance and H&M insurance' invites two textbook sentences and nothing more. The definitions are written so that the STRUCTURAL difference - commercial indemnity against mutual liability cover - is visible in the definition itself, because that is what generates the rest of the comparison.
- 'Why are both types of insurance essential' can be answered as a truism. It is answered concretely instead: without hull there is no security for the mortgage, and without club entry the ship cannot obtain compulsory-insurance certification and cannot trade.
- The collision liability split is usually taught as an invariable three-fourths. It is the settled convention but modern hull forms may write four-fourths by agreement, and the answer states the variation rather than flattening it.
- General average is not mentioned in the stem and could be treated as out of scope. It appears once, at the boundary step, because it is the clearest case where a hull claim and a liability exposure arise from the same act - but it is not allowed to take over an answer about two classes of insurance.
