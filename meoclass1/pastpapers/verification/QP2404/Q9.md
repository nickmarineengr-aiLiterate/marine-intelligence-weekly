## 3. Temporal review - the flag was correct, and one boundary was found

**The intake flag of `STABLE / LOW` was CORRECT**, and that is recorded expressly rather than
passed over, because the protocol requires a flag to be answered either way. UNCLOS entered into
force 16 November 1994 and neither the zone provisions nor Part XII has changed since.

**One live boundary was found and handled.** The **BBNJ Agreement** - the agreement under UNCLOS on
the conservation and sustainable use of marine biological diversity of areas beyond national
jurisdiction - was:

| Event | Date | Position for this paper |
|---|---|---|
| **Adopted** | **19 June 2023** | **before** this sitting |
| Opened for signature | 20 September 2023 | before this sitting |
| **Entered into force** | **17 January 2026** | **long after** this sitting |

So at April 2024 it may properly be named as **adopted and awaiting entry into force**, and it is
so named in the study notes as a closing point for limb (a). **It is expressly not written as
applying, and no proposition about its content appears in the model answer.**

This is a clean illustration of the protocol rule that **approved, adopted and in force are three
different states with three different dates**. Writing the BBNJ Agreement as operative at April
2024 would be a forward temporal violation.

## 4. Donor, deltas, reverse-hint

**No donor.** No solved object in the corpus treats UNCLOS zones or Part XII. Built pollution
material runs on **MARPOL**, which is a different instrument doing a different job, and was not
used. No reverse-hint candidate touches this question. Deltas not applicable.

The relationship between the two instruments is, however, made a **teaching point**: UNCLOS sets no
discharge standards and directs States to act through the competent international organization,
which **is why MARPOL exists**.

## 6. What could not be verified

- **MIW holds no licensed consolidated copy of UNCLOS.** Provisions are described **by subject
  matter rather than quoted by article number to the candidate**. This is deliberate as well as
  necessary: the marks are for the distances, the sovereignty / sovereign rights distinction and
  the enforcement structure, not for citation, and a misremembered article number is worse in an
  examination than an accurate statement of the rule. Acquiring a licensed UNCLOS text would
  promote this to primary and permit article-level citation.
- **Nothing is asserted about India's implementing legislation** or any domestic maritime zones
  statute. The question is wholly international, and treaty ratification and domestic enactment are
  separate matters that should not be run together.
- The BBNJ Agreement is referred to **only as to its status** at the sitting date.
