## 4. Donor, deltas and reverse-hint

**No donor.** No solved object in the corpus treats ammonia as a fuel. Decarbonisation-strategy
answers in the corpus address the IMO GHG Strategy rather than a specific fuel and were not used.
No reverse-hint candidate touches this question. Question, marks and temporal deltas are therefore
not applicable.

**Ammonia carriage under the IGC Code is deliberately kept separate from ammonia use as fuel.**
Carriage is settled and long regulated; use as fuel was not. Merging them would import a settled
regime into a question about an unsettled one, and the study notes name this as a common mistake.
