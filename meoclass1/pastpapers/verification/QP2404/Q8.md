## 1. The edition finding, read at source

**`Resolution A.1188(33)`, "2023 Guidelines on implementation of the International Safety
Management (ISM) Code by Administrations", adopted on 6 December 2023.** Read at source from the
IMO's own published copy and extracted with PyMuPDF. Operative paragraph 5, verbatim:

> "REVOKES resolution A.1118(30)."

`A.1118(30)` dates from 2017, had been the current edition for **six years**, and is what almost
all circulating study material cites. **It was revoked four months before this sitting.**

**Why this trap is dangerous rather than merely wrong.** The substance of the paragraphs relied on
is largely unchanged between the two editions, so an answer citing `A.1118(30)` **reads correctly
and is simply wrong about its authority** - and nothing downstream would catch it. This is the
protocol's "most dangerous single error" in its purest form.

**GENERALISABLE:** the **33rd IMO Assembly (December 2023) is a standing edition boundary for every
2024 sitting**, exactly as the 34th Assembly (December 2025) already is for 2025 sittings. Any 2024
paper citing an Assembly-adopted guideline should be checked against it.

The answer cites `A.1188(33)` throughout and names `A.1118(30)` **only as the revoked
predecessor**. A sweep confirms every one of the eleven candidate-facing occurrences of
`A.1118(30)` is in that form; none uses it as operative authority.

## 3. The open item, resolved honestly

**Limbs (b)(iii) and (b)(iv) - extension of the SMC, and revision of an entry - correspond to NO
named IMO provision.**

The whole of `A.1188(33)` was searched at source for "extension", "revision", "withdraw" and
"invalid": **none appears**. The ISM Code's certification section provides for renewal verification
before expiry and fixes the validity of a new certificate where renewal is completed within three
months of expiry - **but that is a renewal, not an extension**, and there is no entry-revision
mechanism.

**The answer therefore reasons from the recognized organization's delegated authority** - the
Administration verifies and issues, the RO acts only under a written authorization, so technical
findings are the RO's to decide while changes to the terms of certification are not - **and states
in terms that no named provision exists.**

This is `PASTPAPER_PRODUCTION_PROTOCOL.md` section 2.1 applied deliberately: *"an invented
regulation number is a far worse defect than an openly declared engineering-judgement answer."*
A reviewer should treat the later appearance of a specific paragraph number against either limb as
a **regression** unless it has been read at source.
