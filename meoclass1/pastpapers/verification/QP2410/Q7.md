**The donor's own closing advice is therefore wrong for this sitting and is reversed.** It tells the

for its own paper and would cost about four marks on this one. That reversal is recorded because it
is the exact failure a candidate revising from a worked answer to the earlier sitting would make.

The general lesson is worth more than the question: **an examiner who recycles a stem often edits
it, and the edit is where the marks move.**

own energy-efficiency framing is now cited at **P1** - attained EEXI regulation 23, required EEXI

The `QP2402-Q3` pre-2021 numbering defect is **not inherited**: regulation 21 is *Functional
requirements*, not EEDI.

provenance class and is stated rather than disguised.

## Sources and provenance

- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT, settled naval architecture: overall propulsive efficiency as the product of hull efficiency, open-water (propeller) efficiency and relative rotative efficiency, and the devices in this question acting chiefly on the second.
- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT, settled naval architecture: the recoverable loss mechanisms in a propeller race - axial or slipstream loss from accelerating the water aft; rotational loss from the swirl left in the race, which is the largest single recoverable loss in a conventional installation; blade frictional loss; and tip vortex loss where the pressure difference between face and back equalises around the blade edge.
- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT: the thrust loading coefficient as the parameter determining whether an accelerating duct helps or hurts - a duct earns its drag at high loading and low speed and penalises a lightly loaded, fast ship.
- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT: the ducted (Kort nozzle) principle - an annular aerofoil which, in the accelerating form, converges internally to accelerate flow into the disc while itself generating forward thrust through its own circulation, and whose close tip clearance suppresses the tip vortex. The decelerating duct reverses the section to raise pressure at the disc and suppress cavitation and radiated noise, principally a naval application.
- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT: the Kappel principle - blade tips curved smoothly towards the suction side as an integrated continuous winglet rather than a bolted end plate, allowing load to be carried further outboard while the tip vortex is weakened and displaced from the blade. The aeronautical analogy with an aircraft winglet is exact.
- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT: the contra-rotating principle - two coaxial propellers turning in opposite directions, the after one recovering rotational energy from the race of the forward one, requiring either concentric inner and outer shafting with epicyclic gearing or a podded after unit driven electrically in the hybrid arrangement.
- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT: azimuth propulsion - a thruster unit rotatable through 360 degrees producing thrust in any direction, in mechanical Z-drive or L-drive form or as an electrically driven pod, with the pulling (tractor) configuration presenting the propeller to undisturbed flow ahead of its own housing; and its consequences for hull form, machinery arrangement, manoeuvring and dynamic positioning.
- ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT: cavitation as the ceiling on blade loading, arising when local pressure falls to vapour pressure, and its erosion, vibration and radiated-noise consequences; and the wake field as the reason every device must be designed for the specific hull.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED, READ AT SOURCE THIS SESSION - and an upgrade over the donor: Resolution MEPC.328(76), 2021 revised MARPOL Annex VI, in force 1 November 2022. The chapter 4 numbering read off the annex itself - regulation 21 functional requirements, 22 attained EEDI, 23 attained EEXI, 24 required EEDI, 25 required EEXI, 26 SEEMP, 28 operational carbon intensity. This supplies the stem's framing, which the March 2024 donor could only gesture at because it had not read the Annex.
