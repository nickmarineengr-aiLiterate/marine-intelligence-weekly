## A second numbering trap, specific to this paper

The printed stem says *"the EEXI framework under **Phase 2** (of 20% - 30% reduction)"*. **Read at
source, EEXI has no phases**: regulation 25 Table 3 sets a *single fixed reduction factor Y per ship
type and size band*, and the phases belong to the **required EEDI** in regulation 24 Table 1. The
printed wording is preserved exactly in `text_verbatim` and the position is **explained, not
corrected away** - the EEXI factors do land in the 15 to 30 per cent band for most merchant types
and are derived from the EEDI reference lines, so the figure in the stem is right and the phase
label belongs to EEDI.
