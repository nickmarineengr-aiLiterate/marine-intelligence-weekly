MIW holds the ILO consolidated text of **2020**, *"including the Amendments of 2014, 2016 and

## The load-bearing date is before the sitting and is safe

**The 2014 amendments, which introduced Standard A2.5.2 and created the whole financial security
regime, entered into force on 18 January 2017** - seven and a half years before this examination.
Read at source in the ILO Compendium, 4th revised edition, which also records the Office's own
observation that *before the entry into force of the 2014 amendments, on 18 January 2017, the
MLC, 2006 did not directly address the serious problem of abandonment of seafarers*.

So the regime is unambiguously available at this sitting, and the answer states its commencement
date - which is also what explains why older material on the subject reads so differently.

- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED, READ AT SOURCE THIS SESSION from the corpus copy of the Maritime Labour Convention, 2006, as amended - the ILO consolidated text of 2020 including the Amendments of 2014, 2016 and 2018, which is the operative edition at this sitting: STANDARD A2.5.2, paragraph 1 - the Standard establishes requirements to ensure an expeditious and effective financial security system to assist seafarers in the event of their abandonment, in implementation of Regulation 2.5, paragraph 2.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED, same reading: STANDARD A2.5.2 PARAGRAPH 2, THE DEFINITION - 'a seafarer shall be deemed to have been abandoned where, in violation of the requirements of this Convention or the terms of the seafarers' employment agreement, the shipowner: (a) fails to cover the cost of the seafarer's repatriation; or (b) has left the seafarer without the necessary maintenance and support; or (c) has otherwise unilaterally severed their ties with the seafarer including failure to pay contractual wages for a period of at least two months.'
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED, same reading: STANDARD A2.5.2 paragraphs 3 to 5 - each Member shall ensure a financial security system is in place for ships flying its flag, which may take the form of a social security scheme, insurance, a national fund or other similar arrangements, its form determined after consultation with shipowners' and seafarers' organisations. The system shall provide DIRECT ACCESS, SUFFICIENT COVERAGE AND EXPEDITED FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE to any abandoned seafarer on a ship flying the flag of the Member. Necessary maintenance and support shall include adequate food, accommodation, drinking water supplies, ESSENTIAL FUEL FOR SURVIVAL ON BOARD THE SHIP, and necessary medical care.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED, same reading: STANDARD A2.5.2 paragraphs 6 to 8 - ships to which Regulation 5.1.3 applies shall CARRY ON BOARD a certificate or other documentary evidence of financial security issued by the provider, a copy POSTED IN A CONSPICUOUS PLACE where it is available to the seafarers, with the information required in APPENDIX A2-I, in English or with an English translation; where more than one provider gives cover, each provider's document is carried. Assistance shall be granted PROMPTLY upon request by the seafarer or their nominated representative, supported by the necessary justification of entitlement.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED, same reading: STANDARD A2.5.2 PARAGRAPHS 9 AND 10, THE SCOPE OF COVER - assistance shall be sufficient to cover (a) outstanding wages and other entitlements due under the employment agreement, the relevant collective bargaining agreement or national law, LIMITED TO FOUR MONTHS of any such outstanding wages and FOUR MONTHS of any such outstanding entitlements; (b) all expenses reasonably incurred by the seafarer including repatriation; and (c) the essential needs of the seafarer including adequate food, clothing where necessary, accommodation, drinking water supplies, essential fuel for survival on board, necessary medical care and any other reasonable costs or charges from the act or omission constituting the abandonment until the seafarer's arrival at home. The cost of repatriation shall cover travel by appropriate and expeditious means, NORMALLY BY AIR, with food and accommodation from leaving the ship until arrival home, necessary medical care, passage and transport of personal effects.
- P1 PRIMARY VERIFIED, same reading: STANDARD A2.5.2 paragraphs 11 to 14 - the financial security shall not cease before the end of its period of validity unless the provider has given PRIOR NOTIFICATION OF AT LEAST 30 DAYS to the competent authority of the flag State; a provider which has paid acquires the seafarer's rights BY SUBROGATION; nothing prejudices the insurer's right of recourse against third parties; and the Standard is NOT EXCLUSIVE of any other rights, claims or remedies available to compensate abandoned seafarers.
