## 9. ANSWER ROUTE AS BUILT

Archetype `legal`. The principal headings of the model answer ARE this route - `validate_spec.py` enforces the correspondence, so the candidate cannot be taught one order and shown another.

**1. The governing instrument at this sitting** - limb **(a)**

- A.1185(33), Procedures for Port State Control, 2023, adopted 6 December 2023 - operative at this sitting
- The enabling provisions: SOLAS I/19, MARPOL art 5 and reg 11, Load Lines art 21, STCW X, MLC reg 5.2.1
- Do not name a 34th Assembly resolution - that Assembly sat December 2025

**2. "Clear grounds" - the definition** - limb **(a)**

- The initial inspection is limited to certificates, documents and a general impression of condition
- Clear grounds = evidence that ship, equipment or crew does not correspond substantially with convention requirements
- OR that master or crew are not familiar with essential shipboard procedures for safety or pollution prevention
- 'Substantially' excludes the trivial; 'evidence' excludes suspicion
- They unlock the more detailed inspection - and they are a limit on the officer as much as a power

**3. The circumstances that create clear grounds** - limb **(a)**

- Documents - certificates absent, expired, invalid or apparently falsified; Oil Record Book gaps
- Equipment - principal equipment or arrangements absent, or present but visibly unmaintained
- Condition - observed serious deterioration, heavy corrosion, plainly unsafe conditions
- People - crew unable to demonstrate familiarity with essential operations
- Operations - unsafe operations observed, SMS procedures not followed
- Third-party information - pilot, port authority, previous port State, crew complaint, incident report
- Manning - not manned per the Safe Manning Document, or hours of rest not observed

**4. ISM-related deficiencies** - limb **(a)**

- The distinguishing feature: they point at the system, not at an item - so nothing on board can be relied on
- SMC or DOC absent, invalid, expired or not matching the ship
- No SMS documentation, or uncontrolled or superseded documentation, or in a language the crew cannot read
- Master unable to demonstrate his authority under the SMS, or unaware of the master's review
- DPA unidentified or no contact details available
- No evidence of internal audits - or audits that never raise a non-conformity
- Drills not carried out or not recorded; maintenance not per the SMS; critical equipment not identified
- Non-conformities, accidents and hazardous occurrences not reported, investigated or closed
- A major non-conformity - serious threat to personnel, ship or environment, requiring immediate corrective action, including systemic failure to implement the Code
- Consequence can reach the Company's DOC and the rest of the fleet, not just this ship

**5. Five detainable deficiencies, their causes and their prevention** - limb **(b)**

- State the test first: unsafe to proceed to sea, unreasonable threat to the environment, or deficiencies so numerous they show systemic failure
- Emergency source of power - never load-tested, changeover untested; prevent by testing ON LOAD and recording it
- Steering gear and emergency steering - tests done on paper, changeover never exercised; prevent by real drills and posted instructions
- OWS / 15 ppm equipment - coalescer exhausted, monitor uncalibrated, stopping device disabled, ORB not reconciling; prevent by never permitting a bypass and reconciling the ORB yourself
- Fire pumps and fixed systems - emergency pump unprimed, main isolated, bottle survey lapsed; prevent by proving two jets and locking isolating valves open
- Bilge arrangements and machinery space condition - alarms disabled, quick-closing valves wired open, remote stops untested, oil-soaked lagging; prevent by scheduled in-port testing and a standing cleanliness discipline
- Close with the unifying sentence: these are testing and recording failures before they are mechanical failures

**6. PR17 - what it is, and the scenarios that lead to it** - limb **(c)**

- Say what it is: an IACS Procedural Requirement, binding on member societies - class practice, not an IMO instrument
- The trigger: a port State detention of a ship the society classed or statutorily certified as a Recognized Organization
- Scenario - detainable deficiency in an item the RO surveyed and certified, so certificate and condition conflict
- Scenario - a certificate issued when the ship did not meet the requirement, outside the window, or without proper survey
- Scenario - detention shortly after a survey by the society
- Scenario - a major SMS non-conformity where the society also issued the DOC or SMC
- Scenario - several deficiencies showing systemic failure the last survey should have found
- Scenario - class status itself wrong: overdue surveys, conditions of class not imposed or not followed up
- What follows: investigation, report to flag and Company, corrective action, and the detention recorded against the society's published RO performance
- State the evidence limit openly - MIW holds no IACS text; no wording is quoted
