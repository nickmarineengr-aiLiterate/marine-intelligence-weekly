## 9. ANSWER ROUTE AS BUILT

Archetype `explain`. The principal headings of the model answer ARE this route - `validate_spec.py` enforces the correspondence, so the candidate cannot be taught one order and shown another.

**1. What a P&I club is** - limb **(a)**

- A mutual association of shipowners, charterers and operators covering third-party liabilities from the operation of their ships
- The members are the club - no external shareholders, no profit extracted
- Surplus retained for the members; deficit met by them - hence calls, not premiums
- Liability cover, with no fixed sum insured
- Governed by a board drawn from the membership; run by professional managers
- Twelve clubs participate in the International Group - phrase it as a current figure, not a permanent one

**2. Why it is not ordinary marine insurance** - limb **(a)**

- Premium against call; property loss against legal liability; fixed sum insured against none
- Narrow defined perils against very wide cover, extensible by the board's omnibus discretion
- Profit to shareholders against surplus retained for members
- The three the corpus itself names: mutuality, breadth of cover, absence of a fixed sum insured

**3. How the club collects funds - the tiers** - limb **(a)**

- Advance call - on entry for the policy year, in instalments; the estimated cost collected up front
- Supplementary call - where claims and expenses exceed advance calls plus investment income. Not a penalty
- Release call - on leaving, buying out exposure to future supplementary calls on open policy years
- Say why years stay open: liability claims take years to mature
- Deductible - the member's first amount on every claim; suppresses small-claim frequency
- Rated on entered tonnage, then ship type, trade and area, crew nationality and terms, management standard - and above all the claims record
- One Indian line: Admiralty Act 2017 s.4(1)(s) makes an unpaid mutual insurance call a maritime claim supporting arrest

**4. Above the club - the International Group** - limb **(a)**

- The club's own retention
- The International Group Pooling Agreement - claims above retention shared among the participating clubs
- The Group's collective reinsurance, and above it an overspill layer met by members through an overspill call
- Why it matters: cover of a magnitude no single insurer would write, and a solvent target for a coastal State after a major casualty
- Give no figures - retentions and layers are reset annually

**5. "Protection" - liabilities to people and to the world** - limb **(b)**

- Crew - injury, illness, death, medical, hospital and funeral, repatriation, personal effects, wages in defined circumstances
- Other persons - stevedores, pilots, visitors, passengers, third parties
- Collision - the proportion not covered by hull, which classically covers only three-fourths
- Fixed and floating objects - piers, jetties, berths, buoys, cables, pipelines, navigational aids
- Pollution - liability, clean-up and preventive measures beyond the hull cover
- Wreck removal where compulsory by law
- Stowaways, refugees, persons rescued at sea; quarantine and disinfection
- Fines - pollution, cargo shortage, immigration and customs, safety breaches, so far as the rules permit
- Towage and salvage liabilities under approved contracts; legal costs throughout

**6. "Indemnity" - liability to cargo** - limb **(b)**

- Loss, damage, shortage and contamination of cargo carried, from breach of the contract of carriage
- Cargo's proportion of general average unrecoverable because of the owner's breach - the link to Q8
- Liabilities under approved bills of lading and charterparty terms
- Disposal of damaged or unclaimed cargo; through and transhipment liabilities

**7. The boundary - what P&I does not cover, and why** - limb **(b)**

- NOT damage to the member's own ship - that is hull and machinery
- NOT the member's own trading loss - delay and loss of hire is a separate cover. P&I covers liabilities, not your own losses
- NOT war risks; NOT liabilities under unapproved contracts; NOT where trading in breach of class or terms of entry
- The corollary: the club issues the blue cards behind the CLC and Bunkers certificates, the MLC financial security, and the Nairobi wreck-removal certificate
- No entry, no certificates, no trading - link to Q1 and Q6

**8. How the club handles a claim** - limb **(c)**

- Takes control of the defence - appoints or approves lawyers, surveyors and experts; no admission or settlement without the club
- Works through correspondents - attendance on board, statements while the crew are aboard, surveys and joint surveys
- Provides security - the letter of undertaking that prevents or lifts arrest, in place of cash or a bank guarantee
- 'Pay to be paid' - the member discharges first and is indemnified; in practice the club pays claimants directly, especially seafarers and coastal States
- Utmost good faith - full disclosure, and nothing done to prejudice the club's position

**9. The procedure for filing a claim** - limb **(c)**

- Notify immediately - club and local correspondent; rules impose time limits and late notice can defeat the claim. Notify on suspicion
- Preserve and gather evidence at once - logs, engine and bridge data, Oil Record Book, crew statements, photographs, damaged items, cargo and stowage records, soundings, SMS records
- Arrange survey, and a joint survey where cargo or a third party is involved
- Do not admit liability, do not settle; sign nothing beyond a factual receipt or protest
- Note protest and issue letters of protest or holding replies
- Submit the formal claim with incident report, surveys, invoices, wage and medical records, B/L and charterparty, legal correspondence
- Request security where arrest threatens, so the LOU can issue and the ship can sail
- Cooperate through the defence - witnesses, documents, the club's instructions
- Settlement and reimbursement, less the deductible, with the club pursuing recovery by subrogation under the Marine Insurance Act, 1963
- Close on the Chief Engineer's own part: report at once, preserve the evidence, write it down while fresh, admit nothing
