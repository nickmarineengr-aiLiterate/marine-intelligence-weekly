## 2. The anticipated Merchant Shipping Act reversal DOES NOT ARISE

`QP2509_TEMPORAL_AND_DONOR_ANCHOR.md` sections 2.2 and 3.7 flagged Q3 as one of three questions
requiring the **Merchant Shipping Act 2025 to be reversed back to the 1958 Act**, because the
donor sits ten months later and after the **15 March 2026** commencement. The session prompt
carried the same instruction.

**On inspection the donor carries no Merchant Shipping Act limb at all.**

- General average is **contractual** - it operates through the York-Antwerp Rules as incorporated
  by the bill of lading or charterparty.
- The Indian statutory limb is **section 66 of the Marine Insurance Act, 1963** - a **different
  statute**, which the Merchant Shipping Act 2025 neither repeals nor amends. That Act's
  section 324(1) repealed the **Merchant Shipping Act, 1958**, and nothing else.
- A term probe of the donor object for `Merchant Shipping Act 2025`, `MS Act 2025`, `2025 Act`
  and `1958` returned **zero hits in any field**.

**There is nothing to reverse.** Protocol section 2 requires the answer to a flag to be recorded
either way, including *"checked, no temporal issue"*, and the flag list is not what clears a
paper - the sweeps are.

The point is nonetheless **added to the study guide**, in the opposite direction from the one
anticipated: candidates reach for the Merchant Shipping Act on any Indian maritime-law question,
and the note tells them why it is not engaged here. It also records that the operative merchant
shipping statute at this sitting was the **1958 Act**, so that a reader who does go looking is
not left with the wrong one.
