## 2. The defect

### 2.1 What the donor says

> *"The current guidance is the Survey Guidelines under the HSSC, 2025, resolution **A.1207(34)**,
> adopted **3 December 2025**, which revokes A.1186(33)."*

The 34th IMO Assembly session met in **late 2025** and adopted A.1207(34) on **3 December 2025**.
**This examination was sat in September 2025** - three months earlier. At the sitting:

| | |
|---|---|
| Operative edition | **Resolution A.1186(33)**, *Survey Guidelines under the Harmonized System of Survey and Certification (HSSC), 2023* |
| Adopted | **6 December 2023**, 33rd Assembly session, agenda items 11 and 13 |
| Revokes | **Resolution A.1156(32)** |
| A.1207(34) | **did not exist** |

Verified by reading **A.1186(33) itself** - the IMO published copy, 230 pages, extracted with
PyMuPDF. The preamble carries `REVOKES resolution A.1156(32).` and the header carries
`Adopted on 6 December 2023`.

The anchor at section 3.7 warned *"expect eight again, in reverse"*, on the basis of a previous
question where the Merchant Shipping Act was asserted on eight surfaces of a single object. The
real count here is **fourteen**, and on a **completely different instrument** from the one
predicted.

### 2.3 The donor's own trap warning was inverted

The donor's *Common mistakes* list contained:

> *"Citing **A.1186(33)** as the current Survey Guidelines. It was revoked by **A.1207(34)**,
> adopted 3 December 2025."*

**For a September 2025 sitting that instruction is exactly backwards.** A.1186(33) is the correct
citation; A.1207(34) did not exist. A candidate following the donor's warning would have written
the wrong resolution for their own paper.

The bullet has been **replaced** with one warning against the opposite error, and the point is
given its own study-guide section and its own retrieval card (**C9**), because a candidate
revising in 2026 will find current material telling them A.1186(33) is revoked.

## 3. Why the routine sweep did not catch it - a method finding

The standard donor-contamination sweep probes for **future year tokens** (`2026`), the Merchant
Shipping Act, `See Qn` pointers and currency words. Run against this donor it reported:

```
2026   2   (host_recurrence_hint, decomposition_gate.completed - both metadata)
QP26   26  (object ids)
today  1   (harmless prose)
everything else  0
```

**Clean.** The defect is dated **3 December 2025** - the *same calendar year as the sitting* - and
is therefore **invisible to a year-token probe**.

> **A future-contamination sweep must be DATE-aware relative to the sitting MONTH, not
> year-token-aware.** The defect was found only by a second pass that extracted every
> resolution-like token and every date in the object and compared each against September 2025.

This has been carried into the paper-level sweep and is recorded as a reusable method finding.

## 4. The content transfers; only the citation did not

This is what makes the defect dangerous rather than merely wrong. **A.1186(33)** sections 4.2, 4.3
and 4.4 were read verbatim and compared against the donor's assertions:

| Fact | A.1186(33) text | Donor | Match |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual frequency | 4.2.1 - *"within three months before or after each anniversary date of the certificate"* | same | yes |
| Annual purpose | 4.2.2.1 - *"enable the Administration to verify that the condition of the ship, its machinery and equipment is being maintained"* | same | yes |
| Annual scope | 4.2.2.2 - certificate examination; visual examination of *"a sufficient extent of the ship and its equipment"*; certain tests; *"a visual examination to confirm that no unapproved modifications have been made"* | same | yes |
| Periodical frequency | 4.4.1 - Safety **Equipment**: second **or** third anniversary, *"should take the place of one of the annual surveys"*. Safety **Radio**: *"within three months before or after each anniversary date"* | same | yes |
| Periodical scope | 4.4.2.1 - *"an inspection, with tests when necessary, of the equipment"* to ensure compliance, satisfactory condition and fitness for service; 4.4.2.2 - documentation check | same | yes |
| Intermediate | 4.3.1 - second or third anniversary, taking the place of one of the annual surveys | same | yes |

**Every survey fact is identical between the two editions.** No survey content required change.

The consequence is worth stating plainly, because it is the protocol's section 8 case: an answer
built on the donor would have been **internally coherent, confidently written, well-structured and
wrong in exactly one respect** - the resolution number and date - and **nothing downstream would
have caught it**. The word-count check, the validator, the health check and the UI test are all
blind to it.

## 5. Merchant Shipping Act - checked, not engaged

The anchor flagged Q9 as requiring reversal of the Merchant Shipping Act 2025 to the 1958 Act. A
term probe of the donor for `Merchant Shipping`, `1958`, `MS Act 2025` and `2025 Act` returned
**zero hits in any field**.

The question is entirely international - SOLAS II-1/3-1, II-1/3-10 and XI-1/1, the RO Code, IACS
and the HSSC. The Indian Register of Shipping would appear only as an example of a recognized
organization. **The predicted trap does not arise**, and that is recorded rather than passed over,
per protocol section 2.

**Two of the three questions the anchor flagged for the Merchant Shipping Act reversal - Q3 and
Q9 - turned out not to engage that Act at all.** The flag was a reasonable prediction from the
donors' sitting dates, and it was wrong on inspection in both cases. What it did do was cause
both donors to be swept carefully, which is how the A.1207(34) defect was found.
