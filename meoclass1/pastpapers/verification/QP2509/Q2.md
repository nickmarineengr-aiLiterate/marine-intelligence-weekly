**Reused after re-verification** (nothing in the CII regime changed between August and September

**Rewritten - interval arithmetic.** The donor says MEPC 83 fell "four months before this
examination". For September that is **five**. The donor's arithmetic was correct for its own
sitting and silently wrong for this one. This is the quiet failure mode of a one-month forward
pull: nothing is factually imported, but every relative interval is off by one.

**Rewritten - three internal cross-references, and this is the real defect found.** The donor
carried the pointer **"See Q8"** on three separate surfaces:

Copied verbatim, all three would have sent a candidate from the CII answer to an unrelated
answer on seafarer welfare and fatigue. All three are rewritten to state the Net-Zero position

identified: a donor's *internal* references are as sitting-specific as its dates, and they are
invisible to a temporal sweep because they contain no date at all.
