## 9. ANSWER ROUTE AS BUILT

Archetype `explain`. The route is the single canonical sequence; the model answer's
principal headings **are** these steps, and `validate_spec.py` enforces that
correspondence.

1. **The definition - and it is printed twice, not once**
   - ESP Code A.1049(27) paragraph 1.2.9, mandatory through SOLAS XI-1/2
   - general definition - wastage in excess of 75 per cent of allowable margins, but within acceptable limits
   - IACS CSR definition - gauged thickness between t-net + 0.5 mm and t-net
   - 'but within acceptable limits' means the steel is still acceptable: this is a warning state, not a defect
2. **Where the concept sits, and the model it presupposes**
   - rule scantling equals net thickness plus corrosion addition; the addition is the allowable margin
   - renewal thickness is the point at which the margin is exhausted
   - paragraph 1.1.3 - the Code is a MINIMUM and the survey is extended when substantial corrosion is found
   - paragraph 1.2.2 - a combined cargo and ballast space becomes a ballast tank once substantial corrosion is found there
3. **The criteria used to assess it during inspections**
   - measured wastage against the rule renewal thickness, expressed as a proportion of the margin
   - the corrosion PATTERN, not a single low reading
   - coating condition graded GOOD, FAIR or POOR under paragraph 1.2.11, with the 20 per cent and 10 per cent thresholds
   - whether a corrosion prevention system is present and effective, per 1.2.10
   - suspect-area status under 1.2.8 - including the surveyor's independent judgement route
   - the ship's service and repair history, per 1.2.7
4. **Inspection techniques - how it is actually found**
   - preparation - cleaning, de-scaling, ventilation, gas-freeing, lighting and safe access
   - overall survey to determine the extent of additional close-up survey
   - close-up survey - within close visual inspection range, normally within reach of hand (1.2.5)
   - ultrasonic thickness measurement by a certified company, witnessed by the surveyor
   - paragraph 1.5 - measurement simultaneous with the close-up survey
   - MPI or dye penetrant where cracking rather than wastage is suspected; hammer testing
   - tank pressure testing, and recording through the thickness measurement report and Condition Evaluation Report
5. **What a finding does to the renewal survey**
   - the baseline - may commence at the fourth annual and complete by the fifth anniversary (2.1.1)
   - scope under 2.1.4, sufficient to DISCOVER substantial corrosion; dry-dock survey part of it; no concurrent crediting (2.1.7)
   - extend thickness measurement to the annex extent, and complete it BEFORE the survey is credited
   - extend close-up survey to establish the true extent of the affected structure
   - record the locations as suspect areas so they follow the ship
   - assess against renewal thickness, and deal with the cause - coating and anodes - not only the symptom
6. **Repair methods and their acceptance**
   - cropping and renewal as the primary permanent remedy, with approved procedures, welders and consumables and post-weld NDT
   - partial renewal and insert plates with full penetration welds, sited clear of high-stress locations
   - doubler plates as a TEMPORARY and CONDITIONAL measure only, normally with a condition of class
   - weld repair and build-up for grooving corrosion, within the society's limits
   - renewal of the corrosion prevention system and anodes
   - prior approval of the repair scheme and survey of the completed work
7. **The role of the classification society**
   - rule-maker - scantlings, corrosion additions, renewal thicknesses, and adoption of IACS URs and CSR
   - survey planner - the survey programme agreed in advance, and the extent of close-up survey and gauging
   - attending surveyor - overall and close-up survey, witnessing the gauging, applying the criteria
   - approver of the repair scheme, materials, welding procedures and welders
   - keeper of the survey status, thickness records, Condition Evaluation Report and suspect-area register
   - enforcer - condition of class, then suspension; and separately, as the flag's RO, the statutory certificate
   - certifier of the thickness-measurement company under the Code's own annex
