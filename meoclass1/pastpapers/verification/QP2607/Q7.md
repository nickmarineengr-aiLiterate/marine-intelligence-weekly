## 2. Statement-type separation — the point of this question

The brief requires three categories to be kept apart, and the answer does so explicitly:

| Category | Examples in this answer |
|---|---|
| **What the Act legally does** | Repeals the 1958 Act and the 1838 Act; widens ownership eligibility; permits bareboat charter-cum-demise registration; gives domestic effect to IMO/ILO obligations |
| **Government policy expectation** | Tonnage growth, inward investment, improved ease of doing business — objectives, not guarantees |
| **MIW inference** | That the registration reforms remove a genuine legal barrier so the direction of effect is right, but magnitude depends on fiscal treatment, subordinate rules and commercial factors the Act does not control |
