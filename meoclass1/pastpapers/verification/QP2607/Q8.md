## 2. Statement-type separation

- **Mandatory instruments:** STCW reg I/6 and section A-I/6; STCW competence tables; ISM elements 6, 8
  and 9.
- **IMO recommendatory instrument:** res. A.947(23) — vision, principles and goals, not prescriptive
  requirements.
- **Human-factors research:** automation bias, complacency, mode confusion, alarm fatigue, skill fade,
  out-of-the-loop performance decrement. Explicitly presented in the study notes as research findings,
  not as regulation.
- **Engineering judgement:** the competence-versus-performance framing itself.
