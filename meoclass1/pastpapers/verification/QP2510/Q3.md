## 3. Why the risk is structurally low

Worth stating because it is the reason this question ages well: **which edition of the Rules applies
is contractual, not chronological.** It is whichever edition the bill of lading or charterparty
names. The answer's central caution — *"the latest edition does not apply automatically"* — is
therefore itself time-proof, and a 2025 casualty under a bill naming the YAR 1994 is still adjusted
on the 1994 Rules.

## 4. What was REUSED after re-verification

common safety in time of peril**; the sacrifice and expenditure categories; the contribution

the Rule XVII exclusion, and the commission and interest changes).
