## 2. The adjudicated finding — Q1 genuinely carries the cyber circular

The handover recorded *"One update: Q1 (cyber → Rev.3)"* while the paper overview identifies **Q5**
as the cyber question. This looked like a typo and was flagged for adjudication. **It is not a
typo.**

QP2403-Q1's own answer cross-references `MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3` **ten times across eight distinct
surfaces** — `sources`, `model_answer`, `study_notes`, `regulations`, `answer_route`,
`quick_revision.recall_15s`, `quick_revision.critical_numbers` and a `retrieval_card`. The cyber
circular is the authority for the **cyber-risk barrier** in limb (B), which asks about barriers to
adopting big data. The donor's own `temporal_review` states the position exactly:

> *"For QP2510 the Single Window obligation is equally in force and the answer needs no change; the
> cyber cross-reference must move to Rev.3."*

**Verdict: report correct, canonical metadata correct.** Q1 *cross-references* the circular; Q5 *is*
the circular question. **Both updates were required and both were made, separately.**
