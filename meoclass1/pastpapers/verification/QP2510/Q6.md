## 3. **An adjacent instrument, found and deliberately excluded**

The currency check surfaced something close enough to be a trap, and it is a trap of a kind this
corpus has not previously had to guard against:

> Amendments to the **Ballast Water Management Convention** mandating approved **electronic Ballast
> Water Record Books** took effect on **1 October 2025 — three weeks before this examination** —
> with their own IMO guidelines.

**That is a different convention.** This question asks about record books *under MARPOL, as laid
down in resolution MEPC.312(74)*, and a Ballast Water Record Book is **not a MARPOL record book**.
Naming it in limb (a)'s enumeration would be a **category error, not a currency bonus**.

It is recorded in the **Study Guide** as a labelled adjacency, with the instruction to mention it —
if at all — only as an aside demonstrating that the candidate knows the difference. It appears
**nowhere in the Model Answer**, and a `B_CURRENCY_CHECK` guard asks a future maintainer to confirm
it has not migrated there.

> This is the mirror of an ordinary staleness error: **a genuinely current instrument that is simply
> the wrong instrument.** The temporal architecture exists to keep out material that is too new; this
> case shows it must also keep out material that is correctly dated but wrongly scoped.

later revised by MEPC.328(76)**. This remains `B_CURRENCY_CHECK` and is not blocking.
