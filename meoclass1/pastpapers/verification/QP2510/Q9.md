## 3. The four additions — each located verbatim in the 2022 consolidation

These are exact, not approximate, because they were found in the text rather than recalled.

### 3.1 Social connectivity — **Standard A3.1 §17**

> *"Appropriate seafarers' recreational facilities, amenities and services, **including social
> connectivity**, as adapted to meet the special needs of seafarers who must live and work on ships,
> shall be provided on board for the benefit of all seafarers, taking into account Regulation 4.3
> and the associated Code provisions on health and safety protection and accident prevention."*

Sits in **Title 3 — Accommodation, recreational facilities, food and catering**, which limb (a)
expressly names. The associated guidance addresses **internet access with reasonable charges**.

### 3.2 Appropriately-sized personal protective equipment — **Standard A4.3 §1(b)**

> *"reasonable precautions to prevent occupational accidents, injuries and diseases on board ship,
> including through the provision of all necessary **appropriately-sized personal protective
> equipment** and measures to reduce and prevent the risk of exposure to harmful levels of ambient
> factors and chemicals…"*

### 3.3 Controls take precedence over PPE — **Guideline B4.3.1 §3**

> *"…replacing the dangerous by the non-dangerous or the less dangerous, **have precedence over
> personal protective equipment** for seafarers."*

Read with Standard A4.3 §2, which requires account of **engineering and design control, substitution
of processes and procedures**, and then PPE. **This is the hierarchy of controls, now express in the
Convention** — a strong, examinable point for an Engineering Management paper, and one the donor
cannot make.

### 3.4 Deaths reported to the ILO for a global register — **Standard A4.3 §5(a)**

> *"all **deaths** of seafarers employed, engaged or working on board ships that fly its flag are
> adequately investigated and recorded, and reported on an **annual basis to the Director-General of
> the International Labour Office to be published in a global register**."*

### 3.5 Registered owner on the financial security certificate — **Appendix A4-I(g)**

> *"name of the shipowner, **or of the registered owner if different from the shipowner**"*

Among the particulars the certificate of financial security for shipowner liability must carry,
alongside the period of validity and the provider's attestation.
