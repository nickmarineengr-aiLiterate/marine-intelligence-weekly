**3 December 2025 falls roughly seven weeks AFTER the October 2025 examination.**

> **Therefore A.1187(33) was still the current list at this sitting. Citing A.1208(34) would import
> law that did not exist for the candidate.** The A.1208(34) list expressly gathers requirements
> entering into force **by 1 July 2026** — none of which existed for anyone sitting in October 2025.

**A.1208(34) appears on no candidate-facing surface as a current instrument.** It is named in the
Study Guide and in a `B_CURRENCY_CHECK` guard, in both cases as the thing **not** to substitute,
precisely so that a future maintainer checking currency in 2026 does not "correct" the citation.

This is the mirror-image of an ordinary staleness error, and it is the harder direction to catch,
because the wrong answer is the one that looks up to date.
