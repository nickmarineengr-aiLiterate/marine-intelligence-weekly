- Whether the ISM Code contains a defined term 'effective communication'. It does not define it as a term of art. The answer says so explicitly rather than inventing a definition and attributing it to the Code, and then explains the concept the Code's provisions collectively create.
- Whether ERM and BRM are the same thing. The principles are shared - situational awareness, workload management, closed-loop communication, challenge and response, briefing - but the setting differs materially. The engine room has higher noise, more remote and unattended operation, more contractor interface and a different watch structure. The answer notes the shared origin and then writes to the engine room.

It means everyone outside the engineering team - shore technical management, the designated person, riding squads and contractors, terminal and port staff, class surveyors and port State officers, tug and pilot during manoeuvring.
