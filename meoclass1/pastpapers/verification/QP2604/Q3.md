## Left open

- 'Artificial general average' is practitioner terminology for allowances the numbered York-Antwerp Rules permit on a common-benefit basis although they do not satisfy the strict common-safety test in Rule A. It is not a term defined in the Rules themselves, and the answer says so rather than presenting it as a defined category.
- Which edition of the York-Antwerp Rules governs a given casualty depends on the incorporating contract. The answer states the position rather than asserting that YAR 2016 applies universally.
