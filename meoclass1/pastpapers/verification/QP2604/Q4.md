## Verification

### The closest non-exact pair in the whole five-paper set

Normalised similarity **0.9946** against QP2601 Q4, across exactly **two** change blocks, both
purely typographic: April inserts a colon after `with reference to` and prints `(16)`.

**There is no semantic difference between the two stems.**

It is nevertheless classified **NEAR**, because the March precedent defines EXACT as string
equality *including printed mark allocations*, and these strings are not equal. This is a new
sub-class for the register - **semantically identical, typographically distinct** - and it is
treated for reuse purposes exactly as an exact repeat, because it is beyond argument the same
examinable question.

### The real delta is in the answer, not the question

The sitting date moved across a commencement.

- January 2026 was sat **before** the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025 commenced on 15 March 2026.
- April 2026 was sat **after** it.

The January study guide stated that India gave the CLC and Fund regimes domestic effect through the
**Merchant Shipping Act, 1958** *"at the date of this paper - January 2026"*. **That sentence is
false for April** and was re-anchored on the 2025 Act, using the commencement facts read in the
Gazette during the QP2607 work (assent 18 August 2025; whole Act in force 15 March 2026 by
S.O. 1244(E); 1958 Act repealed by s.324(1)).

**It was found by the mandatory sweep, not by reading the question.** A reader comparing only the
stems would have concluded that nothing needed to change.

### Unchanged

CLC, Fund, Bunkers, LLMC and Nairobi Wreck Removal positions, and the Marine Insurance Act 1963
propositions.

## Left open

- The question does not name the coastal state, so no claim is made about which conventions that state has ratified or whether it has extended the Nairobi Convention to its territorial sea. The answer states the mechanism and flags the dependency.
- Whether cover actually responds depends on the cause of the tank explosion, which the question does not give. The answer treats the cause as the central unknown rather than assuming the owner is covered.
