## Question as printed

> MARPOL is considered as one of the most flexible and efficient international environmental treaties currently in force. The adoption of a new amendment is enumerated in Article 16 of the MARPOL. This article establishes multiple ways for amending the convention, depending on what part of MARPOL is being amended. Kindly discuss the various ways in which the MARPOL convention may be amended. (16)

## Printed marks

The paper prints (16) against the whole question and sets no limbs. The question nevertheless contains an explicit organising instruction - 'depending on what part of MARPOL is being amended' - so the answer is structured by the part amended, which is how Article 16 itself is drafted.

---

## Verification

### What was actually read

The whole of **MARPOL Article 16**, paragraphs (1) to (9), in the text of the Convention -
including every sub-paragraph of 16(2)(f) and 16(2)(g). This was not taken from a restatement,
and that mattered: published summaries of this article are frequently loose.

### What reading it at source changed

Three claims would have been wrong if written from search results.

1. Several summaries give the objection threshold for a tacit amendment as *two thirds*. The
   article says **not less than one third of the Parties, or Parties holding not less than 50 per
   cent of world gross tonnage, whichever condition is fulfilled** - 16(2)(f)(iii).
2. The acceptance test for an **article** amendment is **conjunctive** - two thirds of Parties
   *and* those Parties holding at least 50 per cent of tonnage - 16(2)(f)(i). The objection test
   is **disjunctive**. Blurring the two is the commonest error in this topic.
3. Tacit acceptance is drafted at **(f)(iii) for appendices to Annexes**; Annexes reach it by the
   cross-reference in **(f)(ii)**, which also carries an express-approval reservation. Saying
   "Annexes are amended by tacit acceptance" is true but loses the structure the article is
   built on, and loses the reservation entirely.

### Deliberately not asserted

MIW holds no licensed IMO consolidated edition of MARPOL. Article 16 is an original article of
the 1973 Convention and no amendment to it was found, but that negative is **not** asserted as
verified. Recorded as `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION`.

The commonly quoted "eighteen months to two years" from adoption to entry into force is observed
practice, not a provision. The answer derives the **sixteen-month floor** arithmetically from the
article's own ten-month minimum plus six months, and presents the wider range as practice.

## Sources relied on

- PRIMARY: MARPOL, Article 16 (Amendments), paragraphs (1) to (9) - full text read. 16(1) the Convention may be amended by any of the procedures specified in the article; 16(2) amendments after consideration by the Organization; 16(3) amendment by a Conference; 16(4) Optional Annexes; 16(5) adoption of a new annex; 16(6) structural amendments; 16(7) consistency requirement; 16(8) notification of amendments in force; 16(9) declarations in writing to the Secretary-General.
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(2)(a) to (e) - a Party proposes; the Secretary-General circulates to all Members of the Organization and all Parties at least six months before consideration; the proposal goes to an appropriate body; Parties may participate whether or not they are Members of the Organization; adoption is by a two-thirds majority of only the Parties present and voting; the adopted text is communicated to all Parties for acceptance.
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(2)(f)(i) - an amendment to an ARTICLE is deemed accepted on the date it is accepted by two thirds of the Parties, the combined merchant fleets of which constitute not less than 50 per cent of the gross tonnage of the world's merchant fleet. Both conditions must be met.
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(2)(f)(ii) - an amendment to an ANNEX is deemed accepted in accordance with the procedure in (f)(iii) UNLESS the appropriate body determines at adoption that the (f)(i) explicit procedure applies; and at any time before entry into force a Party may notify the Secretary-General that its express approval is necessary before the amendment enters into force for it.
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(2)(f)(iii) - an amendment to an APPENDIX to an Annex is deemed accepted at the end of a period determined by the appropriate body at adoption, which shall be NOT LESS THAN TEN MONTHS, unless within that period an objection is communicated by not less than one third of the Parties OR by Parties whose combined merchant fleets constitute not less than 50 per cent of world gross tonnage, whichever condition is fulfilled.
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(2)(f)(iv) and (v) - Protocol I follows the Annex procedures; Protocol II follows the article procedure.
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(2)(g)(i) and (ii) - entry into force six months after acceptance; under the explicit route it binds only Parties that declared acceptance, and under the tacit route it binds ALL Parties except those which declared before that date that they do not accept it or that their express approval is necessary.
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(3) - on the request of a Party concurred in by at least one third of the Parties, the Organization convenes a Conference of Parties; amendments are adopted there by a two-thirds majority of the Parties present and voting; and unless the Conference decides otherwise, acceptance and entry into force follow 16(2)(f) and (g).
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(4) - for an Optional Annex, 'Party to the Convention' means a Party bound by that Annex; a Party which has declined an amendment to an Annex is treated as a non-Party only for the purpose of applying that amendment.
- PRIMARY: MARPOL Article 16(5), (6) and (7) - a NEW ANNEX is adopted and enters into force by the same procedure as an amendment to an ARTICLE; unless expressly provided otherwise, an amendment relating to the STRUCTURE of a ship applies only to ships contracted, or whose keel is laid in the absence of a contract, on or after the date the amendment comes into force; and an amendment to a Protocol or Annex must relate to the substance of that Protocol or Annex and be consistent with the articles.
