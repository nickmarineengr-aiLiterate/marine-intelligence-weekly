## 5. ANSWER ROUTE

Archetype: `explain`. The principal headings of the model answer ARE this route.

1. **How marine insurance divides**
   - Four insurable subject matters: ship, cargo, freight and liability
   - The first three are PROPERTY risks - capped by an agreed or insurable value
   - Liability is unlimited in principle, so it is handled by mutual pooling, not by premium
   - Marine Insurance Act 1963 (India) governs the contract; s.6 requires insurable interest
   - A contract without insurable interest is a wager and void
2. **Hull and Machinery**
   - Taken by the owner, or by a demise charterer, on the ship and her machinery
   - Institute Time Clauses (Hulls) are the usual market wording; time or voyage policy
   - Covers perils of the sea, fire, piracy, jettison, negligence of master and crew
   - Inchmaree clause extends to latent defect and negligence of repairers
   - Carries the 3/4ths collision liability; the remaining 1/4 falls to the P&I club
   - Excludes wear and tear, inherent vice, want of due diligence by the assured, war and strikes unless written back
3. **Cargo insurance**
   - Taken by the shipper, consignee or whoever bears risk under the sale contract
   - Institute Cargo Clauses A (all risks), B and C (named perils, descending)
   - Usually a valued policy on CIF plus a percentage
   - Excludes insufficient packing, inherent vice, ordinary leakage, wilful misconduct, delay
   - Insurer takes subrogated rights against the carrier on payment
4. **Freight, and loss of hire**
   - Freight at risk is insurable where it is payable only on delivery
   - Loss of hire covers the earnings lost while the ship is repaired after an insured casualty
   - Both are consequential-earnings covers, subject to a deductible in days
5. **Protection and Indemnity**
   - Mutual, not commercial - members are the assureds and contribute by calls
   - Covers third-party liabilities H&M does not: crew injury and death, passengers, pollution, wreck removal, cargo claims against the ship, fines, the 1/4th collision liability
   - Pay-to-be-paid rule; cover follows the club rules, not a policy schedule
   - Backed by the International Group pool and its reinsurance
6. **The exclusions that run across every policy**
   - Wilful misconduct of the assured - s.55, Marine Insurance Act 1963
   - Ordinary wear and tear, ordinary leakage and breakage, inherent vice
   - Delay, even where the delay is caused by an insured peril
   - Unseaworthiness and want of due diligence, where the assured is privy to it
   - War, strikes, terrorism and nuclear risks - excluded, then written back separately
   - Breach of warranty, and non-disclosure under the duty of utmost good faith
7. **The agencies in India which deal with marine insurance**
   - IRDAI - the statutory regulator, under the IRDA Act 1999
   - The public sector general insurers - New India Assurance, United India, National, Oriental
   - GIC Re - the national reinsurer
   - Private general insurers licensed by IRDAI
   - Indian owners take P&I from the International Group clubs, not from a domestic mutual
   - General Insurance Council; average adjusters, and salvage and cargo surveyors
