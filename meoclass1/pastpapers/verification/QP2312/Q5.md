2. **What a safety management system is, and why the Code works through one** _(a))_ - A structured and documented system enabling Company personnel to implement the policy; The Code sets objectives and requires a system; it does not write the procedures; Without the system the Code has nothing to inspect
3. **The six functional requirements** _(a))_ - Policy; instructions and procedures; authority and lines of communication; Reporting of accidents and non-conformities; emergency preparedness; Internal audits and management reviews - improvement appears already at this level
4. **The components** _(a))_ - Part A elements 1 to 12, from policy through to company verification and review; Part B elements 13 to 16 - the certification layer; Document of Compliance to the Company, Safety Management Certificate to the ship
5. **The role the system plays in implementing the Code** _(a))_ - Converts objectives into procedures a named person performs; Creates the shore-to-ship link through the Designated Person; Preserves the Master's overriding authority and generates the evidence for audit
6. **Continuous improvement - the mechanism, and why it is the significant part** _(a))_ - Element 9 reporting, investigation, analysis and corrective action; Element 12 internal audit at intervals not exceeding twelve months, and evaluation of effectiveness; It makes the system self-correcting rather than merely documented
7. **Why the Code exists, and the shift it represents** _(b))_ - A major ro-ro passenger casualty and an inquiry that found the fault ashore as much as aboard; From regulating hardware to regulating management; From inspection of the ship to an auditable system, and from the ship to the Company
8. **The evolution - five stages** _(b))_ - From Assembly recommendation to mandatory instrument through SOLAS chapter IX; The amendment chain to MSC.353(92); technology absorbed through elements 6, 7 and 10 without amendment; Cyber risk required by MSC.428(98) inside the existing machinery; environmental instruments sit outside in MARPOL Annex VI
