**Answer route:**

1. **What goal-based standards are, and the problem they solve** _(a))_ - Prescriptive rules state the means and freeze yesterday's design; Goal-based rules state the outcome and the duration; The regulator moves from writing rules to auditing them
2. **Where the requirement bites** _(a))_ - SOLAS II-1/3-10 - bulk carriers and oil tankers 150 m and above; Ore carriers and combination carriers excluded; Contract on or after 1 July 2016, keel 1 July 2017, delivery 1 July 2020
3. **The tier structure - and how many tiers the instrument contains** _(a))_ - Framework described in five tiers; The adopted standards contain Tiers I to III only; Tier IV is class and national rules; Tier V is industry practice
4. **Tier I and Tier II - the goal, and what it demands** _(a))_ - Safe and environmentally friendly, for a specified design life, throughout life; Recycling, safe access, escape, inspection and maintenance are inside the goal; Fifteen functional requirements in four lifecycle groups; 25 years, North Atlantic
5. **Why this enhances safety and environmental protection** _(a))_ - Whole-life goal forces fatigue, corrosion margin and residual strength into the design; Accessibility written into the goal, because unreachable structure fails quietly; Corrosion control is pollution control; recycling reaches the end of life at design
6. **Tier III - how conformity is verified** _(b))_ - Rules of an organization recognized under SOLAS XI-1/1, or equivalent national standards; Verification under the Revised guidelines, MSC.454(100); Audit team appointed by the Secretary-General reports to MSC; verification is maintained, not granted once
7. **Who does what - the stakeholders** _(b))_ - IMO owns Tiers I to III; the Administration recognizes and enforces; Class writes and maintains the Tier IV rules; designers design to them; Builders deliver construction quality and the File; owners keep the assumptions true
8. **The Ship Construction File - the part that survives delivery** _(b))_ - Required by paragraph 4, provided on delivery; Kept on board and/or ashore and updated through service; Content per MSC.1/Circ.1343 - it carries the design assumptions to the people who maintain the ship
