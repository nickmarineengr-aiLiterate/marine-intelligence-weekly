2. **The driver, as it stands at this sitting** _(a))_ - The 2023 strategy was adopted in July of this year and sets the trajectory - it is policy, not law; EEXI and the operational carbon intensity indicator have applied since 1 January; this is the first data year; No market-based measure and no carbon price has been adopted
3. **Where the mandatory training framework reaches - and where it does not** _(a))_ - STCW V/3 and A-V/3 cover ships subject to the IGF Code; Methanol runs on interim guidelines; ammonia and hydrogen have none adopted at this date; The obligation is thinnest where the hazard is newest, so training is company-led
4. **The anticipated areas of concern - fuel by fuel** _(a))_ - Ammonia - toxicity governs, and it is not zero-emission; Methanol - invisible flame, alcohol-resistant foam; hydrogen - embrittlement and wide flammability; Batteries - thermal runaway and high voltage; LNG - cryogenic and boil-off; biofuels, wind assist, shore power
5. **The competences that are not about fuel at all** _(a))_ - Efficiency operation and data integrity as a compliance obligation; Risk assessment where there is no fleet experience to draw on; Emergency response written for the new hazard, and leading a crew through the change
6. **Why communication is a causal factor, not a contributory nicety** _(b))_ - The information existed on board and did not reach the decision; Vertical, horizontal, handover and ship-to-shore failure shapes; Each is a link in the chain, not background
7. **What the instruments actually require** _(b))_ - ISM 1.4.2 lines of authority; 6.6 working language understood; 6.7 able to communicate effectively; SOLAS V/14 - working language recorded in the log book, English on the bridge; STCW A-VIII/2 resource management, and the Standard Marine Communication Phrases
8. **Why present-day crewing makes this harder** _(b))_ - Multinational crews - declared is not shared; turnover erodes informal knowledge; Power distance suppresses the challenge; reduced manning removes the overlap; fatigue degrades read-back first; And unfamiliar plant raises the cost of an ambiguous message - the two limbs meet here
