## Why the temporal delta really is nil

The donor's own freshness assessment says it in terms: *nothing in this answer turns on a recent
amendment or on a date*. Re-checked and agreed. The question is about:

- **the order in which people are told** — Company, class, insurer — and the fact that a Chief
  Engineer **establishes facts and recommends** while the **Company decides** and **class approves**;
- **NDT method selection**, which is metallurgy: propeller alloys are **non-ferromagnetic**, so
  **magnetic particle inspection is not applicable**; **liquid dye penetrant** is the standard surface
  method, with **eddy current or ultrasonic** techniques where crack **depth** must be established;
- **failure mechanisms** — fatigue, cavitation erosion, impact from debris or ice, **dezincification**
  of manganese bronze, **galvanic attack** where anodes or the impressed current system are defective,
  and sustained overload from heavy running or excessive astern working.

None of that has an edition. None of the three regressions this paper's anchor warns about is
engaged — **checked, not assumed**.

## What was added for this paper

The pairing is exact in kind and no other paper in the corpus prints both:

- **Q5 is the *planned* examination** — a survey programme agreed *before* the dock, close-up survey
  within arm's reach, thickness measurement by a firm **approved by the society**.
- **This is the *unplanned* finding** — the thing nobody put in the docking specification.

What they share is the answer to *who may do what*: in both, the examination that counts must be
carried out by a **competent person the society recognises**, and in both the Chief Engineer's job is
to **establish and preserve the facts**. On Q5 that means not gauging the plate yourself; here it
means **not grinding out the crack before the surveyor has seen it**.
