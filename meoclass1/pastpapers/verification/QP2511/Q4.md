## What was added for this paper

A study-notes block linking this question to **`QP2511 Q2`**, the propeller-curve and torque-rich
question on the same paper. The connection is mechanistic rather than decorative:

Limbs (a) to (c) all exist to control the **rate at which gas is generated and disposed of** — the
bunkering operation is paced by **vapour**, not by liquid. The same logic governs a **gas-fuelled main
engine in service**: a fouled hull or heavy weather drives the engine into the **torque-rich** region,
demanding more fuel at lower speed, and on a gas engine that demand runs into the **fuel-gas supply
system's** pressure and temperature limits — with **knock and misfire** as the symptoms rather than
exhaust temperature and smoke. In both cases the **fuel system, not the fuel tank, is the constraint**.

No other paper in the corpus prints both questions, so no donor could have carried this.
