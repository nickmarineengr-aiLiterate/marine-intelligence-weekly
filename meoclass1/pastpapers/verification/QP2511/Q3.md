## The three deltas

**1. Question delta. NEAR — and this is where the work is.**

This sitting prints **three** demands; the donor prints **four**. The compression is not cosmetic:

| | Donor (Jan 2026) | **This sitting** |
|---|---|---|
| certificate limb | *"the different certificates issued to a vessel **for maintenance of the ship in good health**"* | *"the different certificates **in India**"* |
| issuing limb | *"**Who can issue these certificates in India**"* — a separate demand | folded into the above |

Two consequences follow, and **neither could be inherited**:

- **The qualifier confining the limb to the *ship* has been deleted**, while this stem's own first
  sentence expressly names *"the ship **and the crew** in good health"*. The donor's own
  `decomposition_gate` records this exact ambiguity and resolves it as ship-only — **because of the
  qualifier this paper does not print**. On this stem the crew's certificate is inside the task.
- **"validity and extension options"** is plural, and on this stem it has **two** genuine answers.

**2. Marks delta. NIL in total, real in weighting.** Neither sitting prints an allocation; both are
recorded at 16 under instruction 2. But three demands share the marks rather than four, so
`mark_allocation` was **re-derived**: framework and WHO 5, national agencies 3, and the merged
certificate limb **8** (≈6 ship, ≈2 crew).

**3. Temporal delta. Effectively nil — and confirmed by sweep, not assumed.**

The governing amendment runs the *helpful* way. The **IHR 2024 amendments entered into force on
19 September 2025**, which is **before both sittings**. So the donor's currency point — that Article 37
is now the **Ship** Declaration of Health, not the **Maritime** Declaration of Health — **carries
forward rather than reversing**.

The one figure altered is the interval: the donor says four months; here it is **two**. Restated
rather than deleted, because the shorter interval makes the point *stronger* — at this sitting the
renaming was eight weeks old and every textbook in circulation still carried the superseded name.

## The anchor's predicted risk — checked, and absent

`QP2511_TEMPORAL_AND_DONOR_ANCHOR.md` §3 recorded the Merchant Shipping Act boundary as *"available
on Q3 (Indian health certificates and issuing authority)"*.

**The donor object contains zero occurrences of "Merchant Shipping" in any field.** It attributes the
Indian ship-health function to the **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare**, **DGHS** and the **Port
Health Organisation** under the **Indian Port Health Rules 1955** — none of which any Merchant
Shipping Act touches. **There was no regression to reverse.**

That is now the **second** anchor prediction on this paper investigated and cleared (Q5 was the
first), against one confirmed and reversed (Q8). The predictions were questions, and answering them
either way is the work.

The Act is nevertheless named **once**, deliberately, in the **new** crew-certificate material — where
the operative statute genuinely matters, and where it is correctly given as the **Merchant Shipping
Act 1958**.

## What was authored fresh, and verified this session

A **sixth route step** and a matching model-answer section on the **seafarer's medical certificate**,
because the compressed stem brings it inside the printed task.

**Primary source read this session:** the ILO's own published FAQ on the Maritime Labour Convention,
downloaded as PDF from `ilo.org` and read directly (the text layer was extracted locally; the
`normlex` consolidated text returns HTTP 403 to automated fetch). It restates **Standard A1.2**
paragraph by paragraph:

| Standard A1.2 | Established |
|---|---|
| ¶3 | A medical certificate issued under **STCW** shall be accepted by the competent authority for Regulation 1.2 purposes. |
| ¶4 | Issued by a **duly qualified medical practitioner**; for a certificate **solely concerning eyesight**, by a person recognized by the competent authority. |
| ¶7 | Valid a maximum of **two years**; **one year** if the seafarer is **under 18**; a certification of **colour vision**, a maximum of **six years**. These are maxima — a shorter period may be required by the duties concerned or under STCW. |
| ¶8 | In **urgent cases** the competent authority may permit work on a **recently expired** certificate for a limited period. |
| ¶9 | A certificate **expiring in the course of a voyage** **continues in force** to the next port of call where one can be obtained from a qualified medical practitioner, **not exceeding three months**. |

**This is why the addition earns its place.** The printed word is *"options"*, plural, and the answer
now has two contrasting pairs:

| | Validity | Extension | Authority in India |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ship** | 6 months | **+1 month** — to reach a port where inspection can be done | **Port Health Officer** (MoHFW → DGHS → PHO), IHR + Port Health Rules 1955 |
| **Crew** | 2 years (1 if under 18; colour vision 6) | **+3 months** — to the next port where a practitioner is available | **DG Shipping approved medical examiner**, under the **Merchant Shipping Act 1958** |

Both extensions exist for the same practical reason — the document expires while the ship is
somewhere the issuer is not — and both are capped. That symmetry is the teaching point, and no donor
could have supplied it because no donor's stem asked for it.

## Reasoning recorded

- **The ship's certificates are answered first and at greater length.** The wider reading is taken,
  but deliberately hedged in structure rather than in prose: a marker taking the *narrower* reading
  loses nothing, because the ship material is complete and comes first. The choice and its reason are
  stated in `unresolved` rather than hidden.
- **Health ministry for the hull, shipping administration for the man.** A dedicated study-notes
  block, because putting DG Shipping in charge of ship sanitation certificates is the error that
  costs most marks on the India limb — and the widened limb makes it *easier* to fall into, since
  both authorities now appear in one answer.
- **No Indian port list is reproduced.** It is notified by the State Party to WHO and changes.
- **`recurrence_class`** set to `near_recurrence` (an authoring field; never candidate-facing).
