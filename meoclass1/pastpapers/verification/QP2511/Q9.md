## Reasoning recorded

- **The two named terms get their own heading.** The examiner numbers them (i) and (ii); an answer
  that defines them in passing inside a narrative will not be credited for them.
- **They are contrasted, not merely defined** — accession is one step, signature-subject-to-
  ratification is two, and both reach the same destination.
- **Convention entry into force and amendment entry into force are kept separate.** Threshold plus
  waiting period for the first; tacit acceptance for the second. The stem asks about both.
- **A seventh step was added** that the donor's route does not have: *adoption is not automatic
  either*. The donor could not make this point as a completed fact; this sitting can.
- **The Hong Kong condition is stated as a TRIPLE condition**, not assimilated to the SOLAS double
  condition. Getting this wrong would also damage Q8.
