## Reasoning recorded

- **Limb (a) is the discriminator and it is conceptual, not definitional.** *ESP is a floor; CAP is a
  grade.* Every other difference — mandatory vs voluntary, flag-driven vs owner-driven, certificate
  consequence vs commercial consequence — follows from that one idea, so the route establishes it
  first and the comparison table then makes the consequences visible at a glance.
- **Limb (b) is split into planning documents and history records.** That is how the survey programme
  itself is organised, and it is far easier to write completely under examination pressure than a
  single undifferentiated list.
- **CAP is not CAS.** Kept as the stated major trap. A candidate who answers limb (a) by describing
  the MARPOL Annex I scheme has not merely answered a different question — he has said the thing is
  *mandatory* when the question is precisely about a *voluntary* one.
- **No tonnage threshold is asserted.** Secondary sources state ESP application thresholds
  inconsistently, so the answer names the ship types and quotes no figure. An unverifiable quantity
  is omitted, not quoted (`PASTPAPER_PRODUCTION_PROTOCOL.md` §2.1).
