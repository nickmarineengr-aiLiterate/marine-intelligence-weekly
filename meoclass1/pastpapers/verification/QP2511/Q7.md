## A correction carried forward from the research

IMO's own *"Amendments to IMO instruments"* summary page describes `FAL.15(47)` as concerning
**mandatory garbage record books for smaller ships**. **That is a MARPOL Annex V item, not FAL.**

The **primary resolution text was read in full** and governs: `FAL.15(47)` amends **Recommended
Practice 7.11**. This answer is authored from the resolution, **not** from the summary page. The
correction was first recorded in the checkpoint and is now carried into the object's
`temporal_review` so it cannot be re-introduced by a later session working from the summary.

## Reasoning recorded

- **The stem names the two lenses, so each gets its own heading.** *"particularly in relation to
  digitalization and sustainability"* — an answer discussing "modernisation" generally has not done
  what was asked.
- **The duty-holder is stated explicitly and repeatedly.** Standard 1.3quin obliges **public
  authorities**, not the ship. This is the most commonly inverted proposition in the topic, and it is
  also what explains the honest part of the analysis: the ship's benefit is **derivative** of the
  State having implemented, which is why experience is so uneven port to port.
- **The sustainability argument runs through TIME, not paper.** Reliable pre-arrival data enables
  **just-in-time arrival**; fuel burn falls steeply with speed, so slowing on passage beats steaming
  hard and idling at anchor. Less paper is real but trivial and is given one line.
- **The command verb is `analyze`, so the limits are stated.** A single window that wraps the same
  paper documents in a web form delivers compliance and no benefit; just-in-time needs the
  **charterparty** to permit it, because a conventional laytime regime rewards arriving and tendering
  notice rather than arriving efficiently. **FAL removes the administrative obstacle; the commercial
  obstacle is untouched.**
- **No saving percentage is quoted.** Published estimates for port-call optimisation vary widely with
  trade, ship type and port; none could be verified. The mechanism is explained instead — an
  unverifiable quantity is omitted, not quoted (`PASTPAPER_PRODUCTION_PROTOCOL.md` §2.1).
- **Nothing is asserted about India's national single window.** The question sets no Indian limb and
  none was verified. The paper's Merchant Shipping Act boundary does not arise here.

  the hardest to verify exhaustively; flagged for confirmation before publication.

  established and not asserted.
