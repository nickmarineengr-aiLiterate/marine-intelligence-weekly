# EM2607 — Q9 verification record

**Question (verbatim).** Explain the principle of "Uberrimae Fidei" in Marine Insurance. What are the
different disclosures which must be made by the insured to the insurer in Marine Insurance? List the
circumstances which need not be disclosed to the insurer. What happens to the Marine Insurance
contract if the disclosures are not made to the insurer at the time of the conclusion of the
contract? (16)

## 0. Jurisdiction determination — done FIRST, per the brief

This is the paper's jurisdiction trap, and it was resolved before any drafting.

The examination is Indian (M.E.O. Class I, India 2026). The governing statute is therefore the
**Marine Insurance Act, 1963**, not the English Marine Insurance Act 1906. The Indian Act was modelled
closely on the 1906 Act, so the *structure* is familiar, but the *citations* must be Indian.

**Internal content was rejected on this ground.** `meoclass1/QB9_C.html` states that the six
principles of marine insurance are "defined under the Marine Insurance Act 1906". That is the English
statute. Reusing it would have produced a confidently-cited wrong answer. `meoclass1/QB9_E.html`
handles it correctly, naming "Marine Insurance Act, 1906 (UK) / Indian Marine Insurance Act, 1963".
All statutory citations in this answer were re-derived from the Indian Act.

**The English Insurance Act 2015 is expressly NOT applied.** It reformed English law by replacing
avoidance with a duty of fair presentation and proportionate remedies. It did not amend the Indian
Act. It appears in the study notes as a labelled contrast only.
