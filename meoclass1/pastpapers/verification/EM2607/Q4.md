# EM2607 — Q4 verification record

**Question (verbatim).** As the Chief Engineer of a large merchant ship, you are about to enter an
Emission Control Area. Explain, with justification, the instructions you need to give to your
colleagues with respect to the procedures to be followed regarding fuel changeover, considering the
boilers and engines. Enumerate the statutes involved, if any, in this regard. (16)

## 2. Statement-type separation

- **Legal requirement:** MARPOL Annex VI regs 14, 14.6, 14.7, 18.
- **Regional overlay:** named as a category only — explicitly distinguished from MARPOL.
- **Maker-specific procedure:** rate of temperature change, minimum injector viscosity, burner tip
  selection. Stated as maker limits, never as regulation.
- **Engineering judgement:** thermal shock, lubricity, atomisation and compatibility reasoning.
