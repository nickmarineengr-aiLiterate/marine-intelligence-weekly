## 4. The two flagged claims, and why they are flagged rather than dropped

1. **The guidelines edition.** `MSC-MEPC.2/Circ.12/Rev.2` is **not held** by the corpus and was
   not read. A later revision would change the edition an answer should name, though not the
   substance of the five-step model. Flagged `B_CURRENCY_CHECK`.

2. **The US$3 million criterion.** This is genuinely examinable — a candidate asked about cost
   effective indices who cannot say what they are compared against has not answered the term. It
   is also genuinely perishable: the criterion is derived from societal indicators and updated
   for inflation, and its stated value has moved across revisions of the Guidelines.

   The resolution taken is the same one used for the LLMC figures on Q2: **state the order of
   magnitude, say in the answer itself that it is updated for inflation, and flag it.** The
   derived layers follow — `critical_numbers` reads *"of the order of US$3 million per averted
   fatality, updated for inflation — an order of magnitude, not a fixed figure"*, and card C3
   avoids the number entirely. No derived layer hardens it.

**One further claim was deliberately weakened rather than asserted.** The *cost of averting a
tonne of oil spilled* is described as **proposed** and expressly **not** an adopted IMO criterion,
because it is widely quoted in industry material as though it were one.
