### 3.1 The exclusion, stated as a decision

At January 2025 **there was no approved draft chapter 5 text**. The GHG Fuel Intensity mechanism,
its tiers, and any remedial unit price were three months in the future at approval and nine
months in the future at adoption. Any such content on this paper would describe a document that
did not exist.

It is therefore **absent by decision, not by oversight**, and the answer's uncertainty section
says so in terms — so that a reader who has seen a later MIW answer understands why this one
stops where it does.

### 3.2 The superseded-edition trap

`MEPC.376(80)` is the resolution most MIW material cites, because `MEPC.377(80)` ¶3.2 names it
directly. At this sitting it is **superseded**. An answer that presents the 2023 edition as
current is describing the position as it stood nine months before the paper. The answer names the
**2024 edition as current** and the 2023 edition **as its predecessor**.

### 3.3 The regional/global trap

The only *binding* well-to-wake regime in force at this sitting is **European**, not IMO. The IMO
position is **a strategy plus guidelines**, neither of which is mandatory on a ship. Both halves
of that are stated explicitly, in the answer body, in `major_trap` and in card C5.
