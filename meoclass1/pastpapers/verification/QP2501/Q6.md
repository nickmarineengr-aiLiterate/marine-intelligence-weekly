## 3. Temporal verification — January 2025: the four-statute problem

This question sits in the middle of the 2025 Indian legislative wave. Four statutes surrounding
it were replaced during 2025, and **every replacement is after this sitting**:

| Statute | Status at January 2025 | The 2025 position |
|---|---|---|
| **Merchant Shipping Act, 1958** | **OPERATIVE** | Replaced by Act No. 24 of 2025, assent 18 Aug 2025, commenced 15 Mar 2026 |
| **Carriage of Goods by Sea Act, 1925** | **OPERATIVE** | Replaced during 2025 |
| **Indian Bills of Lading Act, 1856** | **OPERATIVE** | Replaced during 2025 |
| Coasting trade | governed by the 1958 Act | Coastal Shipping Act, 2025, assent 9 Aug 2025 |
| **Marine Insurance Act, 1963** | **OPERATIVE** — not replaced | — |
| **Indian Contract Act, 1872** | **OPERATIVE** | — |
| **Multimodal Transportation of Goods Act, 1993** | **OPERATIVE** | — |

**None of the 2025 replacements is named, described or cited anywhere in this answer.** Recording
their existence is the anchor's job (§2.2), and it exists so that a later session pulling a 2026
answer backwards **reverses** the statute reference instead of inheriting it. The answer itself
tells the candidate the same thing in its uncertainty section, without naming instruments that
did not exist at the sitting.

**Convention currency also checked.** CLC 1992, Bunkers 2001 and Nairobi 2007 were all in force
at this sitting and are used as the strict-liability examples. **The HNS Convention 2010 is
deliberately absent** — it was not in force at this sitting, and its later ratification
milestones are all after it. Citing HNS as operative is the standard currency error on this
topic.
