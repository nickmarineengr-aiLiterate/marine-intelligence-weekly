## 4. The printed hailstorm — adjudicated, not corrected

The stem places a post-Panamax container ship *"overtaken by a hailstorm"* and asks for the
effects **under parametric rolling**.

**The technical difficulty is real.** A hailstorm is a convective squall of short duration. It
does not by itself raise a seaway of **ship-length wavelength**, and that wavelength is a
*necessary* condition for parametric roll. Taken literally the scenario does not produce the
phenomenon it names.

**Three responses were available:**

1. Silently rewrite it as "a storm" — **rejected**, because the playbook requires printed truth to
   be preserved and examiner errors never normalised into what the examiner "meant".
2. Answer that parametric rolling cannot occur — **rejected**, as pedantry that forfeits the
   question and misreads the examiner's evident intent.
3. **Reproduce the stem exactly, and adjudicate the physics in the open** — taken.

The answer reads the squall as the **sudden onset of severe weather** — wind, heavy precipitation,
poor visibility, a rapid rise in sea state — **superimposed on the swell already running**, and
says so. It then draws the distinction that makes the reading coherent: *the disturbance that
triggers the resonance comes from the squall; the resonance itself comes from the swell.* Card C9
carries the same point, so the derived layer does not flatten it.

The stem is reproduced verbatim in `text_verbatim`, including the omitted *to* in *"due the
condition"*.

## 5. The second piece of hidden content — the loaded condition

**1300 containers on a post-Panamax hull is a light, part-loaded condition, not a full ship.**
A candidate who does not notice writes a correct general answer and forfeits the reasoning marks,
which on a *justify* question are most of them. The consequences, all stated in §6 of the answer:

- reduced draught and displacement, large freeboard;
- much of the flare and transom above water at rest → **larger** proportional change in waterplane
  inertia per wave;
- **high GM → short natural roll period** → the resonance condition is reachable at *more* speed
  and heading combinations;
- less immersed hull and bilge keel → **lower damping**;
- large windage → wind heel and yaw in a squall, supplying the initial disturbance.

This is classed `ENGINEERING_JUDGEMENT` in the provenance ledger, because no source states it: it
is derived from the scenario.

## 7. Intake tag corrected

The intake spec tagged this question `subject_tags: ["IMSBC"]`. **That is wrong.** The IMSBC Code
governs *solid bulk cargoes*; this is a container-stack stability question and the IMSBC Code has
no application to it. Re-tagged to **`["Cargo Securing", "SOLAS"]`**.

Both values are **already in use elsewhere in the corpus**, so the closed vocabulary is respected
and no new term is invented — which is the standing rule for author-time tagging.

`primary_category` is left at **Cargo & Bulk Carriage**: the question's consequences are cargo
consequences, and re-categorising would move the question on the topic page for no gain.
