tier stays **C** and the relationship is recorded in `reuse_evidence` instead, where it informs
without misrepresenting.

2025. **The three papers stand on the same AFS text**, so nothing had to be re-based.

**What was deliberately not taken.** The donors' limb (b) — CDP, SPC, hybrid TBT-free and

**The cybutryne statement is the temporally load-bearing claim on this question, and it has
three wrong forms and one right one:**

- *"Cybutryne is not yet controlled"* — **wrong**, two years out of date at this sitting.
- *"Cybutryne is banned; no ship may have it"* — **wrong** for January 2025. Existing ships
  lawfully continue to carry systems applied before 1 January 2023 until the next scheduled
  renewal of the anti-fouling system, subject to the 60-month backstop.
- *"It will be banned"* — **wrong**; the control is in force.
- **Right:** the control is **in force**, application and re-application are prohibited, and
  **existing ships are inside a transition** discharged by removal or by an overcoating sealer.

The answer, `major_trap`, `critical_numbers` and card C3 all carry the transitional
qualification, so no derived layer hardens it into a flat prohibition.
