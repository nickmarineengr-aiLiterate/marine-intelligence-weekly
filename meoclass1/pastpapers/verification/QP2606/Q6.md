## Verification

### THE HONEST POSITION ON AUTHORITY

**This question sits largely outside the regulatory corpus, and no attempt has been made to disguise
that.**

The capital / voyage / operating cost taxonomy and every inventory-control technique in the answer -
ABC, VED, FSN, SDE, HML, the ABC-VED matrix, economic order quantity, reorder level, safety stock,
vendor-managed inventory, standardisation and fleet pooling - are **established shipping-economics
and operations-management practice**. They are **not the content of any IMO, statutory or class
instrument**. There is no primary source to cite, and none has been invented.

This is recorded as a `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION` rather than concealed, and it is the deliberate
application of the standing rule that **attaching irrelevant convention references to a management
technique is a worse error than admitting that none applies**.

### Where regulation genuinely does bear, and it is used only there

**ISM Code paragraph 10.3** - the Company must identify equipment and technical systems whose
**sudden operational failure may result in hazardous situations**, with specific measures promoting
their reliability including regular testing of standby arrangements.

This is the constraint that **rules out unqualified just-in-time for critical spares**, and it gives
the answer its sharpest point: the **Vital** class of a VED classification should be the same list
the Company is already required to identify under 10.3.

**MARPOL Annex VI regulations 25 (EEXI) and 28 (CII)** - in force, and the reason fuel consumption is
now a **compliance outcome** and not only a cost. Verified for Q4 on this paper and reused here.

### What was deliberately NOT claimed

- **No numerical cost proportions.** The share of operating cost taken by crew, or of voyage cost by
  bunkers, varies by ship type, trade, flag and market, and MIW holds no verified dataset. Any figure
  a candidate has seen is illustrative of one segment at one time.
- **No mandatory minimum spare-gear scale.** Whether any flag Administration or classification
  society mandates one was not established, so none is asserted.
