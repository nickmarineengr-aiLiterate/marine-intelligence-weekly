## Verification

### THE HIGHEST-RISK CLAIM ON THE PAPER, TESTED AND REJECTED

**The question's framing invites a false statement, and the answer does not make it.**

The IMO record of designated Particularly Sensitive Sea Areas was checked directly.
**Nineteen PSSAs have been designated worldwide and NONE of them is in India.** Neither the
Lakshadweep islands nor the Andaman and Nicobar Islands holds PSSA designation. Indian waters also
hold **no MARPOL Special Area and no Emission Control Area**.

The designations run from the **Great Barrier Reef (1990)** through the Sabana-Camagueey
Archipelago, Malpelo Island, the Florida Keys, the Wadden Sea, Paracas, Western European Waters, the
Torres Strait extension, the Canary Islands, the Galapagos, the Baltic Sea Area, Papahanaumokuakea,
the Strait of Bonifacio, the Saba Bank, the Coral Sea extension, the Jomard Entrance, the Tubbataha
Reefs, the north-western Mediterranean and Nusa Penida.

**The examiner wrote "could be protected", in the conditional.** The question is prospective, and
answering it prospectively is answering it correctly. Recorded as `B_CURRENCY_CHECK` because
designations are granted at MEPC sessions.

### The governing instrument

**Resolution A.982(24)**, Revised Guidelines for the Identification and Designation of Particularly
Sensitive Sea Areas, adopted 1 December 2005, **as amended by resolution MEPC.267(68)**. Citing
A.982(24) alone is incomplete.

Three criteria groups - **ecological**, **social, cultural and economic**, **scientific and
educational** - of which **at least one** must be met; **plus** a demonstration of **vulnerability to
damage by international shipping activities**, assessed on vessel traffic characteristics and
natural factors; **plus at least one Associated Protective Measure with an identified legal basis**
under an existing IMO instrument.

### The Indian statutory position at a post-15-March-2026 sitting

- **Ship-source pollution**: the **Merchant Shipping Act, 2025**, in force **15 March 2026**, which
  repealed the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958. The Gazette work done for QP2607 Q7 has now paid for
  itself a **fourth** time.
- **But the 2025 Act does not swallow the subject.** The islands' coastal-zone protection rests on
  the **Island Coastal Regulation Zone (ICRZ) Notification, 2019** made under **section 3 of the
  Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**, which **superseded the Island Protection Zone Notification,
  2011**; and habitat protection rests on the **Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972**. This is the
  QP2603 Q9 reasoning pattern - specialist legislation survives - applied again.

### An unresolved citation, recorded rather than guessed

**The exact Gazette citation and date of the ICRZ Notification, 2019 could not be settled.**
Published sources give both a notification dated 17 January 2019 and one dated 1 January 2021
bearing the 2019 title, which is consistent with a draft followed by a final notification. The
substance is consistently supported and is what the answer states. **The precise citation is not
