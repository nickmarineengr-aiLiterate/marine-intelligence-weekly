## Verification

### What was read, and the two temporal anchors

Both anchors were **re-established at the June sitting date rather than inherited**.

**1. The IMO Net-Zero Framework was APPROVED BUT NOT ADOPTED at this sitting.** The extraordinary
session of MEPC adjourned in October 2025 without agreement and was due to reconvene in **November
2026 - five months after this paper**. February's headline finding therefore holds unchanged, and
this is the fourth paper on which it bites. `B_CURRENCY_CHECK`.

**2. HTW 12 met 23 to 27 February 2026 - four months before this sitting** - and agreed **draft
interim training guidelines** for seafarers on ships using **methyl/ethyl alcohol** and on ships
using **ammonia** as fuel, prioritised further work on hydrogen fuel cells, LPG, hydrogen,
battery-powered ships and wind-assisted propulsion, carried forward the **comprehensive review of
the STCW Convention and Code**, and opened a **new workstream on fatigue and hours of work and
rest**.

### The structural finding

**There is presently a gap in the mandatory framework.** STCW regulation V/3 and Code section A-V/3
set mandatory basic and advanced training for seafarers on ships subject to the **IGF Code**.
**Ammonia, methanol, hydrogen and battery-electric propulsion are not covered by a mandatory STCW
competence standard.** IMO is bridging that gap with interim guidelines while the comprehensive
review runs. This is the observation that carries limb (a).

### Attribution corrected before drafting

The widely quoted figure of roughly **800,000 seafarers** requiring upskilling for ammonia is
**industry research** by Lloyd's Register's Maritime Decarbonisation Hub with the Maersk
Mc-Kinney Moller Center for Zero Carbon Shipping. **It is not an IMO figure**, and the answer
attributes it rather than adopting it.

### The homonym trap, avoided

**QP2601 Q2 and this question both contain "decarbonisation" and both contain "communication", and
they are not the same subject.** In QP2601 Q2 "decarbonisation" means **main engine
decarbonisation** - removing carbon deposits on the cylinder-head platform. Here it means the
energy transition. A keyword-driven reuse step would have merged a planned-maintenance question
with an energy-transition question. **Only the communication material was reused.**
