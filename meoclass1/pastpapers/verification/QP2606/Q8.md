## Verification

### What was actually read

**Resolution A.1207(34)**, *Survey Guidelines under the Harmonized System of Survey and Certification
(HSSC), 2025*, adopted **3 December 2025** (233 pages), read directly - the preamble, the revocation
clause, section 4 in full and annex 1.

### The superseded-edition finding

**A.1207(34) REVOKES resolution A.1186(33)**, the HSSC 2023 Guidelines, following successive
revocation of A.1156(32), A.1140(31), A.1120(30), A.1104(29), A.1076(28), A.1053(27), A.1020(26),
A.997(25), A.948(23) and A.746(18). It was adopted at the **same Assembly session as A.1206(34)**,
the Procedures for Port State Control used on Q2 of this paper, and both are **six months old at the
June sitting**. Most published material still cites A.1186(33).

### The load-bearing finding on limb (b) - the periodical survey has TWO frequencies

Taken verbatim from section 4.4.1:

> The periodical survey should be held **within three months before or after the second anniversary
> date or within three months before or after the third anniversary date in the case of the cargo
> ship safety equipment certificate**, and should **take the place of one of the annual surveys**;
> **in the case of the cargo ship safety radio certificate, it should be held within three months
> before or after each anniversary date.**

And from annex 1, the periodical survey attaches to **only two certificates** - the **Cargo Ship
Safety Equipment Certificate** and the **Cargo Ship Safety Radio Certificate**. The annual survey
attaches to Safety Equipment, Safety Construction and the International Load Line Certificate.

**Two certificates, two different frequencies.** This is the fact most answers miss.

### Scope, from sections 4.2.2 and 4.4.2

- **Annual** - a certificate examination, a **visual examination** of sufficient extent of the ship
  and its equipment, and certain tests confirming the condition is being properly maintained, plus a
  visual check that **no unapproved modifications** have been made.
- **Periodical** - an **inspection, with tests when necessary, of the equipment**, confirming
  compliance, satisfactory condition and fitness for the intended service, plus a check that
  certificates, record books, manuals and documentation are on board.

### One resolution number corrected before drafting

An initial search summary gave the RO Code as **MSC.349(90)**. It is **MSC.349(92)**, with
**MEPC.237(65)**, mandatory from 1 January 2015. Caught before it reached the answer.

### One thing deliberately NOT stated

**No count of IACS member societies appears anywhere in the answer.** The IACS membership record
returned **HTTP 403** to automated retrieval, membership has changed more than once in recent years,
and a wrong number is worse than no number.
