# QP2602 Q1 - verification record

**Paper:** QP2602, February 2026, Engineering Management, MEO Class I.
**Built:** 2026-08-08. **State:** Pilot Review Ready.

**Question.** LLMC 1976 - three main principles and linkage with two other IMO liability and
compensation conventions (5); liabilities covered (5); principles of the Salvage Convention 1989,
the rationale for extending limitation to salvors, and the current limit applicable to them (6).

## Reasoning recorded

- **"Three main principles" is not a printed list anywhere.** No source enumerates an official three.
  The answer therefore selects a defensible three - unbreakable right, tonnage-based limit, single
  fund - and anchors each to articles (1 and 4; 6 and 7; 11 to 13). This is stated in the
  decomposition gate as a technical ambiguity rather than presented as received doctrine.
- **The salvor's limit is derived, not quoted.** There is no separate salvor figure. Art 6(4) supplies
  a deemed tonnage of 1,500, which falls in the "not exceeding 2,000 GT" band, which under the 1996
  Protocol as amended in 2012 gives 3.02m / 1.51m SDR. The answer sets out that chain explicitly and
  states the conditionality on which instruments the State has accepted.

## Flags

- **B_CURRENCY_CHECK** - the limit figures. The 1996 Protocol carries tacit acceptance for raising
  amounts; the figures have stood since 8 June 2015 but would change silently.
- **C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION** - India's Part XA implementation rests on practitioner commentary, not
  the statute. Also affected by the MS Act 2025 repeal (15 March 2026).
