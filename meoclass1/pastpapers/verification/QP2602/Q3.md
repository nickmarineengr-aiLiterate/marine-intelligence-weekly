# QP2602 Q3 - verification record

**Paper:** QP2602, February 2026, Engineering Management, MEO Class I.
**Built:** 2026-08-08. **State:** Pilot Review Ready.

**Question.** Application of formal safety assessment in carriage of lithium battery in vehicles and
containers and in RORO ships (10); measures by stakeholders to minimise incidents (6).

## Corrections applied during verification

- **A search summary asserted an entry-into-force date of 1 January 2028 for these amendments.**
  That is wrong: 1 Jan 2028 is the **existing-ship compliance date** (first survey on or after), and
  the amendments entered into force on **1 January 2026**. The class-society notice, which is
  specific and dated December 2025, was preferred and the date corrected. Recorded because it is the
  same class of error as the QP2601 Q9 pre-Manila STCW text - a right instrument with a wrong state.

## Reasoning recorded

- **No dedicated published IMO FSA study on lithium batteries in ro-ro carriage was located.** The
  answer therefore explains how the FSA method applies and identifies the FIRESAFE programme and the
  resulting amendments as the risk-based work actually done. It does **not** assert that such an FSA
  submission exists. Recorded as a C flag.
- FSA is a rule-making tool, not a shipboard risk assessment. The question's wording invites the
  wrong reading; the answer resolves it in step 1.
