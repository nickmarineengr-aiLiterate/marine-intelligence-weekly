## Reasoning recorded

- The limb asks for measures **adopted**. Industry submissions (ICS, INTERCARGO) are demands, not
  remedies, and are kept out of the answer's remedial list; they appear only as context.
- **Status at the sitting date matters.** Phase 2 opened on 1 January 2026, five weeks before the
  exam. The answer states the review as "under way but not complete" rather than resolved.
- CII is not AER for all ship types (cruise and ro-pax use a different metric). Recorded as an
  ambiguity and reflected in the answer's wording.

## Flags

- **B_CURRENCY_CHECK** - the Z factors, including the MEPC.400(83) 2027-2030 values. Phase 2
  expressly covers revision of the Z factor and reference lines.
- **C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION** - MIW holds no licensed consolidated MARPOL Annex VI; reg 28 and 28.11
  are cited from the IMO page and from MEPC.338(76), which quotes reg 28.11 directly.
