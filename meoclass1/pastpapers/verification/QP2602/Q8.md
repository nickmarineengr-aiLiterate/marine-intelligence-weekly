# QP2602 Q8 - verification record

**Paper:** QP2602, February 2026, Engineering Management, MEO Class I.
**Built:** 2026-08-08. **State:** Pilot Review Ready.

**Question.** The revised GHG strategy of the IMO and amendments being considered under MARPOL
Annex VI, including the inclusion of new Chapter-V. (16)

## The headline finding of this paper

**At the February 2026 sitting date the draft new Chapter 5 of MARPOL Annex VI had been APPROVED but
NOT ADOPTED, and was not in force.**

The second extraordinary session of the MEPC (**MEPC/ES.2**) met from **14 to 17 October 2025**
specifically to consider adoption. It could not reach consensus and **adjourned for twelve months**.
This was read on the IMO's own meeting summary and press briefing:

> "The International Maritime Organization (IMO) has agreed to **adjourn** the extraordinary session
> of the Marine Environment Protection Committee (MEPC), which was convened this week (14 to 17
> October 2025) to consider the adoption of draft amendments to MARPOL Annex VI, including the IMO
> Net-Zero Framework."
>
> "The extraordinary session will be reconvened in **12 months' time**."

Decision taken **17 October 2025**. The Intersessional Working Group nonetheless met 20-24 October
2025 to continue developing implementation guidelines.

**Why this matters.** A very large volume of material published between April and October 2025 states
that the framework would be adopted in October 2025 and enter into force in 2027. Any answer built on
that material is **wrong for this sitting**. This is the same failure mode as the QP2601 Q9 pre-Manila
STCW text and the QP2607 Q7 "not yet in force" secondary source: a correct-looking source describing a
superseded state of affairs. The examiner's own wording - *amendments **being considered*** - confirms
the paper was set with the October 2025 outcome in view.

## Reasoning recorded

- The **Strategy is policy; Chapter 5 would be law.** The answer keeps them apart explicitly and
  states the status in its own route step so it cannot be lost in an essay.
- Every Chapter 5 figure is written as conditional ("would", "as approved in draft") because the
  text was never adopted and **no consolidated text exists**. This is a semantic-integrity point:
  the derived layers - route points, cards, quick revision - must not flatten "draft" into "is".

## Flags

- **B_CURRENCY_CHECK** - the approved-not-adopted status. The session was due to reconvene around
  October 2026. If adopted since, the sitting-date answer stays correct but the current position
  differs and both must be stated.
- **C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION** x2 - the numeric content (draft text not read, no consolidated text
  exists, values provisional by nature); and the procedural detail (Rule 31.1.2, roll call vote)
  taken from a class brief rather than the session report.
