## 1. What the question demands

> a) Enumerate the structure of IACS and explain difference between UI, UR, PR from IACS. Does it have any significance to ship owners/operators? (8) b) What is a Recognized organization? What are the salient features of the R.O. Code? How do Administrations monitor R.O.s? (8)

**Intent.** Six tasks in two limbs. Limb (a): describe IACS's structure, distinguish its three Resolution types, and give a reasoned answer on whether they matter commercially to owners and operators. Limb (b): define a Recognized Organization, state the salient features of the RO Code, and explain flag State oversight. The examiner is testing precision on distinctions that are routinely conflated - UR against UI against PR, and classification society against RO.

**Mark allocation.** 16 printed as 8 + 8, three tasks in each limb, so roughly two and a half marks per task. In limb (a) the organisation chart is worth least and is written at greatest length by most candidates; the UR/UI/PR distinction and the significance task carry the weight. In limb (b) the three tasks are roughly equal and the oversight task is the one that rewards structure.

**Examiner expectation.** Precision on UR versus UI versus PR, expressed as what each acts upon rather than as three definitions; the point that delegation to an RO never transfers the Administration's responsibility; and knowledge that Parts 1 and 2 of the RO Code are mandatory while Part 3 is guidance. Naming the adopting and mandating resolutions distinguishes a candidate who has read the instrument from one who has read about it.

The answer is decomposed into these required components:

- (a) What IACS is and how it is organised
- (a) UR, UI and PR - what each acts upon, and its status
- (a) A reasoned answer on significance to owners and operators
- (b) What a Recognized Organization is, and what delegation does and does not transfer
- (b) The RO Code - its adopting resolutions, its parts, and which parts are mandatory
- (b) How an Administration actually monitors an RO, and who audits the Administration

verification, stated at the level that verification established and not extended, and **no
