## 5. ANSWER ROUTE

Archetype: `compare`. The principal headings of the model answer ARE this route.

1. **Why NOx forms at all, and the three levers every measure pulls**
   - The nitrogen comes overwhelmingly from the AIR, not from the fuel
   - Nitrogen and oxygen combine at flame temperature, and the rate rises very steeply with peak temperature
   - Lever one - peak temperature: add something that absorbs heat or raises the charge's specific heat
   - Lever two - oxygen availability: displace part of the oxygen with inert gas
   - Lever three - residence time: shorten the time spent at peak, through timing and rate shaping
   - The governing trade-off: NOx and fuel consumption pull against each other, which is why this is a design problem and not a solved one
2. **A) Primary measures - preventing NOx from forming**
   - Definition: the measure acts on or before combustion, so that less NOx is ever created
   - The combustion event - retarded injection timing; the simplest measure, and it costs fuel directly
   - Injection pressure and rate shaping; common rail, which decouples timing and pressure from speed and load
   - Miller timing with high-pressure charging - the charge expands and cools before compression begins
   - Design measures - combustion chamber and nozzle geometry, swirl, and compression ratio
   - Adding water - fuel-water emulsion, direct water injection, and scavenge air moisturizing
   - Changing the charge itself - exhaust gas recirculation, and lean premixed operation on gas
3. **A) Secondary measures - removing NOx after it has formed**
   - Definition: the combustion is left untouched and the exhaust is treated
   - Selective catalytic reduction is the only mature secondary measure in marine use
   - A reagent is injected upstream of a catalyst; NOx and reagent react to nitrogen and water
   - Temperature-sensitive - which decides whether the reactor sits before or after the turbine
   - Sulphur-sensitive - salts form at low temperature and blind the catalyst
   - Dosing must be right, or unreacted reagent passes through as ammonia slip
   - It adds a reagent, a tank and a supply chain, plus space and exhaust back pressure - severe constraints in a retrofit
   - An exhaust gas scrubber is NOT a NOx measure - it addresses sulphur oxides and particulates
4. **A) Primary against secondary - the comparison the limb asks for**
   - Compare on six axes: where it acts, effect on the engine, plant required, consumables, reach, failure mode
   - Primary: no plant or modest plant, but a fuel penalty. Secondary: no fuel penalty, but a large installation and a consumable
   - Primary measures degrade gradually; a secondary system can fail as a system and lose the whole benefit at once
   - Primary measures are cumulative - several are used together, and they are designed in rather than added on
   - The dividing line is where the NOx goes, not where the equipment sits
   - Which is why exhaust gas recirculation is PRIMARY - the commonest error on this question
   - They are complements, not rivals: primary for the ordinary limit; a heavy primary measure or a secondary one for the strictest
5. **A) The regulatory frame that decides which you need**
   - MARPOL Annex VI regulation 13; at this sitting the Revised Annex VI, in force 1 November 2022
   - Three tiers, and each tier's limit is a function of the engine's rated speed
   - The construction date fixes the tier; the strictest tier also depends on where the ship is operating
   - That gives two operating modes, and the changeover is recorded with date, time and position
   - An output threshold applies, and certain emergency and survival-craft engines are excluded
   - Certification under the NOx Technical Code 2008 as revised; in-service compliance through the NOx Technical File
   - No limit value is quoted - and the point that matters is that the strictest tier is a step change, not reachable by combustion tuning alone
6. **B) Scavenge Air Moisturizing**
   - Water is evaporated into the scavenge air, downstream of the charge air cooler
   - Latent heat absorbed on evaporation, plus water vapour's higher specific heat - both lower the peak temperature
   - A humidification section, often multi-stage with re-heat between stages to carry more vapour
   - Water supply and treatment - what reaches the cylinder must be fresh and clean; salt would be ruinous
   - A water mist catcher - vapour into the cylinder, never droplets. The safety-critical item on the system
   - Costs: corrosion, disturbed cylinder lubrication, fresh water demand, a small efficiency penalty, control complexity
   - It is a PRIMARY measure, substantial in effect, but normally part of a package rather than a complete answer to the strictest tier
7. **B) Exhaust Gas Recirculation**
   - Part of the exhaust is cleaned, cooled and returned to the scavenge air
   - It reduces the oxygen concentration of the charge - the oxygen lever
   - It raises the charge's specific heat through carbon dioxide and water vapour - the temperature lever. Two levers at once
   - Arrangement: scrubber, cooler, water mist catcher, EGR blower, shut-off and change-over valves
   - Turbocharger arrangements to suit - cut-out, variable geometry or auxiliary blowers, because gas is being diverted from the turbine
   - Bleed-off water treatment - it is acidic and carries soot and metals, and is monitored before any discharge
   - Costs: fouling, acid corrosion, soot into the cylinder, complexity, blower power and waste streams
   - It is a PRIMARY measure, and it is capable of reaching the strictest tier on its own
8. **B) SAM against EGR - what each costs to fit and to run**
   - SAM pulls temperature only; EGR pulls temperature AND oxygen, which is why it reaches further
   - SAM adds water vapour to the charge; EGR adds cleaned, cooled exhaust
   - SAM is moderate plant; EGR is heavy plant - a scrubber, a blower and a water treatment system
   - SAM consumes fresh water; EGR consumes blower power and produces sludge and bleed-off water
   - SAM's principal risk is water carry-over; EGR's is fouling and acid corrosion
   - Both put water and acid where the engine's designer did not intend them
   - So both transfer a burden onto cylinder lubrication and its monitoring - scrape-down analysis is how it is watched
