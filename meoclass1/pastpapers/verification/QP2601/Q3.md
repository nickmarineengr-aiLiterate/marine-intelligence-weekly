## 2. What was verified, and how

Salvage Convention Articles 8, 12, 13 and 14 were read in the Convention text. The ten Article 13(1) criteria (a) to (j) are reproduced in the order they appear. Article 14's increases - up to 30%, and to a maximum of 100% of expenses at a tribunal's discretion - were read rather than recalled. York-Antwerp Rule A and Marine Insurance Act 1963 s.66 were carried forward from the QP2607 Q5 verification.

**The one weak term is 'Artificial'.** It is not defined in the York-Antwerp Rules. It is consistent practitioner usage for allowances the numbered Rules permit on a common-BENEFIT basis where the Rule A common-SAFETY test is not met - port of refuge expenses, and crew wages and maintenance during detention, being the standard examples. The answer explains it in exactly those terms and states openly that the term is usage rather than a defined category. Recorded as a class C accepted limitation.

## 5. Open points carried in the answer

- 'Artificial general average' is practitioner terminology for allowances the numbered York-Antwerp Rules permit on a common-benefit basis although they do not satisfy the strict common-safety test in Rule A. It is not a term defined in the Rules themselves, and the answer says so rather than presenting it as a defined category.
- Which edition of the York-Antwerp Rules governs a given casualty depends on the incorporating contract. The answer states the position rather than asserting that YAR 2016 applies universally.
