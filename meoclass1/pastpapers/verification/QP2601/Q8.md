## 2. What was verified, and how

The Casualty Investigation Code, resolution MSC.255(84), was downloaded from IMO and read in full. Paragraph **2.22** is quoted verbatim. The supporting definitions were read: 2.9 marine casualty, 2.10 marine incident, 2.11 marine safety investigation, 2.16 material damage, 2.18 serious injury (more than 72 hours, commencing within seven days), 2.19 severe damage to the environment, 2.20 substantially interested State. The Part boundaries were confirmed from the text: **Part I General provisions** from chapter 1, **Part II Mandatory standards** from chapter 4, **Part III Recommended practices** from chapter 15. Chapter 6.1 requires an investigation into every very serious marine casualty; 14.1 requires the final report of every such investigation to go to IMO. The Code preamble confirms that MSC.257(84) amended SOLAS chapter XI-1 to make Parts I and II mandatory, effective **1 January 2010**. Chapter 7 cites SOLAS regulation I/21 and UNCLOS article 94(7) together.

**Verified negative.** The expression 'serious marine casualty', standing alone, was searched across the full Code text and returns **zero occurrences**. Only 'very serious marine casualty' is defined. The other term comes from IMO's separate reporting guidance. The answer says so, rather than presenting a graded scale the Code does not contain.
