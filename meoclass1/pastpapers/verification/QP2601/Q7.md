## 1. Question as printed

> State the UNCLOS requirements for member States, (a) To register ships flagged with them and (b) To control safety, pollution and social aspects of ships. (c) What mechanism is used in India to comply with these requirements enumerated at (a) and (b) above?

**No per-subpart marks are printed.** The paper prints limbs (a), (b) and (c) but allocates no marks between them. The weighting used in production is a working assumption and is not printed detail.

## 2. What was verified, and how

UNCLOS Articles 91, 92, 94 and 217 are stated from the Convention text. The Casualty Investigation Code, read for Q8, independently cites UNCLOS Article 94 and Article 94(7) as the basis of the flag State inquiry duty, which corroborates the 94(7) reading.

**The decisive verification is a date, and it was already in hand.** QP2607's Q7 verification read the Gazette text of the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025 and of commencement notification S.O. 1244(E): assent 18 August 2025, whole Act in force **15 March 2026**, 1958 Act repealed by s.324(1). This paper was sat in **January 2026**. The 2025 Act therefore had assent but had **not commenced** at the question date, and the correct answer to limb (c) is the **Merchant Shipping Act, 1958**. The same verified facts point the opposite way for the July 2026 sitting. This is the clearest instance in the paper of question-date truth diverging from current study truth, and the study guide states both positions.

**Not asserted:** any test for 'genuine link' (UNCLOS requires one and defines none), and any Indian subordinate rule numbers, which were not read at source.

## 5. Open points carried in the answer

- UNCLOS does not define 'genuine link' and no authoritative test has been settled. The answer states the requirement and its indeterminacy rather than inventing a test.
- The detail of Indian subordinate rules under the Merchant Shipping Act is not enumerated; the answer names the statute, the administration and the mechanism rather than citing rule numbers.

## 6. Re-verification register

### `B_CURRENCY_CHECK` - Indian subordinate rules under the Merchant Shipping Act are not enumerated, and the administration is described at institutional level

Rule-making under the Merchant Shipping Act 2025 was still in progress as at August 2026, and the naming of the administration itself is in transition following commencement. The answer names the statute and the administration without citing rule numbers. Re-check the institutional naming before publication.

### `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION` - No test is offered for the UNCLOS 'genuine link' requirement

UNCLOS Article 91 requires a genuine link but does not define it, and no authoritative test has been settled. Stating the requirement and its indeterminacy is the honest position and is published as such.
