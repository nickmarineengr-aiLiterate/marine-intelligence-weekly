## 1. Question as printed

> A VLCC suffered hull damage following a tank explosion and sank after a few days while undergoing salvage operations in the territorial sea of a coastal state. Discuss ship-owner's protections with reference to (a) marine insurance and (b) liabilities under international conventions.

**No per-subpart marks are printed.** The paper prints the limbs (a) marine insurance and (b) liabilities under international conventions, but allocates no marks between them. An even split is a reasonable working assumption and is NOT recorded as printed detail.

## 2. What was verified, and how

Convention entry-into-force dates were taken from IMO's own Status of Treaties summary: CLC Prot 1992 and Fund Prot 1992 both 30 May 1996; Supplementary Fund Prot 2003, 3 March 2005; Bunkers 2001, 21 November 2008; Nairobi WRC 2007, 14 April 2015; LLMC 1976, 1 December 1986 and its 1996 Protocol, 13 May 2004; Salvage 1989, 14 July 1996.

**Two claims were deliberately not made.** First, the question does not name the coastal state, so no assertion is made about its ratifications - and specifically none about whether it has extended the Nairobi Convention to its territorial sea, which that Convention makes an opt-in. Second, the cause of the tank explosion is not given, and it determines whether cover responds at all; the answer treats causation as the open question instead of assuming a covered peril. Where India is mentioned, the note is confined to the CLC and Fund regimes and flags that the domestic vehicle changed on 15 March 2026.

## 5. Open points carried in the answer

- The question does not name the coastal state, so no claim is made about which conventions that state has ratified or whether it has extended the Nairobi Convention to its territorial sea. The answer states the mechanism and flags the dependency.
- Whether cover actually responds depends on the cause of the tank explosion, which the question does not give. The answer treats the cause as the central unknown rather than assuming the owner is covered.
