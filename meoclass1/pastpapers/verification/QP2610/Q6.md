## 4. INTERPRETATION FLAG (Founder: independent review of Q6(b))

The answer's conclusion is a **conditional yes**. Rule VII allows damage to machinery when all of these hold:
- the ship is ashore and in a position of peril;
- the machinery is worked in endeavouring to refloat;
- the damage results from that endeavour.

The answer also applies the Rule Paramount's reasonableness test. This is **interpretation**, not a quoted rule:
- an adjuster could disallow the damage if working the engine was unreasonable on the facts;
- the applicable edition of the Rules depends on the contract of carriage.

The answer states both dependencies.
