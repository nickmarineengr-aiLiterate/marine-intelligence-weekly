- **B-01 (EGCS).** The Answer-tab inference "so scrubbing a stack emission into the sea is not compliance" is removed. Article 195 is presented as a duty on States, not a ban on any technology. The answer now states that MARPOL Annex VI regulation 4 allows a flag Administration to approve an EGCS as an equivalent to the regulation 14 limits, subject to regulation 4.4; that the IMO EGCS Guidelines (MEPC.340(77) for systems installed from 1 June 2022) set discharge-water criteria; and that some coastal and port States restrict or prohibit washwater discharge locally. PSSAs are designated under A.982(24), whose para 7.5.2.3 lists 211(6) as one of three legal bases for associated protective measures. A new Common-mistakes item was added and the double-hull follow-up was corrected (211(5) and 211(6)(c)). Sources: UNCLOS art. 211(6); A.982(24) s.6.1 and 7.5.2.3; IMO Special Areas and PSSA pages.

Sources: UNCLOS art. 235; IOPC Funds legal-framework page.
