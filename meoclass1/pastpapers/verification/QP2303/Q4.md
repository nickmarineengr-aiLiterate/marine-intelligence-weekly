## 7. ANSWER CONSTRUCTION

**Route archetype:** `compare`  ·  **Steps:** 8  ·  **Model answer blocks:** 46  ·  **Study note blocks:** 36  ·  **Retrieval cards:** 10

The answer route is the single canonical spine; the decomposition, memory cue, quick revision, retrieval cards
and study notes all derive from it. Numbered scoring propositions, explicit subheadings and a marks-aware depth
allocation are used throughout, and the exam-manageable sequence is stated in `memory_cue`.

**Answer route:**

1. **Why NOx forms at all, and the three levers every measure pulls** - The nitrogen comes overwhelmingly from the AIR, not from the fuel; Nitrogen and oxygen combine at flame temperature; the rate rises very steeply with peak temperature; Lever one - peak temperature: add something that absorbs heat or raises the charge's specific heat; Lever two - oxygen availability: displace part of the oxygen with inert gas; Lever three - residence time: shorten the time spent at peak through timing and rate; The governing trade-off: NOx and fuel consumption pull against each other
2. **A) Primary measures - preventing NOx from forming** _(A.)_ - Definition: acts inside the cylinder so that less NOx is ever created; Combustion event - retarded injection timing (simplest, and costs fuel directly); Injection pressure and rate shaping; common rail decoupling timing from speed and load; Miller timing with high-pressure charging - lower charge temperature at start of compression; Chamber, nozzle and swirl geometry, and compression ratio - design measures; Adding water - fuel-water emulsion, direct water injection, scavenge air moisturizing; Changing the charge - exhaust gas recirculation, and lean premixed gas operation
3. **A) Secondary measures - removing NOx after it has formed** _(A.)_ - Definition: the combustion is untouched and the exhaust is treated; Selective catalytic reduction is the only mature secondary measure; Reagent injected upstream of a catalyst; NOx and reagent react to nitrogen and water; Temperature-sensitive - which decides whether the reactor sits before or after the turbocharger; Sulphur-sensitive - salts form at low temperature and blind the catalyst; Dosing must be right, or ammonia slip; and a reagent, tank and supply chain are added; Space and back pressure - severe constraints in a retrofit; An exhaust gas scrubber is NOT a NOx measure - it addresses SOx and particulates
4. **A) Primary against secondary - the comparison the limb asks for** _(A.)_ - Compare on where it acts, engine effect, plant, consumables, reach, failure mode; Primary: no plant or modest plant, but a fuel penalty; secondary: no fuel penalty, but a large installation and a reagent; Primary degrades gradually; secondary can fail as a system and lose the whole benefit at once; Primary measures are cumulative and are used together, designed in rather than added on; The dividing line is where the NOx goes, not where the equipment sits; Which is why EGR is PRIMARY - the commonest error in the question; They are complements: primary for the ordinary limit, a heavy primary or a secondary measure for the strict one
5. **A) The regulatory frame that decides which you need** _(A.)_ - MARPOL Annex VI regulation 13; at this sitting the Revised Annex VI, in force 1 November 2022; Three tiers, each limit a function of the engine's rated speed; Construction date fixes the tier; the strictest tier also depends on where the ship is operating; Two operating modes, and the changeover recorded with date, time and position; An output threshold applies, and certain emergency and survival-craft engines are excluded; Certification under the revised NOx Technical Code 2008; compliance in service through the NOx Technical File; No limit value is quoted - and the point that matters is that the strictest tier is a step change unreachable by combustion tuning alone
6. **B) Scavenge Air Moisturizing** _(B.)_ - Water evaporated into the scavenge air after the charge air cooler; Latent heat absorbed on evaporation, and water vapour's higher specific heat - both lower the peak temperature; A humidification section, often multi-stage with re-heat between stages; Water supply and treatment - what reaches the cylinder must be fresh and clean; salt would be ruinous; A water mist catcher - vapour into the cylinder, never droplets. The safety-critical item; Costs: corrosion, disturbed cylinder lubrication, fresh water demand, a small efficiency penalty, control complexity; A primary measure, substantial, but normally part of a package rather than a complete Tier III answer
7. **B) Exhaust Gas Recirculation** _(B.)_ - Part of the exhaust cleaned, cooled and returned to the scavenge air; Reduces oxygen concentration - the oxygen lever; Raises the charge's specific heat via CO2 and water vapour - the temperature lever. Two levers at once; Arrangement: scrubber, cooler, water mist catcher, EGR blower, shut-off and change-over valves; Turbocharger arrangements to suit - cut-out, variable geometry or auxiliary blowers; Bleed-off water treatment - acidic, soot and metal laden, monitored before any discharge; Costs: fouling, acid corrosion, soot in the cylinder, complexity, blower power, waste streams; A PRIMARY measure, capable of the strictest tier on its own
8. **B) SAM against EGR - what each costs to fit and to run** _(B.)_ - SAM pulls temperature only; EGR pulls temperature AND oxygen, which is why it reaches further; SAM adds water vapour; EGR adds cleaned, cooled exhaust; SAM is moderate plant; EGR is high plant with a scrubber, blower and water treatment; SAM consumes fresh water; EGR consumes blower power and produces sludge and bleed-off water; SAM's principal risk is water carry-over; EGR's is fouling and acid corrosion; Both put water and acid where the engine's designer did not intend them; So both transfer a burden onto cylinder lubrication and monitoring - scrape-down analysis is how it is watched
