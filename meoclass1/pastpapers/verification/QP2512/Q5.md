## The finding the question turns on

The stem's premise invites the wrong answer — that a foreign ship is the flag State's business. The
statute settles it, and it was read directly:

> **s.358(1)** *"…(a) on or near the coasts of India, **any ship** is lost, abandoned, stranded or
> materially damaged; (b) on or near the coasts of India, **any ship** causes loss or material
> damage to any other ship; (c) any loss of life ensues by reason of any casualty happening to or on
> board **any ship** on or near the coasts of India; (d) in any place, [such casualty] occurs to or
> on board any **Indian ship**…"*

**Limbs (a)–(c) are flag-neutral; only (d) and (e) are confined to Indian ships.** India's
competence rests on the **geography of the casualty, not the nationality of the vessel**, and
`s.357` extends *"coasts"* to creeks and tidal rivers, bringing ports inside.

**And the boundary reappears where it should.** `s.369(2)` — where the investigation affects a
master or officer of **a ship other than an Indian ship** holding a foreign certificate, the Central
Government **may transmit a copy of the report together with the evidence to the proper authority in
that country**. `s.363(1)(b)` — a foreign certificate is reachable outside a formal investigation
**only where the misconduct occurred on board an Indian ship**. **India owns the facts; the flag
State owns the officer.** Both provisions were read in the Act's own text.

### One citation deliberately split rather than merged

**GISIS appears nowhere in the Casualty Investigation Code.** The Code says only that the report is
submitted *to the Organization*. The **GISIS Marine Casualties and Incidents module** and the
electronic reporting route come from **`MSC-MEPC.3/Circ.4/Rev.1`**, a separate circular implementing
the reports required under SOLAS I/21 and XI-1/6, MARPOL articles 8 and 12 and Load Lines article
23. Attributing GISIS to the Code would have been a **citation error**; the two are stated
separately for that reason. Established by searching the Code text (`GISIS in CIC: 0`).
