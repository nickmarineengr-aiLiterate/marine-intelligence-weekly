# QP2508 Q3 - verification record

**Paper:** QP2508, August 2025. **Built:** 2026-08-10. **State:** Pilot Review Ready.
**Reuse:** Tier D. Answer donors **QP2602 Q3**, **QP2603 Q8**, **QP2604 Q2** (all 2026, identical

**Question.** (a) The application of formal safety assessment in the carriage of lithium batteries in
vehicles and containers and in RORO ships. (10) (b) Measures to be taken by stakeholders to minimise
incidents. (6)

## THE FINDING OF THIS PAPER: an intake temporal flag that was wrong

**The intake spec records `temporal_review: {state: "STABLE", risk: "LOW", classes: []}` for this
question. That is incorrect.**

The donor's step 5 closes on:

> "resolution MSC.550(108) then amended SOLAS chapter II-2 regulations 7 and 20 and MSC.555(108)
> amended FSS Code chapters 7 and 9, both adopted at MSC 108 in May 2024 and **in force on
> 1 January 2026 - five weeks before this examination**"

| Stage | Date | At the August 2025 sitting |
|---|---|---|
| **Adopted** at MSC 108 | May 2024 | past |
| **<- AUGUST 2025 SITTING** | | **adopted, NOT in force** |
| **Entered into force** | **1 January 2026** | **five months in the FUTURE** |
| Applies to ships **constructed on or after** | 1 January 2026 | future |
| Existing ships in scope, by first survey on or after | 1 January 2028 | future |

So an in-force date falls **between this sitting and every built donor in the family**. The same
printed question has a materially different regulatory conclusion at the two ends. That is not a
stable question, whatever the stem looks like.

**How it was caught.** Not by the flag - by the **mandatory assembled-answer sweep** required for
every Tier D reuse. This is the clearest evidence in the paper that the sweep is not a formality:
had the flag been trusted, a five-month currency error would have shipped inside a question labelled
low risk. **Reported to the Founder rather than silently corrected in the intake spec.**

## What the corrected step 5 says

At this sitting the **operative** IMO instrument on the subject was **MSC.1/Circ.1615**, the interim
guidelines, alongside SOLAS II-2 as it then stood and the IMDG Code. MSC.550(108) and MSC.555(108)
were adopted, dated and known to industry, and are stated as what was **coming** - which also makes
limb (b) more important at this sitting than at the later ones, because the gap between the known
hazard and the mandatory response was still open and had to be closed operationally.

## Section 35 boundary observed

The donor QP2602 Q3 itself carries `reuse_kind: "shape_only"` pointing at **QP2607 Q1** (FSA applied
to **iron ore pellets** in bulk carriers), because only the FSA *method* was reused there - different
cargo, hazard, ship type and carriage requirements. That is **not** a recurrence edge.
**No edge to QP2607 Q1 has been created here.** This question's link to QP2602 Q3 is a genuine
same-task edge on identical printed stems, and `reused_from` was left as the intake set it.

## Reasoning recorded

- **The method must be visible.** A 10-mark method question is marked on whether the five steps are
  named *and* applied. The route does both, in order.
- **The physics governs the risk control options.** Runaway liberates oxygen from the cathode, so it
  cannot be smothered; cooling limits **propagation** rather than extinguishing the cell. Stating
  that water "puts it out" misdescribes both the chemistry and the tactic.
- **Adopted / in force / applicable** are separated explicitly, which ties this question to Q7 of
  the same paper and makes the temporal correction a teaching point rather than an erratum.

## Flags

- **B_CURRENCY_CHECK** - the adoption and in-force dates. Load-bearing here in a way they are not in
  the 2026 objects: the whole of step 5 turns on 1 January 2026 falling *after* this sitting.
- **C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION** x2 - amendment content from a class notice, no licensed SOLAS/FSS Code
  held; and the negative finding that no dedicated published IMO FSA study on lithium batteries in
  ro-ro carriage was located.

## Family benefit and a warning

The FSA methodology, the hazard analysis and the whole of limb (b) are sitting-independent and serve
every member of this four-sitting family. **Step 5 is not**: it is the only part that changes, and it
changes at 1 January 2026. Any future paper in this family must be placed on the correct side of
that date before the step is reused.
