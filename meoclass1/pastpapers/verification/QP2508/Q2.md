## Reasoning recorded

- **"Adopted" is read strictly in limb (b).** The industry wish-list (a cargo-based metric, relief
  for waiting time, short-sea relief) had **not** been adopted. Writing it as the outcome is both a
  scoring error and simply untrue, and the study guide says so in its own section.
- **The mechanism is given before the complaints.** A list of grievances without the ratio
  arithmetic that causes them reads as grumbling; the ratio explanation is the analytical mark.
- **The commercial half is given equal weight.** Limb (a) names commercial impact expressly and it
  is where a management-level candidate separates himself.
