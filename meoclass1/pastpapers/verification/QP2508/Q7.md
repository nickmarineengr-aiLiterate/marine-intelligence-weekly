## Reasoning recorded

- **The two named terms get their own heading.** The examiner numbers them (i) and (ii), so an
  answer that defines them in passing inside a narrative will not be credited for them.
- **They are contrasted, not merely defined** - accession is one step, signature-subject-to-
  ratification is two, and both reach the same destination. That contrast is the mark.
- **Convention entry into force and amendment entry into force are kept separate.** Threshold plus
  waiting period for the first; tacit acceptance for the second. The stem asks about both and
  merging them loses the distinction.
- **The answer ends on domestic effect.** International entry into force does not bind the ship; the
  flag State's implementing legislation does. This links the question to Q5 and Q8.
