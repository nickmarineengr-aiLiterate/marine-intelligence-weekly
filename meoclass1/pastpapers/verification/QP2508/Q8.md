# QP2508 Q8 - verification record

**Paper:** QP2508, August 2025, Engineering Management, MEO Class I.
**Built:** 2026-08-10. **State:** Pilot Review Ready.
**Reuse:** Tier D from **QP2602 Q8** (February 2026), identical printed stem.

**Question.** The revised GHG strategy of the IMO and amendments being considered under MARPOL
Annex VI, including the inclusion of new Chapter-V to achieve the decarbonisation goal and
transition to a Net Zero future in shipping. (16)

## The headline finding

**At the August 2025 sitting the draft new Chapter 5 of MARPOL Annex VI had been APPROVED at
MEPC 83 and CIRCULATED to Parties for adoption, but had NOT been adopted and was NOT in force.
The adoption session had not yet been held.**

This is a *different* correct answer from the one the February 2026 donor gives, and the difference
is the whole point of the question. The chronology, fixed against primary text:

| Date | Event | Status of the Framework |
|---|---|---|
| 3-7 July 2023 | MEPC 80 adopts the 2023 IMO GHG Strategy, **resolution MEPC.377(80)** (adopted 7 July 2023) | policy adopted; no legal obligation |
| **7 to 11 April 2025** | **MEPC 83 considers and approves** draft amendments to MARPOL Annex VI including the **IMO Net-Zero Framework (new chapter 5)** | approved |
| **11 April 2025** | **Circular Letter No.5005** transmits the draft revised consolidated Annex VI **under MARPOL article 16(2)(a)**, "for consideration with a view to adoption at **MEPC/ES.2 (14 to 17 October 2025)** in accordance with articles 16(2)(b), (c) and (d)" | approved **and circulated** |
| **<- AUGUST 2025 SITTING** | | **approved and circulated, NOT adopted, NOT in force; adoption session still in the future** |
| 14 to 17 October 2025 | MEPC/ES.2 meets, cannot reach consensus, **adjourns for twelve months** | *after this sitting - study guide only* |

**Why this matters.** The February 2026 donor's status step is an account of the October 2025
adjournment. Reused unaltered into this paper it would have described as completed history an event
that lay **four months in the future** when the paper was sat. This is the mirror image of the usual
staleness failure and is the reason the donor's step 6 was **replaced rather than adapted**.

**A second, subtler contamination was caught.** The donor lists among its *common mistakes*:
"Stating that the Net-Zero Framework was adopted in October 2025, or that it enters into force in
2027. Neither is true." That is a correct February 2026 statement. Imported into an August 2025
answer it would have taught the candidate that the **reasonable and correct expectation at the
sitting** was an error. It was removed.

## Correction to the donor's recorded limitation

QP2602 Q8 records as `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION` that "the draft text of the new Chapter 5 was not read"
and that "because the amendments were never adopted **no consolidated text exists**".

**The second half of that is not correct.** A draft consolidated revised MARPOL Annex VI does exist:
it is the annex to **Circular Letter No.5005**, and it is the very text circulated under
article 16(2)(a) for adoption. It was read for this answer. Every numeric claim about chapter 5 in
QP2508 Q8 is therefore **primary-verified against the draft regulations**, where the donor held the
same claims at authoritative-secondary. This does not make the figures binding - a draft is
provisional until adopted - but it does move them from "reported by a class society" to "read in the
text that was on the table".

Recommended follow-up (not actioned here, outside this paper's scope): revisit QP2602 Q8's
`unresolved` and `reverify_before_publication` entries in the light of this.

## Reasoning recorded

- **Strategy is policy; chapter 5 would be law.** The answer keeps them apart explicitly and gives
  the status its own route step so it cannot be lost inside an essay.
- **Every chapter 5 figure is written conditionally** ("as drafted", "the draft would require").
  This is a semantic-integrity requirement: the derived layers - route points, retrieval cards,
  quick revision - must not flatten "draft" into "is". Checked on all of them.
- **The circulation step is named.** Citing CL.5005 and article 16(2)(a) is what allows the status
  to be stated precisely rather than as "still being discussed", and it ties the question directly
  to Q7 of this same paper, which examines the entry-into-force machinery in the abstract.
- The **EU ETS / FuelEU Maritime** context that the donor carries in its study guide is omitted
  here. It belongs to the donor's account of *why consensus failed at ES.2*, an event outside this
  sitting; importing it would have smuggled the later narrative back in through the side door.

## Flags

- **C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION** - the chapter 5 numbers are primary for what the draft says, but a draft
  is provisional until adopted. The answer states them as draft figures throughout.
- **B_CURRENCY_CHECK** - the study guide's *current note*. The reconvened session falls due around
  October 2026. If the Framework is adopted or deferred again the current note must be updated;
  the model answer, fixed at August 2025, must not be.

## Family benefit

The Strategy content (steps 1-3) and the draft chapter 5 content (step 5) are sitting-independent
between April 2025 and October 2025 and are reusable without change by any paper sat in that window.
The **status** step is not reusable across the October 2025 boundary in either direction.
