## 8. RE-VERIFICATION REGISTER

### `B_CURRENCY_CHECK`

**Claim:** The statement that no mid-term measure had been adopted, that there was no IMO carbon price, and that the first CII ratings had not yet been issued.

**Why:** These are TRUE AS AT THE FEBRUARY 2024 SITTING and are the correct answer to a question about ongoing developments at that date. They are emphatically NOT true today - the basket work advanced at MEPC 81 and MEPC 82, and the IMO Net-Zero Framework was approved at MEPC 83 in April 2025. This answer must never be reused at a later sitting without being re-anchored, and a reviewer reading it after 2025 must not 'correct' it to the current position.

### `B_CURRENCY_CHECK`

**Claim:** The SEEMP guidelines edition stated as operative is MEPC.346(78).

**Why:** Correct for February 2024 and verified from the corpus. It was amended by MEPC.388(81) in March 2024 and replaced by MEPC.395(82) in October 2024. Any reuse at a later sitting must move to the then-current edition, and any reuse at an earlier 2024 sitting must stay on MEPC.346(78).

### `C_ACCEPTED_LIMITATION`

**Claim:** No CII reduction factor value, reference line coefficient or A-to-E rating boundary figure is quoted.

**Why:** Those values live in the CII guideline series (G1 to G5), which was itself revised at MEPC 81 five weeks after this sitting. Quoting a figure would create an unnecessary currency exposure on a question that does not ask for one. Deliberate omission, recorded so a later session does not read it as a gap.
