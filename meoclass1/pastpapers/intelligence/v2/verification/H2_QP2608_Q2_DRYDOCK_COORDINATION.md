## Comparison

**Shared core:** the opening scenario and the first task, essentially word for
word (`co-ordination`/`coordination`, `information exchange`/`exchange of
information`, `for successful entry`/`for entering the dock`).

**Material differences:** the modern question is roughly **three times the
work**. Limb 3 in particular inverts the operation — the historical question
never leaves the docking evolution, while the modern one carries the candidate
through to undocking.

## Why the classification is SAME_CORE_ASK, not NEAR_VERBATIM

At limb level, limb 1 is `NEAR_VERBATIM`. At question level it is not: a
candidate who prepared the July 2012 answer would write about a third of what
August 2026 demands and would miss the undocking limb entirely.

This is the clearest illustration of the limb-level finding in
`SIMILARITY_MODEL.md`. Publishing "Q2 is a near-verbatim repeat of 2012" would be
true of the sentence a candidate reads first and false of the question they have
to answer.
