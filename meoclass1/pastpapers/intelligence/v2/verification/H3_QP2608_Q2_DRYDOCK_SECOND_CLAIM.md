## Finding

The dry-dock/Master-coordination stem **was** located and **is** a genuine
ancestor of QP2608-Q2's first limb — but the located attestation is
**July 2012**, three months before the date claimed here.

Full stem comparison, limb analysis and classification reasoning are in
[H2](H2_QP2608_Q2_DEC2011.md) and are not repeated.

## On the two competing dates

H2 and H3 offer **December 2011** and **October 2012** for the same question.
Both are month-level, neither carries a question number, and the located
evidence supports **neither** — it supports July 2012.

The near-miss is itself informative. July 2012 and October 2012 are two sittings
apart. A recollection that a question "was in the 2012 papers" is consistent with
the evidence; a recollection that it was specifically October is not supported.
This is the expected failure mode of month-level recall and is the reason §13
requires text comparison over asserted recurrence.

It is possible the stem was set in October 2012 as well. DieselShip's `2012/OCT`
set exists (63 questions, 7 categories) but is subscription-gated and was not
accessed. The claim is **untested**, not refuted.

## Why this does not become a second family

A single family — `FAMILY-EM-0002` — covers the dry-dock core ask. H2 and H3 are
two competing date claims about **one** recurrence, not evidence of two separate
historical occurrences. Recording them as two occurrences would double-count the
family's frequency and corrupt the dormancy calculation, which is exactly the
"do not collapse different sittings, do not invent them either" discipline in
§33.
