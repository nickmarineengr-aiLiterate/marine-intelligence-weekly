## Why PARTIALLY CONFIRMED and not CONFIRMED

The **recurrence is real** — this is as close to verbatim as the model's
`NEAR_VERBATIM` class allows, and it is the single most convincing find of the
session.

The **date in the hypothesis is not.** The only located attestation is
**July 2012**. Nothing was found placing this stem in June 2010, and:

- MIW holds no source copy of any sitting before January 2021;
- DieselShip's June 2010 set is public only as a count — **21 questions across
  7 exam categories** — and its contents are subscription-gated;
- a 21-question set spanning seven categories is unlikely to contain a complete
  nine-question Class I Engineering Management paper at all.

June 2010 is therefore **neither confirmed nor refuted**. A question can recur
many times, and it may well have been set in 2010; but on the evidence obtainable
without bypassing a paywall, the earliest attested appearance is July 2012.
