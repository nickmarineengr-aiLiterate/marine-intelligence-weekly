# Paper-setter hypothesis — National Testing Agency

**Verdict: `NO OFFICIAL EVIDENCE FOUND`**

**RESEARCH ONLY.** `current_as_of: 2026-08-17`

## The rumour

Group rumour reported by the Founder: the **National Testing Agency (NTA)** may
now be involved in setting the MEO Class I written paper.

Treated throughout as an **unverified hypothesis**.

## Bounded evidence search

Searched for DG Shipping notices, Ministry notices, NTA notices, tenders and
procurement documents, examination outsourcing contracts and circulars, across
terms including: MEO Class I, Marine Engineer Officer Examination, Engineering
Management, DG Shipping, MMD, National Testing Agency, NTA, exam conduct,
question paper, paper setting, computer-based test, written examination,
outsourcing.

### What was found

| Source | Relevance |
|---|---|
| DG Shipping notices on examination system and conduct of orals | Describe DG Shipping's **own** examiner arrangements — external/internal examiners drawn from ex-examiners of the Directorate and serving professionals. No third-party agency. |
| DGS Order No. 9 of 2024 | Reviews curriculum, eligibility, examination, assessment and certification for MEO **NCV** grades. No NTA. |
| NTA public exam portfolio | NEET, JEE Main, NCET, UGC-NET and similar. **No maritime examination appears.** |
| Ministry of Education statement, July 2024 | States that core activities involving the setting of question papers for NTA-conducted exams are **not** outsourced. Concerns NTA's own exams; no bearing on MEO. |

### What was NOT found

- No DG Shipping notice, order or circular mentioning NTA.
- No NTA notice, syllabus or calendar entry mentioning MEO, marine engineering
  or any DG Shipping examination.
- No tender, procurement document or contract transferring MEO paper-setting.

### A structural point

The two bodies sit under different ministries. NTA is an autonomous testing
agency under the **Ministry of Education**, constituted for higher-education
entrance testing. MEO certification is statutory competency certification under
the **Directorate General of Shipping**, Ministry of Ports, Shipping and
Waterways, administered under the Merchant Shipping Act and STCW.

A transfer of paper-setting across that boundary would require a visible
administrative instrument. None was found. **Absence of such an instrument is
meaningful here**, in a way that absence of evidence often is not — this is a
class of change that cannot happen quietly.

## Do not overfit the theory (§39)

The recurrence measured in this session does **not** support an inference about
setter identity, and would be weak evidence even if it were larger:

1. **The recurrence is modest.** ~17% of QP2608 by marks (see
   `QP2608_PAPER_DNA.md`), not a rebuilt paper.
2. **Recurrence is normal here.** MIW's own corpus shows dense recurrence
   *within* 2021–2026 under whatever arrangement already obtained — QP2512-Q4's
   motivation sentence recurs verbatim from March 2021. Reuse is the standing
   pattern, not a new signature.
3. **The two revived stems are old, not new.** July 2012 material returning is
   evidence of a long-lived question bank, which is the *opposite* of a new
   setter arriving.
4. **The paper's newest questions are its most novel.** Q6 (scrubber discharge
   zones) and Q7 (HV switchboard) have no ancestor at all — Q7 none in the whole
   corpus. A paper drawing on an old bank would not produce them.

Ordinary explanations — a stable syllabus, a long-lived question bank, examiner
convention, institutional templates, a setter consulting old papers, or simple
clustering — account for what was observed without invoking NTA.

**Correct result: `NO OFFICIAL EVIDENCE FOUND`.** Not "contradicted": nothing was
found asserting NTA involvement, and nothing was found denying it either.

**Do not communicate this rumour to candidates in any form.**
