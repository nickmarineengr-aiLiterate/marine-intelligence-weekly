## PILOT C — unseaworthy vessels · Merchant Shipping Act · ANSWER IMPACT **MODERATE**

`delta_id: TD-EM-0008-C` · `family_id: FAMILY-EM-0008` *(see below)*
`official_bank_ancestor: BANK-160` · `current_question_id: null` — not in QP2608

The strongest temporal case in the corpus, and the only one Phase 2 found with a
demonstrable `DO NOT WRITE TODAY`.

**ASKED BEFORE**
Five verified MIW sittings of `BANK-160` — July 2023, October 2024, June 2025,
August 2025, **February 2026** — plus the undated official bank ancestor. Date
confidence **HIGH**: these are MIW-held papers with anchored sittings.

**WHAT STILL STANDS**
- The concept of an unseaworthy ship, and the distinction between an *unseaworthy*
  and an *unsafe* ship.
- The owner's obligations to the crew in respect of seaworthiness.
- The answer's architecture, and most of its substance.

**WHAT CHANGED**

| Claim | Category | Authority | Exam relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| The **Merchant Shipping Act, 2025 (Act 24 of 2025)** received assent 18 August 2025; the whole Act came into force **15 March 2026** by S.O. 1244(E) | `UPDATED_STATUTE` | MIW True Source, verified at `QP2607-Q7` and `QP2608-Q1` | `ESSENTIAL_CURRENT_POINT` |
| It **repeals the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958** at section 324(1) | `REMOVED_OBSOLETE_POINT` | as above | `ESSENTIAL_CURRENT_POINT` |
| The stem, following the bank, still names the 1958 Act — so the candidate must answer the question asked *and* state that the governing statute has changed | `UPDATED_REGULATORY_STATUS` | as above | `ESSENTIAL_CURRENT_POINT` |

**DO NOT WRITE TODAY**

| Obsolete claim | Why | Superseded by |
|---|---|---|
| “The Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 **is** the operative Indian statute” | Repealed 15 March 2026 | The Merchant Shipping Act, 2025 (Act 24 of 2025) |
| Citing a section of the 1958 Act **as current law**, without saying it is repealed | The section is no longer in force | The corresponding provision of the 2025 Act |

This is the pattern the feature was designed for: a **named**, **dated**,
**verifiable** supersession — not a warning to “check the latest amendments”.

**STATE TODAY**
- Answer the concept as the stem asks, under the 1958 Act the stem names.
- State explicitly that the 1958 Act stands repealed from 15 March 2026, and that
  the 2025 Act is now the operative Indian statute.
- Date the change. A bare “the Act has been replaced” scores less than the
  instrument and the date.

**ANSWER IMPACT: MODERATE**

**Reasoning.** Not `MAJOR`: the concept of unseaworthiness, the unseaworthy/unsafe
distinction and the owner's obligations are intact, so a candidate writing the old
answer still earns most of the marks. Not `MINOR`: this is no reference refresh —
the statute the whole answer hangs from has been repealed, and a script presenting
the 1958 Act as live law in August 2026 states something false about current Indian
law. That is a scoring consequence, which is what `MODERATE` means.

**A note on MIW's own answers.** All five held occurrences already handle this
correctly, and visibly more thoroughly as commencement approached: mentions of
repeal run 2 → 6 → 14 → 15 → 16 across July 2023, October 2024, June 2025,
August 2025 and February 2026. **No correction candidate arises.** The delta serves
a candidate studying from an older answer; it is not a defect in MIW's.

verified sittings, an official ancestor, and the only demonstrable
