Concept Map — DP Equipment Classes (MSC.1/Circ.1580)

Define the classes by the failure criterion, not equipment counts — the hardware (split boards, dual computers, three position references of two principles) exists to satisfy the criterion.

Confusable Pairs — Examiner Favourites

⚠ Confusable Pair: GT vs DWT
GT = dimensionless VOLUME index, Tonnage Convention 1969, GT = K₁V — never "gross tonnes". DWT = MASS in tonnes = displacement at summer draught − lightship. Triangle: Δ = Lightship + DWT. CII capacity: DWT for most types, GT for cruise/vehicle carriers.
⚠ Confusable Pair: Condition of Class vs Memorandum
CoC = time-limited requirement (measures/repairs/surveys) to RETAIN class — overdue → suspension. Memo/Note = recorded information (e.g. wastage being monitored) with NO compliance deadline. And neither is a PSC deficiency or an SMS non-conformity — separate regimes.
⚠ Confusable Pair: Class Surveyor vs Class (ISM) Auditor
Surveyor = hull/machinery condition against class rules. Auditor = management-system verification (ISM/ISPS/MLC) by audit. Separate competency streams and separate named authorizations under RO Code Part 2 — one does not imply the other.
⚠ Confusable Pair: Loading Manual vs Stability Booklet
Loading Manual = class-approved STRENGTH document (permissible SWBM/SF, loading conditions). Stability Booklet = approved STABILITY information (IS Code/Load Line). They travel together but answer different failure modes: breaking vs capsizing.
⚠ Confusable Pair: Lifting Gear Pre-2026 vs Post-2026
Before 2026: class rules + ILO C.152 + flag practice only. From 1 Jan 2026: SOLAS II-1/3-13 (MSC.532(107)) applies directly — register, testing, thorough examination and maker-based maintenance are SOLAS matters, PSC-checkable.
⚠ Confusable Pair: CSR scope vs IMO GBS scope
IACS CSR = bulk carriers 90 m+, double-hull tankers 150 m+, contract on/after 1 Jul 2015. SOLAS II-1/3-10 (GBS) = 150 m+ for BOTH types, contract on/after 1 Jul 2016 (keel 2017 / delivery 2020), excluding ore and combination carriers. So a 120 m bulk carrier can be a CSR ship but NOT a Reg. 3-10 ship. Two separate tests — apply both.
⚠ Confusable Pair: CSR — which CSR?
Common Structural Rules (IACS, structures chain) vs Continuous Synopsis Record (SOLAS XI-1 Reg. 5, documentation chain) vs CSM = Continuous Survey Machinery. Ask the examiner which he means before answering — that is awareness, not hesitation.

Topic Clusters — Tap to Recall

Class & Survey Regime
Q1. Class auditor qualifications
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
RO Code Part 2 framework: engineering degree or management-level CoC + sea service → society training → ISO 19011-based lead-auditor course (ISM/ISPS/MLC) → supervised audits → named authorization by RO/flag; continued-competence monitoring under IACS QSCS.
Q2. Condition of Class — who & when
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Issued by the classification society (attending surveyor) — never flag/owner. When: survey findings, damage/breakdown, postponed items, transfer of class. IACS definition: specific measures within a specific time limit to retain class. Overdue → suspension → statutory/insurance cascade.
Q6. Structural plans onboard
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Five groups: (1) GA + capacity plan; (2) midship section/scantlings, shell expansion, docking plan; (3) loading manual + stability booklet; (4) damage control plan/booklet + fire control plan; (5) CTF (PSPC), TM/survey reports, ESP file, Ship Construction File (SOLAS II-1/3-10).
Q7. Lifting appliance documents & CE checks
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
SOLAS II-1/3-13 (MSC.532(107), 1 Jan 2026). Documents: register · test certificates · annual thorough exams · loose-gear certs · maker-based PMS. CE checks: SWL vs certificate, brakes/safety devices per approved procedure (ship-specific values), wire discard criteria, limits/E-stops, register currency.
Q8. CSR — Common Structural Rules & scope
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
IACS Common Structural Rules for Bulk Carriers and Oil Tankers (CSR BC & OT). Bulk carriers 90 m+, double-hull oil tankers 150 m+, contracted for construction on or after 1 July 2015 (original separate CSR: 1 Apr 2006). Never quote the length without the contract date. Not the same scope as SOLAS II-1/3-10 GBS, which needs 150 m+ for both types.
Ship Measurement & Design Trends
Q3. GT vs DWT
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
GT: dimensionless volume index, ITC 1969, GT = K₁V (K₁ = 0.2 + 0.02 log₁₀V) — drives regulatory thresholds/fees. DWT: mass (t) = Δ(summer) − lightship = cargo+fuel+FW+ballast+stores+crew. Δ = LS + DWT.
Q4. Container ship size trend & why
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Plateau at 24,000+ TEU (~400×61 m). Why bigger: cube-square scaling → fuel/CO₂/cost per TEU-mile falls → EEDI/CII benefit. Why the pivot: port/canal limits, cargo-risk concentration (Ever Given 2021), flexibility → ordering shifted to mid-size (8–17k TEU) methanol/LNG dual-fuel.
Dynamic Positioning
Q5. DP-1 vs DP-2
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
MSC.1/Circ.1580: DP-1 — a single fault may lose position, no redundancy required. DP-2 — no position loss from any single ACTIVE-component fault (static components excepted if protected): split power + auto PMS with blackout prevention, independent thruster groups, ≥2 DP computers, 3 PRS of ≥2 principles; proven by FMEA + trials; class stated in the DPVAD. DP-3 adds fire/flood of one compartment.
