What RulesApp Engine does today

RulesApp Engine decomposes authoritative maritime publications — SOLAS, MARPOL, STCW, IMO codes and resolutions, IRS classification rules, DGMA circulars — into structured, cross-referenced records, and connects real vessel machinery (steering gear, emergency generators, oily water separators, fire main and pump systems, and more) to the regulations that govern it.

  • Search across decomposed regulatory content and connected Engineering Objects
  • An Engineering Workspace per system — official regulatory context, practical/inspection guidance, and the full relationship graph in one place
  • Cross-references between regulations, definitions, and equipment
  • Where linked, MIW's own Question Bank / Oral Notes / Written Answer content connected to the relevant Engineering Object
What this is not, today: RulesApp Engine does not generate exam answers, does not verify or publish new Question Bank content, and does not provide examiner-specific indexing. Those remain separate, not-yet-built capabilities — this page reflects only what is actually working right now.
