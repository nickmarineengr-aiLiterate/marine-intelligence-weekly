Dual-coding recall sheet for QB4_J (10 questions) — colour-clustered flip-cards, an ISM documentation-pyramid diagram, and confusable-pair drills for ISM Code, ISPS, MLC 2006 & STCW.

How to use this sheet

Don't just read the cards — try to answer from the question before tapping. That retrieval attempt (even if you get it wrong) is what builds the memory trace, not re-reading the answer. Tick "Reviewed — confident" only once you can answer without tapping.

Concept Map — SMS Documentation Pyramid (ISM Sec. 11)

Form is free (Sec. 11.3 — "form the Company considers most effective"); function is mandatory (Sec. 1.4). Answer with the pyramid plus section numbers.

Confusable Pairs — Examiner Favourites

⚠ Confusable Pair: ISM Audit vs Survey
Audit = management-system verification (paper, people, practice) → DOC/SMC, non-conformities. Survey = physical examination of ship/machinery → statutory & class certificates. There is no "ISM survey" — correct the premise, then link them: bad hardware = objective evidence of a Sec. 10 failure.
⚠ Confusable Pair: Training Manual vs Maintenance Manual
Training = USE: LSA III/35, fire II-2/15.2.3 — ship-specific, every mess/rec room, A/V allowed. Maintenance = UPKEEP: LSA III/36 instructions, fire II-2/14 maintenance plan. Two manual families, two purposes.
⚠ Confusable Pair: STCW III/1 vs III/2 (and III/4-5)
III/1 = OOW engine (operational level) — the minimum to hold an independent watch. III/2 = 2/E & CE management level (leadership & managerial skills table). III/4 & III/5 = ratings forming part of / able seafarer engine — they support, they cannot HOLD the watch.
⚠ Confusable Pair: MLC Captivity Protection vs War-Zone Refusal Right
MLC 2018 amendments (in force 26 Dec 2020): SEA + wages + repatriation continue during piracy/armed-robbery captivity. Right to refuse a warlike area = CBA layer (IBF/ITF Warlike Operations Areas) — NOT in the MLC text. Two layers; keep them separate.
⚠ Confusable Pair: Abandonment Security vs Death/Disability Security
Both from the 2014 amendments (in force 18 Jan 2017), both certificates posted on board. A2.5.2 = abandonment: 4 months wages cap + repatriation + essential needs, triggered by the three limbs (incl. ≥2 months unpaid). A4.2 = contractual death/long-term disability claims.

Topic Clusters — Tap to Recall

STCW & Watchkeeping
Q1. ER watch composition
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
No fixed universal number: STCW A-VIII/2 Part 3-2 — adequacy decided from plant state, weather, traffic, ship condition; floor set by the Safe Manning Document (SOLAS V/14). UMS ship (II-1/46–53): duty engineer on call in lieu of a manned watch. Rest hours bind the roster: 10/24 & 77/7.
Q2. When can an engineer keep an independent watch?
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Three gates: (1) STCW III/1 certification (OOW engine); (2) ISM Sec. 6.3 ship-specific familiarisation completed and documented; (3) CE's assessed release into the watch/UMS duty roster. Ratings under III/4/III/5 support but cannot hold the watch.
Q8. Types of leadership
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Autocratic = emergency; democratic = planning; laissez-faire = proven specialist. Transactional runs the system, transformational grows the people; situational leadership matches style to competence & commitment (direct→coach→support→delegate). STCW hook: Table A-III/2 leadership & managerial skills (HELM-M).
ISM Code & Manuals
Q3. Risk Based Planning
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
ISM 1.2.2.2: assess ALL identified risks and establish safeguards. Tools: risk assessment (5×5 matrix), ALARP, hierarchy of controls; maintenance side: criticality-driven planning (Sec. 10.3 critical equipment) and CBM within class-approved boundaries.
Q4. Training manual contents
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
TWO manuals. LSA (III/35): wear it · board it · launch it · survive in it · get rescued from it — for this ship's gear. Fire (II-2/15.2.3): prevent · report · respond · operate systems · escape. Ship-specific, illustrated, every mess/rec room, A/V allowed.
Q5. Safety manuals onboard
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Four families: SMS (ISM Sec. 11); SOLAS train/maintain/operate (III/35, III/36, II-2/14, 15.2.3, 16.2); MARPOL plans (SOPEP/SMPEP, garbage, BWMP, SEEMP); cargo (CSM VI/5.6, stability/loading manuals). Each = owner + approval basis + document control.
Q6. ISM manuals / SMS structure
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Pyramid: policy (Secs. 2/3/4/5.2) → procedures (6/7/8/9/10) → work instructions, checklists, records — all under Sec. 11 document control, Sec. 12 audit/review loop. Form is free (11.3), function is mandatory (1.4). DOC 5 yr/annual; SMC 5 yr/intermediate 2nd–3rd anniversary.
Q7. ISM survey vs audit
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Correct the premise: ISM verifies by AUDIT. Audit = system (DOC/SMC, NCs, major NC = serious threat / serious lack of implementation). Survey = physical ship (statutory/class certs). Bridge: poor hardware is objective evidence of a Sec. 10 failure.
MLC 2006
Q9. MLC & war risk / right to refuse
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
Two layers: MLC 2018 amendments (26 Dec 2020) — SEA continues, wages payable, repatriation survives during piracy/armed-robbery captivity (A2.1.7, A2.2.7). Right to refuse a war zone = CBA (IBF Warlike Operations Areas): refuse + repatriation at owner's cost + war bonus (typ. 100% basic) + doubled compensation.
Q10. Seafarer abandonment
Tap to reveal the 15-second answer →
A2.5.2 (18 Jan 2017): abandoned = no repatriation cover / no maintenance & support / ties severed incl. ≥2 months unpaid. Certificate on board, posted, English; direct access to provider. Cover: 4 months wages + repatriation + essential needs. Fallback: flag → port → labour-supplying State; ILO/IMO database.
