What MIW has, and what it does not

Stated first, because everything below is question wording and nothing below is an answer.
HeldThe printed question paper for 11 sittings, transcribed from a source copy: every question in full, with the printed serial, the time allowed and the total marks exactly as printed.
Not heldNo solved answer for any of these questions. No per-question mark split either — these papers print a total only. Where a question carries no longer-term signal below, that means no governed recurrence family reaches it yet, not that it has never recurred.
DatesEvery sitting below is dated from the printed source copy, which is the same standard as MIW’s solved papers. MIW holds recurrence evidence reaching further back than this, but those dates rest on secondary sources and are therefore never printed as a year or a count.

How to read the longer-term signal

The same governed projection the solved papers and the other year sheets use, rendered by the same code. This page computes no recurrence of its own.
Persistent · Rising · Re-emerging · Active in recent papersHow the underlying examinable concept behaves over MIW’s governed horizon. Qualitative on purpose: no count and no year is claimed.
No currentness risk flagged · Current answer verified · Currentness check pending · Answer under currentness reviewThese describe MIW’s answer to the CONCEPT, on a later solved paper — never this 2022 question. None of the questions on this page has been answered by MIW. A concept that recurred here and was later set again in a year MIW has solved carries the readiness of THAT answer, which is why an all-clear can appear beside a question with no answer behind it.
Current-framework answer in preparationMIW holds no current answer to this concept yet. That is a statement about MIW, not about the question.
Current framework: see …A governed review has found that a later solved question covers what this one asks, and names it. It does not mean this 2022 question has been answered. It means the concept has a current home elsewhere in the corpus.
Current framework answer →MIW holds a present-day answer to the concept this question examines, and the link opens it. This 2022 question has still not been solved. What is on the other side is not a past paper and never was: it has no sitting, no printed serial and no printed marks, it was set by no examiner, and it answers “what should I write about this now” rather than “what was correct at this sitting”. It carries a review date for exactly that reason.
A. … · B. … — one chip per partWhere a question asks for several independent things, MIW answers them separately and each part is routed on its own — some to a current framework answer, some to a later solved question that covers that part. Read the parts you are offered: a part with no chip is a part MIW does not yet answer, and the absence is deliberate rather than an oversight.
