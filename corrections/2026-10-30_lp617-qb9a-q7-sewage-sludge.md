**Summary:** QB9_A Q7 still named sewage sludge among the London Protocol's explicitly permitted substances after Resolution LP.6(17) removed it from Annex 1.
**What Was Wrong:** The London Convention section of Q7 read "a limited number of explicitly permitted substances (such as dredged material, sewage sludge, or organic waste of natural origin)". This contradicted the corrected QB1_A Q13.
**Correct Position:** Resolution LP.6(17), adopted 7 October 2022, deleted sewage sludge from Annex 1 of the 1996 Protocol. It entered into force on 15 January 2023. No new dumping permits may be issued. Active permits run until they expire, and no later than 31 May 2027.
**Primary Source:** Resolution LP.6(17) (IMO); Status of IMO Treaties Vol. II, 30 September 2026 (IMO, depositary). Registered as SRC-IMO-LP6-17 and SRC-IMO-STATUS-TREATIES-VOL2-2026-09-30.
