**Summary:** QB1_A Q13 taught sewage sludge as still on the London Protocol's Annex 1 permitted list after Resolution LP.6(17) removed it.
**What Was Wrong:** The CE Oral Tip read "Sewage sludge: on the permitted list under 1996 Protocol (subject to conditions)". The reverse-list paragraph and the 60-second answer also listed sewage sludge among the Annex 1 permitted materials. The SQ free-sample twin carried the same text.
**Correct Position:** Resolution LP.6(17), adopted 7 October 2022, deleted sewage sludge from Annex 1 (sub-paragraph 1.2), with a consequential deletion in Annex 2 paragraph 4. It entered into force generally on 15 January 2023 under Protocol Article 22.4. Canada, Finland and China initially objected; all three objections have since been withdrawn. No new dumping permits may be issued, and active permits end at expiry, no later than 31 May 2027. The amendment binds Contracting Parties to the 1996 Protocol, not States party only to the 1972 Convention, and does not affect MARPOL Annex IV or Annex VI.
